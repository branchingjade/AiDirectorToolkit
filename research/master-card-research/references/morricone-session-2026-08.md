## 已验证引语锚（卡片已引用，grep 通过）

- **主题即角色（莫里康内自述）**："Sergio and I concentrate more on the characters and their feelings; these feelings have to be interpreted musically so that the audience is able to understand them." + "Music must be international"（[S12] Cinema Papers 1984）
- **主题即角色（莱昂内佐证）**："From Ennio I ask for themes that clothe my characters easily. He's never read a script of mine to compose the music, because many times he's composed the music before the script is ever written." + "I've always said that my best dialogue and screenwriter is Ennio Morricone"（[S4] 莱昂内 1987）
- **配乐先行**："Sergio asked me to write and record the music before he shot the film. I then found out that he'd played it while shooting on location."（[S5] BFI 2006）；维基："developing the principal themes before filming began... staging camera movements and editing patterns to match the score"（[S2] GBU 专条）
- **口哨旋律起源**："Leone liked it and said why don't you find me the backing track... rewrote it and invented a new melody with a whistle."（[S3]）；伊斯特伍德："no one had tried something so operatic for a western. His music helped dramatize me"（[S3]）
- **教会双主题**："Later, Morricone defined The Mission theme as a duet between the 'Spanish' and 'Guaraní' themes."（[S9] 维基）
- **Gabriel's Oboe 入戏**："The melody features as part of the plot itself when Father Gabriel extracts his oboe at the heart of the forest"（[S10] Classic FM）
- **美国往事排箫**："notable for Morricone's incorporation of the music of Gheorghe Zamfir, who plays a pan flute"（[S13] 维基）；"would give any director pause. Yet it worked perfectly."（[S6] Quietus）
- **怀旧曲式**："a powerful and infinite nostalgia"、"the pedal technique, i.e. a bass note held while the chords change"、"ever-widening intervals"（[S11] Italian Piano）
- **八恶人**："He just wanted me to compose music about snow. That's the only clue he gave" + "he does not wish to repeat himself"（[S6]）

## 任务预设修正点（配乐轮特有：预设乐器必须逐项取证）

1. **「长笛」→ 排箫（pan flute）**：任务预设《美国往事》配乐含长笛；取证为 Gheorghe Zamfir 的排箫（[S13][S6]）。已按证据修正。
2. **「管风琴」未取证到**：《教会》配乐取证到的声部为礼拜仪式合唱（西班牙主题）、土著乐器（瓜拉尼主题）、巴洛克合唱×亚马逊鼓点、双簧管；无任何来源提管风琴。诚实声明如实写"未取证到"，卡片不写入。
3. **「人声吟唱」→ 口哨+无词 vocalizations**：镖客三部曲人声为 Alessandroni 口哨 + Edda Dell'Orso 无词花腔女高音；全片唯一有歌词歌曲是剧情内演唱的"The Story of a Soldier"（[S2]）。
