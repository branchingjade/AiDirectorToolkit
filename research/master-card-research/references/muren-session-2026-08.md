## 已验证引语锚（卡片引用，归一化 grep 全过）

- **约束到胶片世界**："part of the process is constraining it down to what film records... When it is constrained to the world of photography, then I can start to understand it again"（ilm_ysh）
- **镜头优先/作弊原则**："What you care about is what the camera sees"；"it's a cheat, just black and white to light it up"；"we cheated all the way through"（深渊水蛇折射全作弊）（ilm_ysh）
- **弄脏铬面（pewter）**："I went in and muddied it up a little bit, so that the surface isn't 100 percent reflective... That's what anchors it into the shot"（denofgeek）；同事 Seiden："We called it the 'pewter' look. Without that, the T-1000 didn't have any mass"（vfxblog）
- **运动模糊手工法**："I didn't want the stained glass knight to look like it came from ILM, that it was stuck onto the background"；每帧渲染 9 次（ilm_ysh）
- **分层合成**："Each element was shot in front of a blue screen, so when we combined them, twelve pastries would be in the same shot"（ilm_ysh）
- **试错制**："If we find out in two months it's not working, we can back off and do it another way"（ilm_ysh）
- **艺术大于技术**："I've never been concerned about technology. I'm interested in the imagery; the power of the imagery and how emotional the image is."（screencrush）；"Everything in movies is feelings... you might as well be doing it by telephone"（ilm_ysh）
- **一年休假学 CG**："It gave me the confidence to go back and risk doing Terminator 2 and Jurassic Park because it was no longer a mystery to me"；"you need to know what you can do with it"（3dvfx）
- **故意让作品过时**："as soon as I finish it I sort of intentionally try to make it obsolete"（3dvfx）
- **反抄袭**："we were not copying anything"（screencrush）；"a xerox of a xerox of a xerox"（denofgeek）
- **T2 与 JP 递进**："I always credit T2 as being equal at least to Jurassic Park"；"we added skin to the effects... not look like plastic or artificial dinosaurs"（screencrush）
- **步态研究**："It was then that we started to realise how incredibly difficult it is to do a person"（denofgeek）
- **ILM 文化**："People look to ILM for the future because we've been the future for so long"；"John Dykstra said, 'We can do it.'"（looper）
- **AI 按钮论**："you can't push a button... if it is button pushing, it'll look very boring"（denofgeek）
- **JP 转折**（维基转引）："a CG test of a walking skeleton T-Rex"；三个月皮肤突破（"organically moving flesh and muscle... animal-like skin texture... sun and bounce lighting"）；Spielberg "It's going to be amazing. People are really going to believe that dinosaurs are walking this earth today"

## 视效轴特有查证要点（ILM 系）

1. **ILM 官网 interview 栏目**（ilm.com 2026 年重访《少年福尔摩斯》）是官方一手金矿——技术细节（激光扫描仪 1 帧/分钟、1-2K 渲染、150 吨树枝盐真雪、25 英尺翼展真飞行器）远超维基。
2. **重映/周年节点催生深度重访访谈**：T2 3D 重映（2017）→ ScreenCrush + Den of Geek 两篇长专访；搜索时带"3D re-release/anniversary"关键词。

4. **维基转引纪录片口径**：Spielberg/Lucas/Jim Morris 评语出自 Iwerks 纪录片《Industrial Light & Magic: Creating the Impossible》(2010) 与 StarWars.com 口述史（2019）——视频无文字稿，卡片一律标「维基转引」。
5. **镜头数双口径**：T2 CG 镜头约 50（编者按）vs "52 shots or something like that"（本人）——按本人自述写。
