## 1. 专业签名（一句话）

**好莱坞声音设计界的"实体派"宗师——坚持用真实世界的声音素材（动物、门、日常物件）经"变调+分层+放慢"改造成异界之声，让观众信以为真的同时感到陌生；他视音效为"角色"与"音乐"，把声音当故事讲（"Sound is emotion"），以 7 座奥斯卡成为本·伯特之后最有权重的声音设计师。**

依据：20 次奥斯卡提名 7 次获奖（1992《终结者2》声音+音效剪辑双奖、1994《侏罗纪公园》双奖、1998《泰坦尼克号》、1999《拯救大兵瑞恩》双奖，[S1][S10]）；Mix 杂志称其"arguably the finest sound designer and re-recording mixer of his generation"（同代最杰出声音设计师兼混音师，[S9]转引[S3]）；"Sound is emotion. Not just music, but all sound."（[S2]）；约翰·拉塞特评价其《跳跳灯》声音"教会他声音如何成为讲故事的伙伴"（[S2][S9]）。

## 2. 美学体系（手法 | 成片实例 | 取证来源）

| 维度 | 手法 | 成片实例 | 取证来源 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 声音/素材观 | 实体素材优先：不用合成器"造"声，从现实世界录音改造——"There's a danger in processing sound too much. I believe the best sound effects come from the best raw recordings, and are tweaked as little as possible"（处理过度有危险；最好的音效来自最好的原始录音，尽量少加工） | 《侏罗纪公园》全部恐龙声由真实动物录音混合；《虫虫危机》《Cocoon》外星光=香槟酒杯摩擦声 | [S2] |
| 声音/改造三件套 | 三大改造工具：变调（pitch）、分层（layering）、放慢（slowing down）——"pitch and layering, which are my primary tools for bending real sounds to my will"；"take a sound and slow it down: It becomes much bigger"（把声音放慢会变得巨大），师承 Burtt 的 Rancor 怪兽=奇瓦瓦狗叫声放慢 | 《侏罗纪公园》恐龙=数层动物声混成"听起来像一只动物"（Synclavier 采样器时代首创）；《终结者2》T-1000 戳眼球=自家狗吃狗粮的咀嚼声放慢 | [S2][S4][S5] |
| 声音/角色化 | 音效是"演员"：给物件角色与性格——Luxo Jr. 台灯"声音即角色"；"I always think of sound design being like prospecting for gold... find the one percent that has something interesting about it"（声音设计像淘金：做一堆声音，找出有趣的百分之一，贴到画面上会发生魔法般的融合） | 《跳跳灯》灯声（拧灯泡/刮金属声实验）；《怪物公司》门声（业界评价"门成为角色"，见诚实声明 2） | [S2][S3][S8] |
| 声音/情绪论 | 声音的本质是情绪而非技术——"I think of sound design as an emotional thing. And not as a technical thing... I think of sound design as a way to generate an emotion. Whenever possible in a scene even room tones or refrigerator hums... any sound, not just music can make you feel something"（声音设计是情绪之事；房间氛围声、冰箱嗡嗡声只要唤起情绪就有价值） | 《西区故事》1957 纽约街景氛围；《Minority Report》 | [S7] |
| 声音/变化律 | "第一定律：声音必须有变化"——"Sound comes to us over time. You don't get a snapshot of sound. Therefore, what you notice with sound, the essential building block, is change"（声音随时间到来，基本构件是变化）；同一音效每次出现都要有变奏 | 恐龙声每次出场不同；《侏罗纪公园》各类叫声按场景重混 | [S3] |
| 声音/静默观 | 沉默是一种声音，如同"零"是一个数字——"Silence can be thought of as a type of sound. It's like when somebody years ago figured out that zero was a number"；静默制造紧张（"Tension comes from the silence of that scene"） | 《碟中谍》CIA 潜入戏被 De Palma 抽空全部音效；《私恋失调》静默→巨响的暴力切换 | [S2][S3] |
| 声音/清晰度 | 混音要"引导耳朵"而非堆砌——"what bothers me with sound is it feels like it's a scattered approach, it's got a lot of things happening, but I don't know what to listen to"（最怕声音散乱、不知道听什么）；像斯皮尔伯格引导视线一样引导听觉；"the biggest challenge often is to make the track articulate... not turn into mush"（大场面最大的挑战是让声轨"说得清楚"，不糊成一团） | 《绿巨人》《拯救大兵瑞恩》混音；《侏罗纪公园2》独木难支的"一声玻璃裂" | [S7][S3] |
| 声音/音画关系 | 声音可以讲画外故事——"He's using the soundtrack to tell that part of the story"（斯皮尔伯格用声轨讲画外那部分故事）；声效与音乐无边界："I don't think an audience is going to care which parts of the soundtrack are coming from an orchestra and which parts are sound effects" | 《西区故事》开场拆迁声持续到画外；《AI》特意让音效"非常音乐化" | [S7][S3] |
| 声音/节奏观 | 声音即节奏：做声音多年养成的"电影本能"——"sound is all about rhythm... telling a story with these kind of rhythms is really key"；动画导演时用临时声轨向动画师传达节奏 | 《Lifted》未开画先做临时声轨定节奏；星战前传飞车赛"引擎有脉搏" | [S2][S6] |
| 声音/声景差异 | 同一介质的不同空间要有不同声景——"we played with different ambiances for wide-open ocean versus a constricted aquarium and different parts of the ocean"（开阔大洋、逼仄水族箱、海洋不同区域各配不同氛围声） | 《海底总动员》全片水下却分区设声景；《星战前传一》奥托冈加水下世界"一切被压低" | [S8][S6] |
| 声音/组织法 | "玩具箱"工作法：声音设计者不钉死每个音，而是按情绪/类别整理素材库交给剪辑师自由组合——"I always thought of it like a toy box. I tell the editors: Here's your toy box... put them together somehow"；声效素材像语言的"音素"（phonemes）可任意重组 | 《西区故事》按情绪分类建库；Synclavier 音效"部件库" | [S7][S3] |

## 3. 创作思路（工作方法/理念，附原话与出处）

- **师承 Ben Burtt，声音为故事服务**："All of my approaches and philosophies about sound come from Ben Burtt... how much he cared about using sound to tell a story"（我全部的声音方法都来自本·伯特——他多么在乎用声音讲故事）[S2]；做《侏罗纪公园》时"Ben 去度假了，我磕磕绊绊直到他回来才真正开始"（"I couldn't get started until he was in the building"）[S2]。他定义 Burtt 的革命性："redefined how sound design work was done, essentially by becoming a director of the soundtrack"（重新定义了声音设计——本质上成为声轨的导演）[S3]。
- **实体之声与"淘金论"**："I never used the FM synthesis – even making electronic sound effects I would try to use natural sounds, just because I find real sounds are more interesting"（连电子音效我都尽量用自然声，因为真实的声音更有趣）[S2]；"I always think of sound design being like prospecting for gold. Start by, say, goofing around, making lots of sounds, then find the one percent that has something interesting about it. Put this against the film, and there's a magical moment when the sound, if it's right, merges into the image, brings it to life. They were not cartoon-y. They were fun, reality-based sounds."（[S3]，Mix 2004；同段亦见 [S2]）——Luxo Jr. 的灯声来自"拧灯泡、刮金属"等真实声音实验，拉塞特起初根本没想到灯会有声音（"He'd never imagined they'd have voices and was wary of the idea"）[S2][S3]。
- **声音是情绪**："Sound is emotion. Not just music, but all sound. Humans (who can hear) seem to take sound in general for granted... How manipulative can we be when no one's paying attention!"（声音即情绪，不只是音乐；人类对声音习以为常，正好让我们悄悄操纵）[S2]；"My job as a sound designer is... trying to gauge my own emotional reaction to a scene or moment. Often it's very subtle, and subtle is good"（我的工作就是测量自己对场景的情绪反应；微妙是好的）[S7]。
- **第一定律：变化**："your first law of sound is to always have variation. Sound comes to us over time. You don't get a snapshot of sound. Therefore, what you notice with sound, the essential building block, is change"（[S3]）；节奏与动态对比是手段："In an action film, say Terminator 2, contrasts, sometimes even massive contrasts, are essential"（[S3]）。
- **枪声的"管弦乐法"**："You take the high snap of a pistol and add to it the low boom of a cannon and the midrange of a canyon echo. You orchestrate it."（手枪高频脆响+大炮低频轰鸣+峡谷回声的中频，像配器一样组合）[S3]——恐龙声、枪声、引擎声的通用构造法。
- **静默是声音**："Silence can be thought of as a type of sound. It's like when somebody years ago figured out that zero was a number. And silence is just as valid as an amazing sound. Every sound editor can't help but think of how to fill up a track; it's what we're paid for."（[S3][S2]）；《碟中谍》CIA 潜入戏被导演抽空音效后，"It made everyone lean in, pay closer attention, get nervous. Tension comes from the silence of that scene."（[S3][S2]）；"The quiet scenes can be the challenge... that's just a single glass crack, is that the right glass crack? There's nothing else on the track, it better be!"（安静场面才难：《侏罗纪公园2》里单独一声玻璃裂，轨上没别的东西，它必须是对的）[S3]。
- **先感受电影，再决定声音**："your first step... is to get to know the movie just to feel the movie... give yourself over to the movie... What's the movie trying to do?"（第一步是感受这部电影，把自己交给电影，问"这部电影要干什么"）；"I try to find that tone. What's the movie trying to do?"——声音的调性必须跟随电影的调性（自然主义与风格化的混合比例）[S7]。
- **以观众身份工作**："it's one of the secrets to film work is to think like an audience when the time is right... Sometimes you have to forget the details and you go on the other side of the screen and feel like you're sitting in an audience and seeing this for the first time"（电影工作的秘密之一：该当观众时当观众，忘掉细节坐到屏幕对面第一次看它）[S7]。
- **声音与音乐是谈判关系**："A lot of the time, there is a negotiation going on between sound and music... I wanted to pull the effects back to let the music lead"（声音与音乐经常在谈判；我常把音效拉低让音乐领路）[S6]；《AI》则反着来："we made a real effort to make the sound effects very, very musical"（[S3]）。
- **为"变化"保留空间**：混音宁可轻不可轰——"We get overly loud striving for impact. It's counter-intuitive, but sometimes we'll get more punch from a mix by being less loud"（[S3]）；声音流程是漏斗："The sound process is a big funnel, narrowing tracks and making choices as it goes forward"（[S3]）。
- **跨界导演的节奏红利**："Working in film sound is a great way to immerse yourself in the rhythms of a movie... sound is all about rhythm... Animation is really about rhythms and timing"；《Lifted》在动画开画前就用临时声轨把节奏意图传给动画师 [S2]。
- **保持不被定型**："I went from Terminator 2 to A River Runs Through It, from Strange Days to Toy Story. This kept things interesting. I think in any career you have to be careful not to get pigeonholed."（《终结者2》到《大河恋》，《末世纪暴潮》到《玩具总动员》——别被定型）[S3]。

## 4. 招牌作品拆解（声音怎么做）

### 作品一：《侏罗纪公园》（1993）——恐龙声音的"动物混音学"

- **从零造声**："He had to create dozens of distinct dinosaur noises essentially from scratch, since no one really knows what these long-dead animals would have sounded like"（没人知道恐龙怎么叫，全部从零造）[S5]；"It scared me when I first saw [the offer]... But there was no bigger candy store for sound design than Jurassic Park"（被吓到，但再没有比它更大的声音设计糖果店）[S4]；该片获奥斯卡最佳声音+最佳音效剪辑双奖，也是影史第一部 DTS 格式公映片 [S1]。
- **雷龙（梁龙科）叫声——驴的"约德尔唱法"**："The brachiosaur's singing is one of my favorite sounds in the movie because it's beautiful, but like all good sound design, it's made from a non-beautiful source, which is donkeys... You think of donkeys, and they kind of yodel... There's this pitch shift in donkey vocals, and if you slow them way down, you get almost a hooting, songlike quality. That's the brachiosaur when it's in its splendor mode."（雷龙的歌唱是全片我最爱的声音之一——美，但出自不美的素材：驴。驴叫有音高滑动，像约德尔唱法，放慢到极致就成了近乎歌唱的鸣声）[S5]；NPR 版本补充：雷龙日常的"隆隆"是象+牛+驴的混合，而"唱歌"段落只用驴（"almost sounds like an animal yodeling... So you slow that way down, and it sounds like song"）[S4]。⚠️ 雷龙打喷嚏=鲸鱼喷气孔+消防栓 [S5]。
- **霸王龙——小象尖叫+虎吼，外加自家狗**："The key element of the T. rex roar is not a full-grown elephant but a baby elephant"（关键不是大象而是小象）；旧金山动物园那头小象只叫了一次、再不肯重复，于是"同一卷录音用在每一声霸王龙吼里" [S4][S5]；低频层来自老虎之类 [S4]；杰克罗素梗 Buster 也贡献了撕咬感（"The way they animated the T. rex was very doglike"——动画师把霸王龙做得像狗甩玩具）[S5]。
- **迅猛龙——交配中的陆龟与"爱的语言"**："when the raptors bark at each other to communicate, it's a tortoise having sex... It's a mating tortoise!"（迅猛龙互相交流的叫声是交配中的陆龟）；厨房门口逼近的呼吸声是马；偷袭 Muldoon 前的嘶声是鹅（"You've got to get a goose mad"）；尖叫声含"发情中的公海豚"；只有一个人的声音混入——朋友 Dietrich 的喉音低吼（"it felt like cheating... I felt like I was cheating the sound-design gods!"）[S5][S4]。
- **三角龙——牛群+人吹纸筒**：农场牛群打底，但病中呼吸声的关键素材是"我朝一根带弹簧的长纸筒呼吸"（reverb device）——全片少有的非有机素材 [S5]。
- **放慢即变大（核心法则）**："One of the fun things in sound design is to take a sound and slow it down: It becomes much bigger... if you slow something down, something small, it brings out elements of the sound that you could probably never get if you recorded something big"（把小的声音放慢会显出录大声音永远得不到的细节）[S5]——此法则师承 Burtt《绝地归来》Rancor=奇瓦瓦狗慢放 [S5]。
- **遗产复用**：T-Rex 吼声后来被用于《虫虫危机》的 Dim、《阿凡达》的 Thanator、《波西·杰克逊》的水怪（[S1]）；《Backdraft》原创音效库被《指环王》三部曲、《怪物史莱克》等反复引用 [S1]。

### 作品二：皮克斯动画声音（Luxo Jr. / 玩具总动员 / 怪物公司 / 海底总动员）

- **起点《跳跳灯》（1986）**：拉塞特点名找 Burtt 未果，活儿"弹"到 Rydstrom 手里 [S3]；他用拧灯泡、刮金属等真实声音给台灯做出"声音性格"——"It felt like the birth of something new, even then"（当时就觉得是某种新事物的诞生）[S2][S3]；拉塞特后来说正是这段经历"taught him how sound can be a partner in the storytelling of a film"（教会他声音如何成为讲故事的伙伴）[S2][S9]。
- **《玩具总动员》系列（1995/1999）**：声音设计师+重录混音师 [S1][S9]；Rydstrom 自述其职业策略就是不被定型——"from Strange Days to Toy Story"（[S3]）。本批来源未取到他对玩具世界"小声音"思路的直接访谈引文（见诚实声明 3）。
- **《怪物公司》（2001）**：声音设计师+混音师，获奥斯卡最佳音效剪辑提名 [S1][S9]；声音设计界对其门库追逐段的通行评价是"门的声音成了角色"（SoundWorks Collection 视频简介："the sounds of the doors, which became characters of their own... the famous 'door vault' chase"；视频未转录，仅确认存在，见诚实声明 2）。
- **《海底总动员》（2003）**：全片水下的声景策略——"There are a lot of cliche things that we do for things underwater in movies... When it's a short scene, we might muddle the dialogue or add echo, but we couldn't really do that because the entire film was underwater. But we played with different ambiances for wide-open ocean versus a constricted aquarium and different parts of the ocean."（水下戏的惯例是糊对白加回声，但全片都在水下就没法用；于是按"开阔大洋/逼仄水族箱/海洋各区"分别设计氛围声）[S8]；细节：Nemo 残疾鳍=甩动的湿纸巾，浴缸气泡=海洋氛围之一 [S8]。
- **导演转型**：《Lifted》（2006，奥斯卡动画短片提名）、Toy Story Toons《Hawaiian Vacation》、卢卡斯影业动画长片《Strange Magic》（2015）；Pixar 长篇《Newt》2011 年因故事问题被取消 [S1]。

### 作品三：《星球大战前传一：幽灵的威胁》（1999）——以混音师身份进入星战

- **角色定位（诚实版）**：Rydstrom 在《幽灵的威胁》中是"重录混音师"（re-recording mixer），专混音效轨——"I mixed the sound effects, including lightsabers, explosions, R2-D2, lasers, and all that kind of stuff"（光剑、爆炸、R2-D2、激光都是我混的）[S6]；音效设计由 Ben Burtt 主导，他混的是 Burtt 团队准备的素材（"Of course, it's Ben Burtt, so it was exciting"）[S6]。此前他只在《太空炮弹》和《星际旅行》游乐设施上摸过星战世界的边（"Spaceballs... kind of counts"）[S6]。
- **飞车赛=纯音效的节奏戏**："one of the great things about the speeder bike chase and the podrace is that they hold off on music until the end... It's just sound effects. You feel like you're really there in the middle of this insane race"（飞车赛把音乐压到最后，纯音效把你拽进赛道中央）；"Rhythm is one of the key elements. The engines have a pulse to them. Ben took great care to make the key pod sounds completely different so that you can identify them from cut to cut"（引擎有脉搏；每台飞车的声彼此可辨）；还靠"把最响的段落切开、插进安静时刻"制造动态，并利用峡谷回声（"you can hear the reverb and echo of the podracers"）[S6]。
- **环绕声技术突破**：前传一是首批用 Dolby Digital Surround EX 的电影——"It gave us an extra dimension by placing sounds directly behind your head. You hadn't heard that before"（声音第一次从你脑后经过）；光剑战用后环绕让打斗"感觉就在脸前" [S6]。
- **声音与音乐的谈判**：《命运之决斗》配乐密不透风——"There was no competing with it. I could've made the lightsabers loud as heck, and they wouldn't win... I could do anything rhythmically within it because it was so fast"（光剑再响也赢不了这段音乐，反而可以在它的节奏里自由腾挪）[S6]；水下世界奥托冈加"Everything was subdued... Jar Jar's world is pretty beautiful"（一切都压低——加·加的世界很漂亮）[S6]；卢卡斯名言"这不过是部 B 级片"让混音团队从容工作，前传一也是第一部在 Skywalker Ranch 混音的星战片 [S6]。

## 5. 可复用时机（对接国风志怪/武侠/历史创作）

| 场景 | 用哪招 | 证据出处 |
|---|---|---|
| 妖兽/精怪叫声 | 动物混音学：取 2–3 种真实动物声按频段叠层（如"小象高频尖叫+老虎低频"构造霸王龙）；"非美素材出美声"——驴叫慢放成雷龙之歌；凡兽之声+人类喉音混入=非人感 | [S4][S5]（T-Rex/雷龙/迅猛龙构造法） |
| 巨物/大神出场 | 放慢即变大：小的声源放慢后比大素材更"大"——巨妖的脚步声、低鸣都从小的日常声放慢而来；慢放会带出原素材里人耳从未注意的细节 | [S5]（Burtt Rancor 先例、Buster 狗） |
| 志怪氛围/妖气先至 | 声音讲画外故事：妖未现身，声先到——"用声轨讲画外那部分故事"；场景氛围用"声音情绪"而非堆砌音效（冰箱嗡鸣级别的细微声也可承载情绪） | [S7]（西区故事拆迁声例）、[S2]（Sound is emotion） |
| 恐怖瞬间/静默顿悟 | 沉默=零：在密集声场后突然抽空全部声音（De Palma 式"全部拿掉"），紧张由静默自产；安静场面里单一声响必须"是对的"（一声玻璃裂） | [S2][S3]（沉默是零/碟中谍/玻璃裂） |
| 妖界vs人间 | 同介质分区声景：人界与妖界即使视觉相近，声景必须两套（开阔大洋≠水族箱）；妖界"一切被压低"的窒息感（奥托冈加水下处理） | [S8]（海底总动员）、[S6]（奥托冈加） |
| 打斗/大战混音 | 声轨管弦乐法+清晰度纪律：枪声=手枪高音+大炮低音+回声中音三层配器；大场面最大敌人是"糊成一团"，最多留 2–3 个可辨认层；"引导耳朵"让观众知道该听什么 | [S3]（配器法/articulate）、[S7]（引导听觉） |
| 怪声不重样 | 第一定律=变化：同一妖声每次出现都要有变奏（速度/音高/层次），声音是时间的艺术，静止即死亡 | [S3]（variation 定律） |
| 配乐与音效 | 谈判关系：国风配乐（琴箫）与怪物音效对位——音乐密时音效让路（"光剑再响也赢不了"），音乐疏时音效领衔（飞车赛前段纯音效） | [S6]（命运之决斗）、[S6]（podrace） |
| 声效素材库 | 玩具箱工作法：按"情绪+类别"建妖声素材库交给剪辑/混音自由组合，而非钉死每一个音；素材像音素可重组 | [S7]（toy box）、[S3]（phonemes） |
| 动画/分镜节奏 | 临时声轨先行：未开画先做声音节奏轨，向动画/特效团队传递"这一场的节拍" | [S2]（Lifted 临时声轨） |

⚠️ 本节为分析框架：把已取证的 Rydstrom 手法映射到目标类型，非 Rydstrom 本人关于中国题材的自述。

## 6. AI 提示词对接（美学语言 → 视频生成提示词）

按取证要点编写，非原片描述。声音设计五组：

**妖兽叫声（动物混音+慢放）**
- EN: "A qingniao demon's cry assembled from three real animal layers: a crane's screech (high, sharp, foreground), a donkey's bray slowed to one-third speed (mid, yodeling, eerie melody), a tiger's low growl (sub-bass foundation); each layer occupies a different register so the result sounds like one unnatural animal, beautiful yet wrong"
- 中注：高频鸟鸣+驴叫慢放成"妖歌"+虎吼低频——三层频段叠成一只非人兽（依据 [S4][S5] 雷龙/霸王龙构造法）

**巨妖脚步（小素材放慢）**
- EN: "The footsteps of a giant mountain deity: a sack of wet rice dropped on stone, slowed down 400 percent, revealing deep resonances no real giant recording could capture; the shake of a temple bell as its breathing"
- 中注：小声源放慢变"巨大"且带出隐藏细节（依据 [S5] "slow it down: It becomes much bigger"）

**妖气先至（画外之声）**
- EN: "A mountain village at dusk, visuals perfectly ordinary; the soundtrack carries the story off-screen: distant bronze bell striking thirteen times, children's voices fading into wind, the sound of silk robes brushing stone where no one walks; the demon never appears in frame but the sound tells its approach"
- 中注：画面无事、声音先行——用声轨讲画外故事（依据 [S7] 西区故事拆迁声例）

**静默恐怖（抽空声场）**
- EN: "After a dense wall of sound (rain, temple chanting, insects), everything is stripped from the track for five seconds — no ambience, no music, nothing; then a single sound: one glass crack, unmistakably wrong; tension comes entirely from the silence"
- 中注：密集声场后彻底抽空=把零当数字用；安静里的一声必须"是对的"（依据 [S2][S3] 沉默是零/玻璃裂）

**妖界声景（分区+压低）**
- EN: "Two worlds, two soundscapes: the human market loud and layered (hawkers, chickens, copper coins); the spirit realm visually similar but everything subdued — voices muffled as if underwater, footsteps soundless, a single zither note hanging in the air; the audience feels the wrongness through the mix before the visuals reveal it"
- 中注：同介质分区声景+妖界"一切被压低"（依据 [S8] 海底总动员、[S6] 奥托冈加）

**怪声变奏（变化律）**
- EN: "The same demon's roar appears four times; each time it is recognizably the same creature but never identical — pitch shifted down, layer count reduced, echo lengthened; sound arrives over time, so every repetition must carry change"
- 中注：第一定律=变化，妖声每现必变奏（依据 [S3] variation 定律）

## 7. ⚠️ 诚实声明（来源局限）

1. **未听辨原声**：本代理未实际聆听《侏罗纪公园》《玩具总动员》《海底总动员》等成片原声带；本卡中所有"听起来如何"的描述（雷龙之歌如驴叫慢放、霸王龙吼的构成等）均引自 Rydstrom 本人访谈/广播/媒体转述的原文并保留出处，未经听辨核对。若需用于创作参考，建议以成片实际听感为准。
2. **《怪物公司》门声细节取证不足**：本批文字来源未取到 Rydstrom 本人关于门库追逐场景的直接访谈；仅确认其担任该片声音设计师并获奥斯卡提名（[S1][S9]），以及 SoundWorks Collection 视频简介中"门成为角色"的通行评价（该视频未转录原文，仅确认存在）。卡片中未写入未经来源支持的细节（如门声具体录音过程）。
3. **《玩具总动员》声音理念引文缺失**：本批来源未 grep 到 Rydstrom 关于"玩具世界声音要小/塑料感"的直接引语（网传说法未验证），故仅写其署名与职业策略原话（[S1][S3]），不引用未验证表述。
4. **星战角色边界（防误植）**：Rydstrom 在《幽灵的威胁》《克隆人的进攻》中为"重录混音师"（音效轨混音），音效设计主导者为 Ben Burtt [S6]；《克隆人的进攻》中著名的"地震炸弹"（seismic charge）声音系 Burtt 的概念（"audio black hole"，从《新希望》时期就构思）[S11]——均不得记到 Rydstrom 名下。Rydstrom 后来亦以混音师身份参与《原力觉醒》（2015）[S1][S10]。
5. **一手/二手区分**：一手=USO Project 2011 访谈全文（S2，含转引 Mix Magazine 引语）、Mix 2004 Tom Kenny 访谈全文（S3，Wayback 存档）、StarWars.com 2024 官方访谈（S6）、NPR 2013 广播访谈（S4，记者转述+直接引语）、Vulture 2013（S5，直接引语）、Tonebenders 2022 转录（S7，AI 生成转录，可能有转写错误）、FilmSound.org 2004（S8，直接引语）；二手=英文维基（S1）、Designing Sound 专题页（S9，含 Mix 2003 报道转引）、Skywalker Sound 官方履历（S10）、Wookieepedia（S11）。
6. **奖项口径**：7 座奥斯卡=1992《终结者2》声音+音效剪辑、1994《侏罗纪公园》声音+音效剪辑、1998《泰坦尼克号》声音、1999《拯救大兵瑞恩》声音+音效剪辑；20 次提名 [S1]。网传"12 次提名时 7 中"为 Mix 2004 当年口径 [S3]，与今不同。
7. **Mix 2003/2004 两篇**：设计之声 2009 页转引的 Mix 报道（"leaving Skywalker Sound" 原文）与 Wayback 存档的 Mix 2004 年 Tom Kenny 访谈为同篇（Feb 1, 2004），本卡统一以 S3 引用；原 mixonline.com 链接现 404，经 Wayback 取证。
