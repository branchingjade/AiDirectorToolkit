## 一、动作场面编排：如何用镜头语言编排一场打斗

### 1.1 动作戏的编排本质：先安全、再讲故事、最后才是速度

动作编排（fight choreography）的第一原则是"制造幻觉而不伤人"，第二原则是"让观众跟得上动作的叙事"。维基百科对舞台/银幕打斗编排的定义：

> "Stage combat, fight craft or fight choreography is a specialised technique in theatre designed to create the illusion of physical combat without causing harm to the performers."（舞台打斗是一种专业技法，旨在制造真实肢体对抗的幻觉而不伤害表演者。）
> "The overriding concern is for the safety of the actors and audience."（压倒一切的关注点是演员与观众的安全。）
> "Choreography is typically learned step by step, and practiced at first very slowly before increasing to a speed that is both dramatically convincing and safe."（编排通常逐步学习，先极慢练习，再加速到既有戏剧说服力又安全的节奏。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fight_choreographer

**导演要点**：打斗编排 = 分步学习 → 慢速排练 → 逐步加速。镜头编排同理：先建立空间与站位（让观众看懂"谁在哪、谁打谁"），再逐层加码速度与密度。

### 1.2 为什么动作要"可读"：观众必须能跟上打斗的叙事

> "One of the biggest reasons that theatrical fight directors often do not aim for strict realism is that the live audience could not easily follow the 'story' of the action if bodies and blades were moving in the ways trained fighters would move them... the fight director knows that the audience couldn't follow the action as well if the attacks were faster, so most fight choreographers would make choices to help the audience follow the story."（打斗导演不追求严格写实的一大原因：如果身体与兵刃按真正武者的方式运动，现场观众无法跟上动作的"故事"……因此多数编排师会做取舍，帮观众跟上剧情。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fight_choreographer

**导演要点**：一场打斗本身就是一段叙事，镜头设计的第一任务是"可读性"——建立→交锋→打击感→收束的结构，本质是让观众始终知道：谁在打、为什么打、谁赢了。

### 1.3 动作场的视觉节奏：快速剪辑制造冲击，慢动作强调瞬间

剪辑节奏直接决定打击感。镜头时长（帧数）是导演控制节奏的核心杠杆：

> "The duration of each shot, determined by the number of frames or length of film, contributes to the overall rhythm of the film... For example, a brief flash of white frames can convey a sudden impact or a violent moment. On the other hand, lengthening or adding seconds to a shot can allow for audience reaction or to accentuate an action."（每个镜头的时长决定全片节奏……例如一闪而过的白帧可以传达突然的冲击或暴力瞬间；反之，延长镜头几秒可以让观众反应或强调某个动作。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_editing

**导演要点（打击感怎么拍）**：打击瞬间 = 极短镜头（甚至 1-2 帧的白闪/闪切）+ 打击后延长镜头让观众"感受"余波。这就是"快切建立打击感、慢动作强调后果"的底层原理。

慢动作在动作片中的系统化用法：

> "Slow motion is used widely in action films for dramatic effect, as well as the famous bullet-dodging effect, popularized by The Matrix. Formally, this effect is referred to as speed ramping and is a process whereby the capture frame rate of the camera changes over time."（慢动作在动作片中广泛用于戏剧化效果，以及由《黑客帝国》带火的子弹时间。学名"变速摄影"：拍摄时改变摄影机帧率。）
> "The Matrix made a distinct success in applying the effect into action scenes through the use of multiple cameras, as well as mixing slow-motion with live action in other scenes. Japanese director Akira Kurosawa was a pioneer using this technique in his 1954 movie Seven Samurai."（《黑客帝国》用多机位把慢动作成功应用于动作场面，并把慢动作与实拍混剪；日本导演黑泽明是这一技法的先驱，1954 年的《七武士》即用此技。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_motion

**导演要点**：慢动作 + 多机位是动作戏"升格打击感"的标准组合；变速（speed ramping）让单个镜头内从慢到正常速度，制造时间操纵感。

### 1.4 范式一：黑泽明——"运动上剪辑"与多镜头覆盖

黑泽明的动作/场面调度风格，维基百科总结为其标志性技法组合：

> "In his films of the 1940s and 1950s, he frequently employs the 'axial cut', in which the camera moves toward or away from the subject through a series of matched jump cuts rather than tracking shots or dissolves. Another stylistic trait is 'cut on motion', which displays the motion on the screen in two or more shots instead of one uninterrupted one. A form of cinematic punctuation strongly identified with Kurosawa is the wipe... in his mature work, the wipe became Kurosawa's signature."（在 1940-50 年代影片中，他频繁使用"轴向剪辑"：摄影机通过一系列匹配跳切向主体推进或远离，而非用轨道镜头或叠化。另一个风格特征是"运动上剪辑"：把同一个动作用两个以上镜头呈现，而非一个不间断的镜头。与他强关联的标点是划像……成熟期划像成为黑泽明的签名。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Kurosawa

《七武士》的制作规模佐证其动作戏的编排密度：

> "The simple story... was given a full epic treatment, with a huge cast (largely consisting of veterans of previous Kurosawa productions) and meticulously detailed action, stretching out to almost three-and-a-half hours of screen time. Three months were spent in pre-production and a month in rehearsals."（这个简单的故事被赋予全史诗级处理：庞大卡司、极其精细的动作设计，片长近三个半小时。前期制作三个月、排练一个月。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Kurosawa

**导演要点**：黑泽明体系 = ①同一动作多机位/多镜头分段呈现（cut on motion，即今天的"动作连续性剪辑"源头）；②轴向推拉跳切代替轨道移动；③划像作为场景间的强标点。

### 1.5 范式二：成龙——身体喜剧 + 真实特技 + 极致重拍

成龙把动作戏变成"身体喜剧"，且以真特技与反复重拍著称：

> "Chan has performed most of his own stunts throughout his film career, which are choreographed by the Jackie Chan Stunt Team... Chan and his team undertake many of the stunts performed by other characters in his films, shooting the scenes so that their faces are obscured."（成龙职业生涯大部分特技亲自上阵，由其成家班编排……许多配角特技也由他和团队完成，拍摄时遮挡面部。）
> "Dragon Lord (1982), which featured a pyramid fight scene that holds the record for the most takes required for a single scene, with 2900 takes"（《龙少爷》中的金字塔打斗场面保持单场景重拍次数纪录：2900 条。）
> "Critics have compared his comedic stunts in Project A to Buster Keaton, who was also known to perform his own stunts"（评论界将《A计划》中的喜剧特技与同样亲自上阵的巴斯特·基顿相比。）
> "American filmmaker Quentin Tarantino classified Chan's style of acting and filmmaking as physical comedy, and considered him one of the greatest in the genre."（昆汀·塔伦蒂诺将成龙的表演与执导风格归类为"身体喜剧"，认为他是该类型最伟大者之一。）
> "Edgar Wright... notes that, in contrast to other action heroes... Chan presents himself as a loveable 'goofball' underdog who overcomes the odds with almost 'superhuman' acrobatic stunts and fighting abilities."（埃德加·赖特指出：与布鲁斯·李等动作英雄相反，成龙把自己呈现为可爱的笨拙弱者，以近乎超人的杂技特技与格斗能力翻盘。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Chan

港片史视角的位置：

> "He was succeeded in the 1980s by Jackie Chan—who popularized the use of comedy, dangerous stunts, and modern urban settings in action films."（80 年代接棒的是成龙——他在动作片中普及了喜剧、危险特技与现代都市场景。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_action_cinema

**导演要点**：成龙范式 = ①动作即喜剧（身体作为笑点载体）；②真特技的"现场感"（无替身剪辑痕迹，一次拍成）；③重拍到极致换取动作精度；④利用环境道具（"use of his surroundings to fight people in unique ways"，见同词条）——给 AI 场景的启示：动作设计要"物件互动化"，而非抽象挥拳。

### 1.6 范式三：吴宇森——"子弹芭蕾"与慢动作抒情

> "He is known for his highly chaotic 'bullet ballet' action sequences, stylized imagery, Mexican standoffs, frequent use of slow motion and allusions to wuxia, film noir and Western cinema."（他以高度混乱的"子弹芭蕾"动作段落、风格化意象、墨西哥对峙、频繁使用慢动作著称。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Woo

英雄血染（heroic bloodshed）类型对枪战场面的动作设计描述：

> "Pistols and submachine guns are frequently utilized by the heroes due to the light weight they provide, enabling their wielders to move more quickly. They are frequently dual wielded. The heroes are extremely agile and implement rolls, dives, slides, and falls while they duel, making for a graceful, ballet-like performance in the midst of gunfire."（主角常用手枪与冲锋枪，因其轻便、移动更快；常双持。主角极为敏捷，对决中使用翻滚、鱼跃、滑铲、摔倒，在枪火中形成优雅的芭蕾式表演。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroic_bloodshed

慢动作的抒情用法示例：

> "Another example is Face/Off, in which John Woo used the same technique in the movements of a flock of flying pigeons."（另一例是《变脸》，吴宇森把慢动作用于一群飞鸽的运动。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_motion

**导演要点**：吴宇森范式 = ①慢动作不用于"打击瞬间"而用于"动作的抒情/仪式感"（枪火中的腾挪、飞鸽）；②双持武器 + 敏捷身法 = 用身体语言把枪战舞蹈化；③墨西哥对峙 = 用静止张力反衬动作爆发。

### 1.7 动作场面的预制工具：分镜与预可视化

> "For fast-paced action scenes, monochrome line art might suffice."（快节奏动作场面，单色线条分镜就够用。）——分镜是导演把动作段落"先拍一遍"的低成本手段。

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storyboard

> "Previsualization... is usually employed in scenes that involve stunts, special effects (such as chroma key), or complex choreography and cinematography."（预可视化通常用于涉及特技、特效或复杂编排与摄影的场景。）
> "Previsualization's advantage is that it allows a director, cinematographer, production supervisor, or VFX supervisor to experiment with different staging and art direction options... without incurring actual production costs."（预可视化的优势：让导演/摄影/制片/视效总监在零生产成本下试验不同调度与美术方案。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Previsualization

## 二、转场设计：每种转场的功能与适用场景

### 2.1 匹配剪辑 Match Cut —— 用构图相似性建立隐喻连接

> "A match cut is a cut from one shot to another in which the composition of the two shots are matched by the action or subject and subject matter."（匹配剪辑：两个镜头之间构图/动作/主体相匹配的剪辑。）
> "A graphic match... occurs when the shapes, colors and/or overall movement of two shots match in composition... rather than the seamless cuts of continuity editing within a scene, the term 'graphic match' usually denotes a more conspicuous transition between (or comparison of) two shots via pictorial elements. A match cut often involves a graphic match, a smooth transition between scenes to create an element of metaphorical (or at least meaningful) comparison."（图形匹配：两镜的形状/颜色/整体运动在构图上匹配……匹配剪辑常含图形匹配，在场景间制造隐喻性比较。）
> "A match cut contrasts with the conspicuous and abrupt discontinuity of a jump cut."（匹配剪辑与跳切的突兀断裂形成对照。）

**经典例证（2001 太空漫游）**：

> "After a hominid discovers the use of bones as a tool and a weapon, he throws one triumphantly into the air. As the bone spins in the air, there is a match cut to a much more advanced tool: an orbiting satellite. The match cut helps draw a connection between the two objects as exemplars of primitive and advanced tools respectively, and serves as a summary of humanity's technological advancement."（骨棒抛向空中旋转，匹配剪辑切到轨道卫星——连接原始工具与先进工具，概括人类技术演进。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Match_cut

**导演要点**：匹配剪辑的功能 = ①隐喻/比较（形状、颜色、运动方向的图形匹配）；②时间跳跃但视觉连续；③常用作"画龙点睛"的场景转换，而非普通剪辑。**注意其反面**：匹配剪辑强调"可见的转场"，与连续性剪辑的"隐形剪辑"相反。

### 2.2 跳切 Jump Cut —— 断裂时间、暴露媒介、加速节奏

> "A jump cut is a cut in film editing that breaks a single continuous sequential shot of a subject into two parts, with a piece of footage removed to create the effect of jumping forward in time."（跳切：把一个连续镜头剪成两段，抽掉中间素材，制造时间向前跳的效果。）
> "This kind of cut abruptly communicates the passing of time, as opposed to the more seamless dissolve. For this reason, jump cuts are considered a violation of classical continuity editing... Jump cuts tend to draw attention to the constructed nature of the film."（跳切突兀地传达时间流逝，与更平滑的叠化相反；因此被视为对古典连续性剪辑的违反……跳切倾向于让观众注意到电影的构造性。）
> "Jump cuts can add a sense of speed to the sequence of events."（跳切能给事件序列增加速度感。）

**30 度法则**（跳切的反面教材即连续性剪辑的硬规则）：

> "Continuity editing uses a guideline called the '30-degree rule' to avoid the appearance of jump cuts. The 30-degree rule advises that for consecutive shots to appear seamless and continuous in time, the camera position must vary at least 30 degrees from its previous position."（连续性剪辑用"30 度法则"避免跳切观感：连续两镜的机位必须至少变化 30 度，否则观众会把剪辑感受为物体位置的跳动而非视角变化。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jump_cut

**导演要点**：跳切的功能 = ①压缩时间（去掉中段）；②制造速度感；③间离效果（布莱希特式，提醒观众在看电影——见词条中《战舰波将金号》的政治用法）。对 AI 生成：相邻镜头机位若变化小于 30 度，AI 生成的"伪跳切"会暴露为错误而非风格——要么刻意跳切，要么机位变足。

### 2.3 叠化 Dissolve —— 时间的模糊度量

> "A dissolve is a type of film transition in which one sequence fades over another... A dissolve overlaps two shots for the duration of the effect... Generally, but not always, the use of a dissolve is held to indicate that a time has passed between the two scenes. Also, it may indicate a change of location or the start of a flashback."（叠化：一段画面叠在另一段上……通常用于表示两场戏之间有时间流逝，也可能表示地点变化或闪回开始。）
> "Fades and dissolves typically have a duration of 1 to 2 seconds (24–48 frames)... Short dissolves (6–12 frames) may be used to soften obvious hard cuts which may startle the viewer."（淡入淡出/叠化通常持续 1-2 秒（24-48 帧）；6-12 帧的短叠化用来柔化突兀的硬切。）
> "In narrative terms, the length of the dissolve is dictated by the mood or pacing the director or editor wishes to create."（叙事上，叠化的长度由导演想营造的情绪与节奏决定。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolve_(filmmaking)

连续性剪辑视角（叠化 = 模糊的省略号）：

> "A dissolve is a simultaneous overlapping transition from one shot to another... Both forms of transition (fade and dissolve) create an ambiguous measure of ellipsis that may constitute diegetic (narrative) days, months, years or even centuries."（叠化与淡入淡出制造模糊的省略度量，可代表剧情中的数天、数月、数年甚至数个世纪。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_editing

**导演要点**：叠化 = "时间流逝"的默认符号，时长即情绪（慢叠化 = 沉重/怀旧，快叠化 = 活力/新闻片节奏，见词条中《公民凯恩》两种叠化的对比）。现代片用叠化减少，但它仍是 AI 生成叙事中表示"过了一段时间"最稳的视觉语法。

### 2.4 声音转场（Sound Bridge / Split Edit / L-cut / J-cut）—— 音画错位，先声夺人

> "A split edit is a film transition from one shot to another in which transition of the audio and video happen at different times. This is often done to enhance the aesthetics or flow of the film, allowing the audience to see context either before or after of speaking rather than simply the speaking itself. Without split edit, a conversation between two people can feel like a tennis match."（分裂剪辑：音画在不同时刻切换，让观众在说话前后先看到上下文；没有它，两人对话会像打网球。）
> "Split edits are also used to hide transitions between scenes."（分裂剪辑也用于隐藏场景转换。）
> "A variant of split edit when the audio from preceding scene overlaps the video from the following scene is known as L cut. If the audio from the following scene overlaps the video from the preceding scene, this cut is known as J cut."（前一场景的声音叠到下一场景画面上 = L 切；下一场景的声音提前叠到前一场景画面上 = J 切。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_bridge（词条实际为 Split edit）

Cut (transition) 词条对 L-cut 的例证：

> "An L-cut is when video and audio are edited asynchronously. For example, the sound of approaching cars in an interior shot alerts the viewer that the next scene will most likely involve traffic or take place outside."（L 切：音画异步剪辑。例如室内镜头里出现驶近的汽车声，提示观众下一场戏与交通/户外有关。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cut_(transition)

**导演要点**：声音转场 = ①让对话剪辑不再"乒乓球化"（先闻其声/声留画走）；②隐藏场景切换（用声音预告下一空间）；③对话多机位拍摄时用音画分离平滑不同 take 的衔接。对 AI 生成：这是成本极低却最有效的"高级感"转场——先铺下一场景的环境音，再切画面。

### 2.5 划像/形状转场 Wipe —— 风格化标点（含隐形划像）

> "A wipe is a type of film transition where one shot replaces another by travelling from one side of the frame to another or with a special shape."（划像：一个镜头以移动或特殊形状的方式替换另一个。）
> "The most common uses of the wipe effect is the invisible wipe, where a camera follows a person into another room by tracking parallel to the actor. As the wall passes in front of the camera, the editor has the option of using a wipe... It is also commonly used in quick camera pans in action sequences, to make a cut invisible."（最常用的是隐形划像：摄影机平行跟随人物穿过墙体时划像；也常用于动作段落中的快速摇镜，让剪辑隐形。）
> "George Lucas made frequent use of wipes in his Star Wars films, inspired by a similar use of wipes by Akira Kurosawa."（乔治·卢卡斯在《星球大战》中频繁使用划像，灵感来自黑泽明。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wipe_(transition)

黑泽明的划像签名（见 1.4 引文）："in his mature work, the wipe became Kurosawa's signature"（成熟期划像成为黑泽明的签名）。

**导演要点**：划像 = ①高风格化的强标点（时间/地点/章节切换，星战式"章节感"）；②隐形划像 = 动作段落里顺着运动方向隐藏剪辑（跟摇 + 墙体遮挡），这是"动作连续性"的实用技巧。形状转场（心形、星形、时钟形）用于特定情绪，时钟划像本身即"时间流逝"符号。

### 2.6 三大转场功能对照（Cut 词条的核心论断）

> "The cut represents a continuous transition in setting and time—in turn, the dissolve and wipe respectively identify changes in time and setting."（硬切表示场景与时间的连续；叠化表示时间变化；划像表示地点变化。）
> "The cut, dissolve, and wipe serve as the three primary transitions."（切、叠化、划像三大基本转场。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cut_(transition)

| 转场 | 功能 | 情绪/节奏 | 适用 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 硬切 cut | 时间空间连续 | 中性、最快 | 对话正反打、动作连续性剪辑 |
| 叠化 dissolve | 时间流逝（模糊度量） | 抒情、沉重或轻快由时长决定 | 场景间过渡、蒙太奇、闪回 |
| 划像 wipe | 地点/章节变化 | 风格化、强标点 | 章节切换、动作隐形剪辑 |
| 匹配剪辑 match cut | 隐喻比较 + 时间跳跃 | 醒目、仪式感 | 主题呼应（骨棒→卫星） |
| 跳切 jump cut | 时间断裂/速度/间离 | 突兀、暴露媒介 | 压缩时间、新浪潮风格 |
| 声音转场 L/J cut | 隐藏切换、对话流畅 | 自然、无缝 | 对话戏、场景预告 |

## 三、场景内镜头逻辑：一场戏的镜头序列如何编排

### 3.1 主镜头 Master Shot —— 一切覆盖镜头的根基

> "A master shot (or short master) is a film recording of an entire dramatized scene, start to finish, from a camera angle that keeps all the players in view. It is often a long shot and can sometimes perform a double function as an establishing shot. Usually, the master shot is the first shot checked off during the shooting of a scene. It is the foundation of what is called camera coverage, other shots that reveal different aspects of the action, groupings of two or three of the actors at crucial moments, close-ups of individuals, insert shots of various props, and so on."（主镜头：从能容纳全部演员的机位完整记录整场戏，通常为全景，可兼作建立镜头，是拍摄时第一个被划掉的镜头。它是所谓"镜头覆盖"的基础——其他所有揭示动作不同侧面、关键时刻的双人/三人构图、单人特写、道具插入镜头都建立在它之上。）
> "Historically, the master shot was the most important shot of any given scene. All shots in a given scene were somehow related to what was happening in the master shot."（历史上主镜头是一场戏最重要的镜头，场中所有镜头都与其内容相关。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_shot

### 3.2 建立镜头 Establishing Shot —— 先给全空间，再进局部

> "An establishing shot... sets up, or establishes, the context for a scene by showing the relationship between its important figures and objects. It is generally a long or extreme-long shot at the beginning of a scene indicating where, and sometimes when, the remainder of the scene takes place."（建立镜头：通过展示重要人物与物体的关系来确立场景语境，通常为场景开头的全景或大远景，指明剩余场景发生在何处、何时。）
> "The establishing shot can be used at any time as a reestablishing shot. This might be necessary when a complex sequence of cuts may have served to disorient the viewer."（建立镜头可随时作为"再建立镜头"使用——当复杂的剪辑序列让观众迷失方向时尤其必要。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Establishing_shot（第二句出自 Continuity editing 词条）

### 3.3 切入 Insert（特写强调细节）与切出 Cutaway（信息差/遮剪）

插入镜头与切出镜头的关键区别——**切出拍的是主镜头里没有的东西**：

> "An insert is a shot of part of a scene as filmed from a different angle and/or focal length from the master shot. Inserts cover action already covered in the master shot, but emphasize a different aspect of that action due to the different framing. An insert differs from a cutaway as cutaways cover action not covered in the master shot. Inserts are commonly used to direct the viewer's attention to a story-relevant detail, such as a clock, a letter, a newspaper headline, or an object being handled."（插入镜头：以不同角度/焦距重拍主镜头已覆盖的动作，但用不同构图强调其不同侧面；切出镜头则覆盖主镜头未拍到的动作。插入镜头常用于引导观众注意故事关键细节——钟、信、报纸标题、被把玩的物件。）
> "Inserts and cutaways can both be vexatious for directors, as care must be taken to preserve continuity by keeping the objects in the same relative position as in the main take, and having the lighting be the same."（插入与切出都让导演头疼：必须保持物件与主镜头 take 中相对位置一致、灯光一致，以维持连续性。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insert_(filmmaking)

切出镜头的功能：

> "A cutaway is the interruption of a continuously filmed action by inserting a view of something else. It is usually followed by a cut back to the first shot."（切出：用插入别的东西的视角打断连续拍摄的动作，随后通常切回原镜头。）
> "The most common use of cutaway shots in dramatic films is to adjust the pace of the main action, to conceal the deletion of some unwanted part of the main shot, or to allow the joining of parts of two versions of that shot."（剧情片中使用切出最常见的三个目的：调整主动作的节奏、掩盖主镜头中删掉的片段、把同一镜头的两个版本拼接起来。）
> "The cutaway does not necessarily contribute any dramatic content of its own, but is used to help the editor assemble a longer sequence."（切出本身不必贡献戏剧内容，它帮助剪辑师组装更长的段落。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cutaway_(filmmaking)

**导演要点**：切入 = 强调细节（钟/信/物件特写）；切出 = ①制造信息差（观众看到角色看不到的东西）；②掩盖剪辑点（删掉不想要的片段）；③节奏调节。对 AI 生成：切出是"遮瑕"利器——两个 take 之间动作对不上时，插入一个旁观者反应或物件镜头即可掩盖。

### 3.4 正反打 Shot/Reverse Shot —— 对话戏的标准骨架

> "Shot/reverse shot (or shot/countershot) is a film technique where one character is shown looking at another character (often off-screen), and then the other character is shown looking back at the first character (a reverse shot or countershot). Since the characters are shown facing in opposite directions, the viewer assumes that they are looking at each other."（正反打：先拍 A 看向（画外的）B，再拍 B 看向 A。因为两人面向相反方向，观众假定他们正互相对视。）
> "Shot/reverse shot is a feature of the 'classical' Hollywood style of continuity editing... It is an example of an eyeline match."（正反打是好莱坞古典连续性剪辑的特征，是视线匹配的一个实例。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shot/reverse_shot

视线匹配（Eyeline Match）——正反打成立的心理机制：

> "An eyeline match is a film editing technique associated with the continuity editing system. It is based on the premise that an audience will want to see what the character on-screen is seeing."（视线匹配基于一个前提：观众想看角色在看的东西。）
> "Eyeline match also refers to the practice of setting off-camera eyelines for single shots of characters within a scene such that, when these shots are cut together, each of the characters appear to be looking at the correct character, without any confusion. Factors influencing the position of the off-camera eyeline include the 180 degree rule, camera lens/height/distance to subject and geography of the set."（视线匹配也指单镜头中设定画外视线的做法：把 A 设为看向画右、B 看向画左，剪在一起后两人仿佛互视。影响画外视线位置的因素：180 度法则、镜头焦段/高度/距离、场景地理。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyeline_match

### 3.5 180 度法则 —— 空间一致性的地基（战斗场面的特别价值）

> "The 180-degree rule is a guideline regarding the on-screen spatial relationship between a character and another character or object within a scene. The rule states that the camera should be kept on one side of an imaginary axis between two characters, so that the first character is always frame right of the second character."（180 度法则：摄影机应保持在两角色假想轴线的一侧，使 A 永远在画面右侧、B 在左侧。）
> "The 180-degree rule enables the viewer to visually connect with unseen movement happening around and behind the immediate subject and is particularly important in the narration of battle scenes."（180 度法则让观众在视觉上连接主体周围看不见的运动，在战斗场面的叙事中尤其重要。）
> "If the camera remains on one side of this line, the spatial relationship between the two characters will be consistent from shot to shot. Shifting to the other side of the characters on a cut will reverse the order of the characters from left to right and may disorient the viewer."（机位保持在线一侧，空间关系逐镜一致；切到另一侧会左右颠倒角色顺序，令观众迷失。）
> "To minimize the 'jolt' between shots... a buffer shot can be included along the 180-degree line separating each side."（为减小越轴跳切感，可在轴线上加一个缓冲镜头。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/180-degree_rule

### 3.6 一场戏的标准镜头构成（综合各词条的结构化结论）

1. **建立镜头**（Establishing）：全景/大远景给出空间、时间、人物关系（Establishing_shot）。
2. **主镜头**（Master shot）：整场戏从头到尾的完整记录，是覆盖的"地基"，先拍（Master_shot）。
3. **中景双人/多人镜头**：关键时刻的人物组合构图（Master_shot 中"groupings of two or three of the actors at crucial moments"）。
4. **正反打单人中近景**：对话戏的骨架，靠 180 度法则 + 视线匹配维持空间一致（Shot/reverse_shot；Eyeline_match）。
5. **切入 Insert**：特写强调故事关键细节（钟/信/物件）（Insert_(filmmaking)）。
6. **切出 Cutaway**：拍主镜头之外的东西——制造信息差、掩盖剪辑点、调节奏（Cutaway_(filmmaking)）。
7. **再建立镜头**（Reestablishing）：复杂剪辑序列后重新给观众空间锚点（Establishing_shot / Continuity_editing）。

**剪辑层面的支撑**（连续性剪辑的空间四件套）：

> "Just as important as temporal continuity to the overall continuity of a film is spatial continuity. And like temporal continuity, it can be achieved several ways: the establishing shot, the 180-degree rule, the eye-line match, and match on action."（空间连续性与时间连续性同等重要，靠四件套实现：建立镜头、180 度法则、视线匹配、动作匹配。）

动作匹配（Match on Action）——跨镜头动作连续的关键：

> "A match on the action is when some action occurring before the temporally questionable cut is picked up where the cut left it by the shot immediately following. For example, a shot of someone tossing a ball can be edited to show two different views, while maintaining temporal continuity by being sure that the second shot shows the arm of the subject in the same stage of its motion as it was left when cutting from the first shot."（动作匹配：剪切前的动作由下一个镜头在同一运动阶段接续。例：投球动作被剪成两个视角，第二镜必须显示手臂处于第一镜剪切时的同一运动阶段。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_editing

### 3.7 分镜/镜头清单在导演工作流中的位置

> "A film storyboard (sometimes referred to as a shooting board), is essentially a series of frames, with drawings of the sequence of events in a film... It helps film directors, cinematographers and television commercial advertising clients visualize the scenes and find potential problems before they occur."（电影分镜本质是一系列画框，帮助导演、摄影在问题发生前可视化场景。）
> "During principal photography for live-action films, scenes are rarely shot in the sequence in which they occur in the script... directors can use storyboards on set to quickly refresh their memory as to the desired effect when those shots are later edited together."（实拍中场景极少按剧本顺序拍摄，导演在片场用分镜快速回忆每镜的预期效果。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storyboard

注：英文维基百科无独立 "Shot list" 词条（请求返回 missing），分镜词条确认镜头清单（shot list）是动画工作流的标准环节："This allows the animators and directors to work out any screenplay, camera positioning, shot list, and timing issues"（Storyboard 词条）。

## 四、表演可控性：同一角色在连续镜头中的一致性

### 4.1 连续性的定义：观众感知的一致性

> "In fiction, continuity is the consistency of the characteristics of people, plot, objects, and places seen by the audience over some period of time."（在虚构作品中，连续性 = 观众在一段时间内看到的人物、情节、物件、地点的特征保持一致。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_(fiction)

### 4.2 连续性错误的分级：微错误 vs 明显错误（AI 生成的对照标尺）

> "Many continuity errors are subtle, such as minor changes between shots (like the level of drink in a glass or the length of a cigarette); these minor errors often remain due to relative indifference to the final cut. While minor errors are often unnoticed by the average viewer, other errors may be more noticeable, such as sudden drastic changes in the appearance of a character. Productions will aim to prevent such errors in continuity because they can affect the audience's suspension of disbelief."（许多连续性错误是微妙的——杯中酒的高度、香烟长度；观众常注意不到。但更明显的错误——如角色外貌的突然剧变——会破坏观众的"悬置不信"。）
> "Visual errors are instant discontinuities occurring in visual media... Items of clothing change colors, shadows get longer or shorter, items within a scene change place or disappear, etc."（视觉错误：服装变色、影子变长变短、场景中物件移位或消失。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_(fiction)

**AI 映射**：AI 逐镜生成场景时，最容易踩的正是"视觉错误"这一类——服装颜色漂移、道具位置跳动、角色面部细节漂移。维基的判定标准同样适用：**微错误观众可容忍，角色外观剧变会击穿悬置不信**。

### 4.3 制片侧的连续性措施（可移植为 AI 工作流的检查清单）

> "Most film and TV productions have a script supervisor on hand whose job is to pay attention to and attempt to maintain continuity across the chaotic and typically non-linear production schedule."（多数影视制作配备场记，在混乱且非线性的拍摄日程中维持连续性。）
> "The gathered information and photographs usually records factors both within the scene and the technical details of the production, including meticulous records of camera positioning and equipment settings."（场记记录场内因素与制作技术细节，包括机位与设备设置的精确记录。）
> "The first would be to film all the shots for a particular scene together... This allows actors to remain in costume, in character, and in the same location."（措施一：同一场戏的所有镜头一起拍，让演员保持服装、状态、地点一致。）
> "The second major technique is for costume designers, production designers, prop masters, and make-up artists to take instant photographs of actors and sets at the beginning and end of each day's shooting... This allows the various workers to check each day's clothing, set, props, and make-up against a previous day's."（措施二：服装/美术/道具/化妆每天开拍与收工拍照存档，逐日对照。）
> "The advent of advanced CGI has helped alleviate the challenge of preventing continuity errors from reaching the final cut, as it is easier to 'airbrush' the errant drink glass or cigarette than it once was."（先进 CGI 让"修掉错误的酒杯"比过去容易，缓解了连续性错误进入成片的问题。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_(fiction)

### 4.4 连续性剪辑视角：服装、道具、动作三件套

> "Continuity editing... relied on consistent graphic qualities, balanced composition, and controlled editing rhythms to ensure narrative continuity... For example, whether an actor's costume remains the same from one scene to the next, or whether a glass of milk held by a character is full or empty throughout the scene. Because films are typically shot out of sequence, the script supervisor will keep a record of continuity and provide that to the film editor for reference."（连续性剪辑依赖一致的画面质感、平衡构图、受控的剪辑节奏……例如演员服装是否跨场景一致、角色手中的牛奶杯在全场戏中始终是满的还是空的。因为电影通常不按顺序拍摄，场记会记录连续性并交给剪辑师参考。）
> "The 180-degree system in film editing ensures consistency in shot composition by keeping relative positions of characters or objects in the frame consistent. It also maintains consistent eye-lines and screen direction to avoid disorientation."（180 度体系通过保持画面中人物/物件的相对位置一致来保证构图一致性，同时维持一致的视线与银幕方向。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_editing

### 4.5 动作连续性：AI 场景中"打斗接续"的直接依据

> "Match on action technique can preserve temporal continuity where there is a uniform, unrepeated physical motion or change within a passage. A match on the action is when some action occurring before the temporally questionable cut is picked up where the cut left it by the shot immediately following."（动作匹配可保存时间连续性：剪切前的动作由下一镜在同一运动阶段接续。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_editing

另外，Insert 词条对导演的提醒（AI 同样适用）：

> "care must be taken to preserve continuity by keeping the objects in the same relative position as in the main take, and having the lighting be the same."（插入/切出镜头必须保持物件与主镜头相同的相对位置、相同灯光。）

来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insert_(filmmaking)

### 4.6 对 AI 生成工作流的四条落地原则（综合本笔记）

1. **角色锚点化（对标"场记"）**：为每个角色建立不可漂移的"锚"——脸型/发型/服装颜色/道具（对标 Continuity_(fiction) 的场记记录 + 每日照片对照）。
2. **动作接续检查（对标 match on action）**：跨镜头同一动作必须处于同一运动阶段；AI 生成打斗时，上一镜的拳/脚落点要在下一镜成立。
3. **空间一致性（对标 180 度法则）**：逐镜生成必须保持角色左右关系与视线方向一致；越轴必须用缓冲镜头（buffer shot）或刻意设计。
4. **误差分级管理（对标微错误 vs 明显错误）**：容忍杯内水位等微错误，绝不容忍服装颜色、面部特征、道具位置的"剧变"；必要时用切出镜头掩盖（对标 cutaway 的"遮剪"功能）。
