## 任务预设验证（四项全成立零证伪）

1. 麦高芬自觉运用：英维「The film's plot involves a "MacGuffin"—a term popularized by Hitchcock—which is a physical object that everyone in the film is chasing, but which has no deep relationship to the plot.」〔研S1〕+ 朴赞郁「最出色地演绎麦高芬概念的作品」〔研S6〕——双层麦高芬（表层=雕像秘密胶卷，内层=不存在的卡普兰）。
2. 误认身份结构：英维开头「a tale of mistaken identity」〔研S1〕+ 朴赞郁三段式〔研S6〕。
3. 纯娱乐自觉：特吕弗访谈「something fun, light-hearted, and generally free of the symbolism permeating his other movies」〔研S1〕+ Bogdanovich 1963「It's a fantasy. The whole film is epitomized in the title—there is no such thing as north-by-northwest on the compass.」〔研S1〕。
4. 名场面：玉米地/联合国/拉什莫尔山全部取证拍摄细节（见研S1/研S5/研S10）。
