> 触发：写志怪/国风奇幻里的妖、兽、灵的数字角色戏、动作捕捉（表演捕捉）流程、生物特效设计时加载。视效宗师卡片——表演捕捉（performance capture）与数字角色表演的根因研究。

## 一、专业签名

**乔·莱特里（Joe Letteri，全名 Joseph Bruce Letteri，1957 年生，美国宾夕法尼亚州 Aliquippa 人）**，维塔数码（Weta Digital，2021 年后更名 Wētā FX）高级视效总监，2001 年加入，此前在工业光魔（ILM）十年 [S1][S9]。截至 2026 年共获 **6 座奥斯卡**（《双塔奇兵》2003、《王者归来》2004、《金刚》2006、《阿凡达》2010、《水之道》2023、《火与灰》2026）、4 座 BAFTA（2003/2004/2006/2010）[S1][S2]。2018 年获 VES 终身成就级 Georges Méliès 奖 [S9]。

**签名手法：把"动作捕捉"升级为"表演捕捉"——数字角色不是特效，是演员。** 咕噜是"第一个必须与真人演员同框演对手戏、且观众看不出他是 CG 的角色"；金刚是"无台词的 CG 主角"；阿凡达让"整部电影的叙事由 CG 承载"。其面部系统三代演进（咕噜→金刚→阿凡达）是"数字角色表演"从诞生到成熟的完整编年史 [S6][S19][S24]。

**职业路径**：Metrolight Studios（广告/新闻图形）→ ILM（《深渊》1996 特别版、《侏罗纪公园》1993、《星战特别版》1997，10 年）→ 2001 年为咕噜加入维塔 [S9][S22]。伯克利加州大学 1981 年毕业，专业方向偏向光学/物理/天文与计算机图形 [S1][S9]。

## 三、创作思路（原话 + 出处）

### 1. 数字图像论：用代码造出世界上任何图像（自述，一手）

> "I don't draw, I don't paint, I don't animate in the traditional sense, but using a computer I can figure out how to create any image in the world – or a world yet unknown."
> 「我不画画、不涂色、不以传统意义做动画，但用电脑，我能造出世界上任何图像——或者一个未知的世界。」 [S9]

背景自述（VFX Voice 2018 采访）：他从小着迷"眼睛怎么看东西"与视错觉——"I was trying to understand how tricking the mind speaks to how you understand the world around you"（我想弄懂"骗过心智"如何反过来解释你如何理解世界）；计算机图形让他直接操作像素，"anything you could describe you could capture pixel by pixel"（任何你能描述的东西都能逐像素捕捉）[S9]。他的第一个电影镜头——《星际迷航6》Praxis 星球爆炸——就是"实拍做不出那么大的能量"时用分形云彩程序补上的 [S12]。

### 2. 咕噜突破论：数字角色第一次"是个人"（自述，一手）

> "Gollum was a breakthrough. He was the first character that had to play alongside live-action characters in a way that you couldn't tell he wasn't a real person. He was a CG character, but he was treated with every consideration that any real character would. There was an inner conflict and there was an arc. Nothing about him was thrown away. And we set about to create this villain that we wanted you to like."
> 「咕噜是突破。他是第一个必须以'观众看不出他不是真人'的方式与真人演员同框的角色。他是 CG 角色，却享有任何真实角色享有的全部考虑。他有内心冲突，有弧光。他身上没有一处是被敷衍的。我们着手创造的是一个你愿意喜欢的反派。」 [S6]

配套技术自述：

> "Not only did we come up with the idea of using performance capture and working with Andy Serkis, but we also created a technique called subsurface scattering that allowed us to create realistic skin for the first time. The combination of those elements gave us Gollum, who was believably alive and held up to real actors. Creating digital characters with that emotional range and subtlety has been the basis for all of the characters we've created since then."
> 「我们不仅发明了表演捕捉并和安迪·瑟金斯合作，还创造了次表面散射技术，第一次做出可信的皮肤。这些要素的组合造就了咕噜——可信地活着、经得起与真人演员同框。用这种情感幅度与细腻度做数字角色，是我们此后所有角色的基础。」 [S7][S8]

### 3. 表演捕捉演进论：从"不确定能用多少身体"到"穿着动捕服直接演"（自述，一手）

> "Whereas before, we weren't sure how much of it was Andy [Serkis] just recording a voice and how much of his body we could use, and experimenting with motion capture… On The Hobbit, the very first scene we did was Andy in a motion capture suit, on the stage, with all the cameras and all the lights, something that was really unheard of ten years ago… So the technology really becomes embedded with the whole filmmaking process."
> 「从前我们不确定安迪〔瑟金斯〕是只录了个声音、还是能用上他的身体，一直在拿动作捕捉做实验……到了《霍比特人》，我们拍的第一场戏就是安迪穿着动捕服、站在摄影棚里、周围全是摄影机和灯光——这在十年前简直闻所未闻……技术就这样真正嵌入了整个电影制作流程。」 [S13]

### 4. 物理性基础论：数字世界也要"实体优先"（自述，一手）

> "On Avatar, what we started to learn was in addition to the artistry, you really have to have a good foundation on the physicality of a scene. You need to know the science. The engineering, the math, the architecture, whatever is the physical counterpart to what you're creating."
> 「做《阿凡达》我们开始学到：除了艺术性，你必须在场景的物理性上有扎实的根基。你得懂科学——工程、数学、建筑，任何你正在创造之物的物理对应物。」 [S15]

此哲学师承 ILM 的丹尼斯·穆伦（Dennis Muren）："he had such a sense of history and a keen eye and was always referring back to 'how does this thing happen in the real world' and keeping our work grounded in a physical basis"（他总是回到"这东西在真实世界里是怎么发生的"，让我们的工作扎根于物理基础）[S11]。与视效密码"实体优先，数字补位"同构。

### 5. 视效隐身论（内部人视角，一手）

> "One of the things you strive for when doing visual effects is being invisible. Any effects work that stands out is bad, as the point is NOT to be obvious."
> 「做视效你要追求的一件事是隐身。任何显眼的特效都是坏的——重点就是不能显眼。」 [S18]（咕噜首席模型师 Bay Raitt 回忆维塔团队共识）

### 6. 数字角色哲学：角色即故事载体（自述，一手）

> "I'm inspired by the opportunity to create characters that allow you to tell stories in new ways. In the Apes story, telling it through the eyes of the apes gave us all the opportunity to walk back from our biases and see it told from their perspective… To bring that to the screen, you have to put a lot of effort into mastering the cinematic techniques of storytelling, while also needing to understand what makes a character come alive."
> 「我着迷于创造能以新方式讲故事的角色的机会。《人猿星球》通过猿的视角讲述，让我们都得以退后一步、从它们的视角看故事……要把它搬上银幕，你必须在讲故事的电影技法上投入大量功夫，同时还得理解是什么让一个角色活起来。」 [S9]

## 四、招牌作品拆解

### 1. 维塔数码（Weta Digital / Wētā FX）——组织即作品

- **1993 年创立**：Peter Jackson、Richard Taylor、Jamie Selkirk 三人为《罪孽天使》（Heavenly Creatures）做数字视效而成立 [S3]。
- **2001 年莱特里加入**，领导视效部门直至今日 [S1][S22]。莱特里自述加入动机："I was really interested in working on Gollum, and I knew some of the people here who were working on Lord Of the Rings. It was just sort of a perfect opportunity."（我来就是为了咕噜）[S22]。
- **Avatar 时期**：为潘多拉的动植物生命改造 MASSIVE 群体系统——"Weta Digital modified MASSIVE to give life to the flora and fauna on Pandora" [S4]。
- **2021 年分家**：Unity 以 16.25 亿美元收购维塔的技术/工具部门，视效制作部门更名 Wētā FX 继续做电影 [S5]。组织史即人物史：技术资产（管线、工具）与艺术家团队拆分为二。
- **产能规模**（维塔 wiki 数据）：《阿凡达：火与灰》交付 3,132 个镜头、占 195 分钟成片的 94%，总渲染时长约 12.48 亿渲染小时（单核约 14.2 万年），峰值日产 200-250TB 数据 [S5]。

### 2. 指环王：双塔奇兵（2002）——咕噜，表演捕捉里程碑

- 第一个与真人演员同框、观众看不出是 CG 的角色 [S6]。
- 三要素组合：表演捕捉（Andy Serkis）+ 次表面散射皮肤 + 角色优先级（内心冲突/弧光）[S6][S7]。
- **管线工业化是莱特里的直接推动**：视效总监 Bay Raitt 回忆——"When Joe Letteri came on board as the visual effects supervisor early on during the pre-production for The Two Towers, he really pushed the idea of a pipeline that allowed us to run shots through the facility at an insane pace. His goal was to have the animators be able to publish their daily work… at 5:00 pm every day, and then be able to see that work skinned, rendered, textured and composited in dailies the next morning."（莱特里在《双塔》前期加入后力推管线：动画师每天下午 5 点发布当日工作，第二天早上就能在 dailies 里看到已蒙皮、渲染、贴图、合成好的镜头）[S16]。到《双塔》完成时，**100+ 镜头同时在线上、每天全员回看** [S17]。
- 此系统成为此后所有维塔角色表演的地基 [S8]。

### 3. 金刚（2005）——无台词 CG 主角 + 生物特效三件套

- 官方定义："Kong is a CG title character who delivers a very complex performance without the aide of dialogue. Weta Digital created him with a blend of performance capture technology and keyframe animation. He has around four million hairs on his body rendered using new simulation and modeling software."（金刚是 CG 主角，在无台词帮助下交付了非常复杂的表演；维塔用表演捕捉+关键帧动画混合创造了他；身上约 400 万根毛发用新的模拟与建模软件渲染）[S19]。
- **面部系统=情感状态驱动**（莱特里自述，转引）："The motion capture we did with Kong was a new way of using technology. It's a combination of things that have been done in the past, but we've brought them together in a unique way. We have created a system that's based on emotional states. It depends on us figuring out all the muscles of the face and understanding the correspondence between a human facial system and a gorilla facial system."（我们对金刚做的动捕是一种新技术用法；我们创造了一个基于情感状态的系统——先弄清人类与大猩猩两套面部系统的肌肉对应关系）[S20]。**并明言代际关系**："The facial animation system for Kong is the next generation of the facial system we built for Gollum."（金刚的面部系统是咕噜系统的下一代）[S21]。
- **规则驱动场景**：CityBot——一套基于规则的系统处理建筑细节与纹理，用于生成数字版 1933 年曼哈顿 [S19]。
- 节奏上从指环王无缝接棒："Yes, we went from Lord of the Rings right into King Kong… we just kind of rolled straight into it."（我们直接从指环王滚进金刚）[S23]。

### 4. 阿凡达（2009）——表演捕捉 + 虚拟摄像机 + 全 CG 承载叙事

- 官方定位："the scale, detail, and complexity of Avatar showed that computer generated filmmaking had reached the point where it could carry the story, and maintain suspension of disbelief, through an entire movie."（阿凡达的规模、细节与复杂度证明：CG 电影制作已到达能承载整部电影叙事、并维持观众信念的地步）[S24]。
- **虚拟摄像机**（莱特里自述）："We took stock of everything we did and integrated it with Jim's ideas of the virtual camera. It was sort of like, 'OK, this is a new platform.'"（我们盘点了一切已有成果，与吉姆〔卡梅隆〕的虚拟摄像机理念整合——"好，这是一个新平台"）[S25]。
- **代际真相**（Gold Derby 报道）："The first 'Avatar' utilized the same system that Weta had developed for Gollum for the 'Lord of the Rings' trilogy. Though groundbreaking at the time, the system's limited tools couldn't eliminate some of the 'rubbery' look that would pop through."（第一部阿凡达用的还是维塔为指环王咕噜开发的系统；虽然当年是开创性的，但工具局限没法消除偶尔冒出来的"橡皮感"）[S27]——**技术的连续性：咕噜→阿凡达同一套系统**。

### 5. 阿凡达：水之道（2022）——APFSA 面部系统革命

- 莱特里自述换代动机："We realized that for the fidelity we needed and for the number of characters, the way that we had been doing it wasn't going to suffice… We wrote an entirely new facial system based on a neural network that could understand the relationship between muscles and skin and what you're seeing on the face."（我们意识到：要的保真度和角色数量，老办法已经不够了……我们写了一个全新的、基于神经网络的面部系统，让网络理解肌肉与皮肤的关系）[S26]。
- APFSA（Anatomically Plausible Facial System）取代 FACS 融合变形求解器——"manipulating the muscles directly on the models to get more nuanced performances"（直接在模型上操作肌肉以获得更细腻的表演）[S28]。
- **下颌是表情的主控**（莱特里自述）："the primary control for the state of your face is the jaw. Especially with dialogue, your jaw's constantly moving. It is the main driver of state"（你面部状态的主控是下颌；尤其对白时下颌一直在动，它是状态的主要驱动器）[S29]；系统基于 **178 条肌肉纤维/应变曲线**，数学本质："If you get into the math of it, then it's a big derivative chain. It is basic calculus."（说到底就是微积分）[S30]。
- 制作侧：180 磅重的 3D Sony Venice 摄影机首次水下表演捕捉 [S28]。

- 本卡"数字角色=演员"〔S6〕 = 密码"语法 4① 显形——学人的别扭处"的制作侧落地：妖的表演先有"人"的完整内心，再谈异质。

## 六、AI 提示词对接（Seedance 落地思路）

> 说明：以下为**思路映射**，非已验证提示词模板（诚实声明第 5 条）。

1. **数字角色=演员**：提示词把妖/兽写成"角色"而非"怪物"——给内心状态词（"她恨他，但更怕失去他"），表情由情感驱动：`EN: a fox-spirit woman watches him leave, grief and fury warring in her eyes, every micro-expression human and readable — she is a character, not a monster`。
2. **跨物种面部映射**：先给"人面↔妖面"肌肉对应锚（仿金刚面部系统）：`EN: her face shifts from human to fox only at the jaw, the cheek muscles stay human, emotion drives the shift, not magic`（只破"脸型"一条，肌肉逻辑保持真实——与卡通物理同构）。
3. **次表面散射皮肤**：半透明/皮肤质感描述进光照层：`EN: skin like warm jade, light passes through the ears and fingers with a soft inner glow, subsurface, not glassy`（呼应密码"透明体只做光照属性"）。
4. **生物细节量级**：毛发/鳞片给数量级锚（仿 400 万根毛发）：`EN: fur groomed strand by strand, wind lifts it in layers, each hair catches the light — dense, heavy, alive`。
5. **视效隐身**：特效段提示词避免"炫技词"，写"自然/不显眼"：`EN: the transformation happens in plain sight, no glow, no flash, just muscle and bone moving under skin`。
