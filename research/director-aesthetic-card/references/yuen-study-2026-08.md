## 通道矩阵（按本轮价值排序）

### 1. 合作导演访谈 = 武指人物方法论一手源（最强）
- **IndieWire 2000 李安访谈**（lee_indiewire_interview.txt，33KB）：`Q: Can you talk about working with fight choreographer Yuen Wo-ping?` 起成段一手话——"He's a man of invention who cares for the traditional classic Chinese fighting styles. Not just wire, he likes working on the ground."；"The biggest thing I learned from him is that martial arts film has very little to do with martial arts; it's cinema, it's expression..."；竹林戏 "It was a 10 days company move... [Wo-Ping] took the challenge begrudgingly and then we worked his men to death"；香港武行组装式拍法 "You do one shot at a time; you work just for that angle"。
- **Austin Chronicle 2001 李安访谈**（austin_defying_gravity.txt，12KB）："each time there's a revolutionary change [in fight choreography], he was the one who initiated it"；醉拳 "started kung fu comedy. People were copying that for the next 10 years"；"And then he did Once Upon a Time in China. He made Jet Li."；**威亚观核心证据** "Certainly wire works, which he hates. He is not a believer in wire works. He likes to have more grounded fights. So we married the two together."；"Fighting is to be used as an extension of internal feelings. Exchanging blows is like exchanging dialogue."；竹林 "was sexy. It's almost like a caress, uninhibited desire."；玉娇龙/俞秀莲 "one is more blue-collar, the other is more graceful"。
- 发现路径：`web_search "<合作导演> <目标人物> interview"`（恢复后一次命中）；IndieWire 老文 live 可抓。

That was a way of establishing my brand."；李连杰踢伤两个武行、"we didn't manage to capture the full force of it on film"。

### 5. 武指体系对比节 = 各武指英维条目 Style 段直接成表
- **刘家良条目**（lau_kar_leung_wiki.txt，41KB）："generally considered a purist, who sought to portray the various martial arts as authentically as possible"；"minimizing the use of wirework and trampolines that would prove the staple of other choreographers like Yuen Woo-ping"；SCMP 访谈 "My only aim in making a film is to exalt the martial arts"；《醉拳2》与成龙因打法风格冲突中途离组。
- **洪金宝条目**（sammo_hung_wiki.txt，62KB）：《三德和尚与舂米六》(1977) "what many feel to be the first out-and-out kung fu comedy film"；80 年代 "Hung's choreography, set in modern urban areas, was more realistic and frenetic - featuring long one-on-one fight scenes"；与袁和平合导《林世荣》（英文维基口径，中文维基只列袁和平——双口径标注）。
- 三大家体系表：刘家良=武术原教旨（弘扬武术）/洪金宝=身体奇观（都市硬搏）/袁和平=电影化表达（因人设招）——"刘让武术成为主角，袁让武术成为语言"为提炼句，事实基础逐格有源。

## 新坑（本轮实测）

1. **jina markdown 链接中缀断子串校验（3 假 MISS 全由此类）**：r.jina.ai 输出 `[wirework](https://en.wikipedia.org/...) and trampolines`——验证短语 "wirework and trampolines" 断匹配。与 ⑦（维基 `[[wikilink]]` 剥壳）方向相反：⑦ 剥 `[[` `]]`，本条是 jina 侧 `](url)` 中缀。解法：校验短语从渲染后显示文本取词（不含链接边界词），或 norm 删 `\]\([^)]*\)`。同轮另两类误报：拼写差异（验证词写 "copied"、原文是 "copying"——短语必须逐字来自存档，不可凭记忆变形）；链接内词（"rainstorm in [Foshan](url)"）。**MISS 先 `grep -o` 上下文核查形态，再怀疑来源**。
2. **rest_v1 plaintext 对连字符标题 404**：`Yuen_Woo-ping` 标题正确仍 404——REST API 标题规范化问题；直接 `r.jina.ai/https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<标题>` 抓渲染页免猜（本会话 4 个 wiki 条目全走此通道成功）。
3. **web_search（DDG 后端）中文查询系统性超时**：连续 4-5 次 `TimeoutException`/`No results found` 后停止重试——恢复后（英文查询）一次成功。中文资料改用 jina 直抓已知 URL 或裸词条 URL。
4. **s.jina.ai 匿名 401 需 API key**：`curl https://s.jina.ai/<查询>` 返回 `AuthenticationRequiredError`——搜索端点不免费；r.jina.ai（提取端点）免 key 是已知 URL 取正文的第一顺位。
5. **DGA 文章登录墙 / Kung Fu Magazine 404 / 李安《十年一觉电影梦》在线全文未取到 / 豆瓣中文影评搜索超时**——全部如实标「未取证到」，不硬凑（袁和平轮诚实声明四缺项）。
