## 1. 运镜语义学：每种运镜表达什么

**总原则——运动即情感语言** [S1]：镜头运动本身就是电影的情绪语言，直接作用于观众情绪反应。

> "This movement plays a considerable role in the emotional language of film images and the audience's emotional reaction to the action."（Cinematography）

### 1.1 推摄 dolly in / 拉摄 dolly out
**原则**：推拉是摄影机沿纵深方向的位移，术语上以 "dollying in / dollying out" 描述；推=逼近，拉=退远。[S2]

> "a dolly shot, also known as a tracking or travelling shot, in which the camera moves toward or away from its subject while filming... A dolly shot is generally described in terms of 'dollying in' or 'dollying out'."（Shot (filmmaking)，引 Steven D. Katz《Film Directing Shot by Shot》）

**可操作推论**（基于定义引申，非原文）：推摄物理上压缩观众与主体的距离，制造"强制关注"——对象在画框内持续放大直至占据视觉中心；拉摄拉开距离，产生疏离感或揭示环境（从"人"到"人与处境"）。

### 1.2 摇摄 pan
**原则**：摇=摄影机固定位置上的水平视角转动，语义模型是"转头环视"——用于空间揭示与关系建立。[S1]

> "panning (horizontal shift in viewpoint from a fixed position; like turning your head side-to-side)"（Cinematography）

**可操作推论**：摇摄把摄影机拟人化为"观察者"，适合建立空间内各元素的关系（A 看 B 的视线摇）、揭示环境全貌。

### 1.3 跟摄 tracking / dolly
**原则**：跟摄的核心是"跟随"，可创造运动感、沉浸感，把观众拉进动作之中。[S3]

> "The effect can be used to create a sense of movement, to follow a character or object, or a sense of immersion to draw the viewer into the action."（Tracking shot）

> "In cinematography, a tracking shot is any shot where the camera follows backward, forward or moves alongside the subject being recorded. Mostly the camera's position is parallel to the character, creating a sideway motion, tracking the character."（Tracking shot）

**可操作推论**：侧面平行跟摄=陪伴/观察（角色保持画框内稳定，观众"与他同行"）；前后跟摄=追随/驱赶。

### 1.4 手持 handheld
**原则**：手持=摄影机脱离稳定装置、由操作者手持移动；在风格谱系中与"现实主义"绑定——自然光、手持、纪录片式拍法。[S1]

> "Realism. This style of cinematography aims to create a realistic portrayal of the world, often using natural lighting, handheld cameras, and a documentary-like approach to filming."（Cinematography）

> "Most cameras can also be handheld, that is held in the hands of the camera operator who moves from one position to another while filming the action."（Cinematography）

**可操作推论**：手持抖动=在场感/纪实质感/不安定感；Steadicam 则在不牺牲运动的同时隔离抖动（同一词条：Steadicam "supporting the camera while isolating it from the operator's body movements"），用于"长镜头+平滑跟拍"。

### 1.5 升降 jib / crane
**原则**：升降=垂直位移（离地升起+固定基座上的侧摆）。[S1] 其心理语义由角度学补全：俯拍让人物显得渺小/脆弱/无力，仰拍让人物显得强大/有威胁。[S4][S5]

> "craning (moving the camera in a vertical position; being able to lift it off the ground as well as swing it side-to-side from a fixed base position)"（Cinematography）

> "A high-angle (HA) shot is a shot in which the camera is physically higher than the subject and is looking down upon the subject. The high angle shot can make the subject look small or weak or vulnerable while a low-angle (LA) shot is taken from below the subject and has the power to make the subject look powerful or threatening."（Camera angle，引 Daniel Chandler "Grammar of Television and Film"）

> "High-angle shots can make the subject seem vulnerable or powerless when applied with the correct mood, setting, and effects."（High-angle shot，引 Jennifer Van Sijll《Cinematic Storytelling》）

**可操作推论**：升降镜头在叙事上=权力关系的空间化——升起=抽离/俯瞰全局（人物渺小化），降下=进入/逼近（人物强大化）；升镜也常用于"揭示"（从小景别拉开到全景）。

## 2. 构图理论

### 2.1 三分法 rule of thirds
**原则**：画面想象为 2 横 2 纵三等分线，重要元素放在线或交叉点上；目的=制造张力与活力、避免居中与地平线对半切。[S6]

> "The guideline proposes that an image should be imagined as divided into nine equal parts by two equally spaced horizontal lines and two equally spaced vertical lines, and that important compositional elements should be placed along these lines or their intersections."（Rule of thirds）

> "The theory is that aligning a subject with these points creates more tension, energy and interest in the composition than simply centering the subject."（Rule of thirds）

> "The main reason for observing the rule of thirds is to discourage placement of the subject at the center, or prevent a horizon from appearing to divide the picture in half."（Rule of thirds）

**历史**："rule of thirds" 一词 1797 年由 John Thomas Smith 在《Remarks on Rural Scenery》中首次写下，其比例原理是"三分之二对三分之一"比精确的一半更和谐。[S6] 交叉点被称为 power point / crash point。

### 2.2 负空间 negative space
**原则**：负空间=主体周围与之间的空白，用作中性或对比背景来吸引注意力到主体上；"负空间与正空间同等重要，两者的相互作用构成成功的构图"。[S7]

> "negative space or negative volume is the empty space around and between the subject(s) of an image."（Negative space）

> "Most often, negative space is used as a neutral or contrasting background to draw attention to the main subject, which then is referred to as the positive space."（Negative space）

> "Negative space is just as important as positive space... The interaction of these two elements creates a successful composition."（Negative space，引 Lauer & Pentak《Design Basics》）

**可操作推论**：孤独/疏离/渺小的人物置于大片空景中（大远景+小主体）是负空间叙事的标准用法；负空间也给视线"休息处"（"gives the eye a 'place to rest'"）。

### 2.3 框架内框架 frame within frame
**说明**：维基百科无独立词条（已核实 404），此概念属于构图学内容，其理论依据在场面调度词条的 Composition 一节：构图=对画框内物体/演员/空间的编排，观众天然注意"失衡、错位、异常"的元素。[S8]

> "Composition is the organization of objects, actors and space within the frame, often by use of balance and symmetry for the distribution of light, color, objects and figures in a shot."（Mise-en-scène）

> "audiences are more inclined to pay attention to something off balance, out of place, or abnormal."（Mise-en-scène）

**可操作推论**：门窗、拱廊、镜框、前景物体构成"画中画"，本质是导演用画框内元素二次框选视觉中心——既强制聚焦，又暗示"窥视/囚禁/层级"等主题。Sikov 的场面调度原则支撑其意义合法性：画内每个细节都是"有意义的陈述"（见第 6 节）。

### 2.4 前景/背景层次（纵深调度）
**原则**：深焦 deep focus 保持前后景同时清晰，让远背景中的角色持续可见——这是巴赞推崇的现实主义核心手段之一。[S14]

> "director William Wyler and cinematographer Gregg Toland used deep focus to keep a significant character visible in the far background of the frame."（André Bazin，配图说明《黄金时代》）

> "The concentration on objective reality, deep focus, and lack of montage are linked to Bazin's belief that the interpretation of a film or scene should be left to the spectator."（André Bazin）

**可操作推论**：纵深调度=在单镜头内实现"多信息层并行"（前景动作+背景反应），观众自主选择看点；长镜头传统由此把"剪辑的权力"让渡给观众的视线。

### 2.5 水平线位置
**原则**：三分法应用之一=把地平线放在上/下三分线上，避免画面被地平线对半分割。[S6]

> "placing the horizon on the top or bottom line"（Rule of thirds）

**可操作推论**：地平线高位=地面主体占比大（强调地面人物/事件）；地平线低位=天空占比大（开阔、压抑或神性）；水平线倾斜（荷兰角）则制造失衡不安（维基构图学"misalignment 用于强调画面某部分"原则可外推）。

## 3. 色彩理论

### 3.1 色彩心理学：颜色确实携带情绪
**原则**：色彩心理学=研究颜色作为人类行为决定因素；颜色具有引发特定情绪的性质。[S10]

> "Color psychology is the study of colors and hues as a determinant of human behavior. Color influences perceptions that are not obvious, such as the taste of food. Colors have qualities that may cause certain emotions in people."（Color psychology）

**冷暖对立** [S10]：红=唤起/兴奋，蓝=放松；暖色药片（红/黄）倾向于"兴奋剂"预期，冷色药片（蓝/紫）倾向于"镇静剂"预期（预期效应，非颜色直接作用）。

> "tones of red lead to feelings of arousal while blue tones are often associated with feelings of relaxation."（Color psychology）

> "hot-colored (red, yellow, etc.) pills working better as stimulants and 'cool-colored' (blue, purple, etc.) pills working better as depressants."（Color psychology，引 BMJ 综述）

**文化限定**：色彩联想因文化、年龄、性别而异——导演用色需考虑受众语境 [S10]。

### 3.2 色彩分级 color grading 的叙事功能
**原则**：调色的首要叙事功能=增强/改变场景情绪，是"影片视觉层面的配乐"；当代趋势从技术校正走向创造性目标（建立风格化 look、设定场景情绪）。[S9]

> "Enhancing and/or altering the mood of a scene — the visual equivalent to the musical accompaniment of a film"（Color grading）

> "the trend today is increasingly moving towards creative goals such as improving the aesthetics of an image, establishing stylized looks, and setting the mood of a scene through color."（Color grading）

**可操作推论**：调色是"情绪基调的全局开关"——冷青蓝=压抑/疏离/悬疑，暖橙黄=怀旧/亲密/希望；全片色板统一=风格，场景间色板跳变=情绪转折（如《教父》室内暖黄=家庭权力场 vs 室外冷灰=外部世界）。

### 3.3 色彩-情绪的场面调度用法
**原则**：场面调度中，颜色直接外化人物心境——明亮色=快乐，暗色=悲伤。[S8]

> "Mise-en-scène can be used to suggest a character's state of mind, whether that be happy with bright colors, or sad with darker colors."（Mise-en-scène，引 Barsam & Monahan《Looking at Movies》）

**可操作推论**：饱和度与情绪强度的关系在电影实践中的用法=高饱和对应情感强度高（梦幻/狂喜/警示），低饱和/褪色对应情感抽离、回忆、压抑（闪回段落常用）。

## 4. 剪辑两大传统：苏联蒙太奇学派 vs 长镜头传统

### 4.1 苏联蒙太奇学派：剪辑创造意义

**核心命题**：蒙太奇（法语"装配/剪辑"）是苏联电影理论对世界电影的首要贡献；爱森斯坦断言蒙太奇是"电影的神经"，其意义来自"独立镜头之间的碰撞"。[S11]

> "Eisenstein's view that 'montage is an idea that arises from the collision of independent shots' wherein 'each sequential element is perceived not next to the other, but on top of the other' has become most widely accepted."（Soviet montage theory）

> "montage is 'the nerve of cinema'"（Soviet montage theory，引 Eisenstein "A Dialectic Approach to Film Form"）

**辩证剪辑** [S11]：蒙太奇与连续性剪辑的根本区别——连续性剪辑服从预定叙事、以空间连贯定位观众；蒙太奇认为"影像的辩证碰撞创造影片意义"，空间可以是断裂的（例：维尔托夫《持摄影机的人》）。[S11]

> "Continuity maintains a subservience to a predetermined narrative. Montage, on the other hand, holds that the dialectical collision of images creates a film's meaning, and thus is less concerned with a script than it is the synthesis of associations between shots."（Soviet montage theory）

**库里肖夫效应** [S11]：同一张中性表情的脸，与汤、棺材婴儿、沙发上的女人分别剪辑，观众对表情的解读随之改变——证明"镜头间关系"本身能唤起特定反应（该结论 1992 年被 Stephen Prince & Wayne Hensley 质疑为"电影神话"，但作为理论遗产其影响力毋庸置疑）。

> "In a pinnacle experiment, Kuleshov combined independent shots of Ivan Mosjoukine, a bowl of soup, a baby in a coffin, and a woman on a sofa. The strategic ordering of the shots had a marked effect on audience interpretation of the Mosjoukine's neutral expression."（Soviet montage theory）

**蒙太奇序列的现代含义** [S12]：今天好莱坞的 "montage sequence" 指用短镜头序列压缩时空信息（如《洛奇》训练蒙太奇）——与苏联"时间从属于影像碰撞"的象征性蒙太奇已完全不同，两者都成立、用途不同。

> "a film editing technique in which a series of short shots are sequenced to condense space, time, and information."（Montage (filmmaking)）

> "Montages enable filmmakers to communicate a large amount of information to an audience over a shorter span of time by juxtaposing different shots, compressing time through editing, or intertwining multiple storylines of a narrative."（Montage (filmmaking)）

**适用场景**：观念对比/隐喻（屠宰场↔工人屠杀）、节奏与情绪冲击（敖德萨阶梯）、信息压缩（时间流逝训练蒙太奇）。

### 4.2 长镜头传统（巴赞）：尊重时空连续

**核心命题**：巴赞认为现实主义是电影最重要的功能；他呼吁客观真实——通过深焦与"不用蒙太奇"实现，把整场戏/整部片的解读权留给观众。[S14]

> "His call for objective reality in film, as understood through the use of deep focus as well as the lack of montage, were linked to his belief that the interpretation of an entire movie or a specific scene should be left to the spectator."（André Bazin）

> "He advocated the use of deep focus (Orson Welles, William Wyler), wide shots (Jean Renoir) and the 'shot-in-depth', and preferred what he referred to as 'true continuity' through mise-en-scène over experiments in editing and visual effects."（André Bazin）

> "André Bazin... describes the mise-en-scène aesthetic as emphasizing choreographed movement within the scene rather than through editing."（Mise-en-scène，引 Pramaggiore & Wallis）

**长镜头定义** [S13]：长镜头=持续时间远长于常规剪辑节奏的镜头；常包含复杂运镜与场面调度；"镜头段落"（plan-séquence）=一个完整场景/叙事段落的单镜头，允许"真实或富有戏剧意义的前景/中景活动"同时呈现。

> "a long take (also called a continuous take, continuous shot, or oner) is shot with a duration much longer than the conventional editing pace either of the film itself or of films in general. Significant camera movement and elaborate blocking are often elements in long takes."（Long take）

> "The use of the sequence shot allows for realistic or dramatically significant background and middle ground activity. Actors range about the set transacting their business while the camera shifts focus from one plane of depth to another and back again."（Long take）

**适用场景**：强调时空真实性与连续在场感（如《True Detective》第 4 集 6 分钟跟拍长镜头的"live theatre show"式调度，导演 Cary Fukunaga 自述）[S13]；让观众自主选择看点（深焦多信息层）[S14]；仪式感/压抑感的时间体验（侯孝贤、塔可夫斯基等长镜头作者）[S13 导演名单]。

### 4.3 两派对照表

| 维度 | 蒙太奇学派（爱森斯坦） | 长镜头传统（巴赞） |
|---|---|---|
| 意义来源 | 镜头碰撞产生新意义 [S11] | 单镜头内时空连续的呈现 [S14] |
| 对剪辑 | 剪辑=电影的神经 [S11] | 剪辑是"对现实的切割"，应让位于场面调度 [S14] |
| 观众 | 被引导产生特定心理反应（pathos）[S11] | 解读权留给观众 [S14] |
| 空间 | 可断裂、可辩证对立 [S11] | 连续、纵深、深焦 [S14] |
| 代表作 | 《战舰波将金号》敖德萨阶梯 [S11] | 意大利新现实主义、威尔斯《公民凯恩》深焦 [S14] |
| 适用 | 隐喻、情绪冲击、时间压缩 [S11][S12] | 真实感、在场感、多信息层 [S13][S14] |

## 5. 声音设计

### 5.1 声音三层（对白/音效/音乐）与总负责人
**原则**：声音设计=创造媒体听觉元素的艺术与实践；"Sound Designer" 头衔 1969 年由科波拉授予沃尔特·默奇，定义为"对影片音轨所有方面负最终责任的人——从对白与音效录制到最终混音"。[S15]

> "an individual ultimately responsible for all aspects of a film's audio track, from the dialogue and sound effects recording to the re-recording (mix) of the final track"（Sound design，引 Walter Murch 定义）

> "Sound design is the art and practice of creating auditory elements of media."（Sound design）

**声音的叙事本能** [S15]：用声音唤起情绪、反映心境、强调动作，从史前仪式就开始了——声音不是附属品，而是最古老的情绪媒介。

> "The use of sound to evoke emotion, reflect mood and underscore actions in plays and dances began in prehistoric times when it was used in religious practices for healing or recreation."（Sound design）

### 5.2 叙事性声音 vs 非叙事性声音（diegetic / non-diegetic）
**原则**：diegetic=角色能听到的、属于虚构世界的声音（剧中音乐/现场音效）；non-diegetic=只有观众能听到的配乐/解说。两者混合叫 source scoring。[S16]

> "Diegetic music, also called source music, is music that is part of the fictional world portrayed in a narrative... and is thus knowingly performed or heard by the characters."（Diegetic music）

> "This is in contrast to non-diegetic music, which is incidental music or a film score that is heard by the viewer but not the characters."（Diegetic music）

**进阶维度** [S16]：metadiegetic=角色想象/幻觉中的声音（"sounds imagined by a character within the film, such as memories, hallucinatory sounds, and distorted perspectives"）；cross-over diegesis=声音从叙事域跨越到非叙事域（如《鸟人》全部配乐实为街头艺人演奏）——用于打破第四面墙或制造惊奇。

**可操作推论（三层分工）**：对白=信息与人物；音效=空间真实性与身体触感（Foley）；音乐=情绪引导。三层可交叉调度：音乐转 diegetic（角色关掉收音机，情绪立刻"落地"）、音效升格为音乐（节奏化剪辑）。

### 5.3 声音透视 sound perspective
**说明**：维基百科无独立词条（已核实 404），该概念在电影声音学中=声音的距离感/空间感与画面空间一致（近景声音近、远景声音远、室内混响与房间匹配）。可操作版本：混响量=空间大小，音量/高频衰减=距离，声像位置=画内方位；其"主观化"形态即上述 metadiegetic 声音——角色主观听到的变形声音（如耳鸣、水下闷响）[S16]。

> "metadiegetic sound, which are sounds imagined by a character within the film, such as memories, hallucinatory sounds, and distorted perspectives."（Diegetic music）

### 5.4 无声的运用
**原则**：空白在艺术中的价值与音乐中的休止类似——但只有与相邻的"有"并置时才成立；存在"惰性空白"与"活性空白"之分。[S7]

> "The use of negative space in art may be analogous to silence in music, but only when it is juxtaposed with adjacent musical ideas. As such, there is a difference between inert and active silences in music, where the latter is more closely analogous to negative space in art."（Negative space）

**可操作推论**：抽掉音乐/环境音（"活性无声"）=注意力骤然集中、心理张力顶点、死亡的寂静；关键不在"没有声音"，而在"声音刚刚消失"。

## 6. 场面调度 mise-en-scène 四要素

**定义** [S8]：mise-en-scène=摄影机前出现的一切——布景、道具、服装、演员，甚至灯光；这些元素通过营造时空感与情绪来表达影片的愿景。埃德·西科夫称之为"画面内全部表达性内容的总和"。

> "When applied to the cinema, mise-en-scène refers to everything that appears before the camera—the sets, props, costumes, actors, and even the lighting. These elements help to express a film's vision by generating a sense of time and space, as well as setting a mood."（Mise-en-scène，引 Bordwell & Thompson《Film Art》）

> "the totality of expressive content within the image"（Mise-en-scène，Ed Sikov 定义）

> "every detail... as 'a statement of meaning' regardless of whether the director consciously intended that or not."（Mise-en-scène，Ed Sikov）

### 6.1 布景 Set design
**原则**：布景可放大人物情绪、设定气氛，影响影片的物理/社会/心理/情感/经济/文化意义。[S8]

> "Set design can be used to amplify character emotion or to set the mood, which effects the physical, social, psychological, emotional, economic, and cultural significance in the film."（Mise-en-scène）

**可操作推论**：空间也参与叙事——"空间的使用决定画面是太挤还是太空"；空房间=孤独/平静/空虚（"an empty space may represent peace and an empty mind"）。[S8]

### 6.2 服装 Costume
**原则**：服装用特定颜色/设计区分人物、指认角色身份；常以红色吸引注意，或以明暗对比传达信息。[S8]

> "By using particular colors or designs, narrative cinema uses costumes to make clear distinctions between characters or to signify a particular character."（Mise-en-scène）

### 6.3 表演 Performance
**原则**：表演艺术用动作、手势、语调实现角色；西方电影表演从 19 世纪舞台化夸张走向自然主义，其根基是斯坦尼斯拉夫斯基的方法派——演员完全沉浸于角色。[S8]

> "The art of acting uses movement, gesture, and intonation to realize a character for the stage, motion picture, or television."（Mise-en-scène）

> "This more naturalistic style of acting is largely influenced by Konstantin Stanislavski's theory of method acting, which involves the actor fully immersing themselves in their character."（Mise-en-scène）

### 6.4 灯光 Lighting
**原则**：灯光的强度、方向、质感影响观众对人物、动作、主题与情绪的理解；高光引导注意，阴影隐藏事物、制造神秘或恐惧。[S8]

> "The intensity, direction, and quality of lighting can influence an audience's understanding of characters, actions, themes, and mood."（Mise-en-scène）

> "Highlights, for example, call attention to shapes and textures, while shadows often conceal things, creating a sense of mystery or fear."（Mise-en-scène）

### 6.5 四要素如何共同构建意义
**原则**：四要素由导演与美术总监统一调度；每个细节都是导演的有意识选择，向观众传达影片主题信息；巴赞的场面调度美学=在场景内编排运动而非依赖剪辑。[S8]

> "The director (who controls props, costumes, lighting and sound)... work together to make sure it fits the film well before production begins."（Mise-en-scène）

> "André Bazin... describes the mise-en-scène aesthetic as emphasizing choreographed movement within the scene rather than through editing."（Mise-en-scène）

**可操作推论（组合拳）**：同一场戏中，布景（环境信息）+服装（身份信息）+表演（心理信息）+灯光（情绪信息）四层叠加 = 单镜头内的高密度叙事；省略也构成意义（"不仅包括在场之物，也包括缺席之物"——如本该挂画的白墙）[S8]。
