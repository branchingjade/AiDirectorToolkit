# 一、人物塑造体系

## 1.1 角色小传（character profile）的核心要素

**要点：角色小传分两层——「表面档案」（外形/出身/财产）与「内在机制」（世界观/欲望/创伤）。编剧层的关键在后者，且必须通过「动作与言谈」外显，而不是靠旁白贴标签。**

- **直接刻画 vs 间接刻画（S1）**：刻画人物只有两条路——作者直接告诉观众，或让观众从行为/言语/外貌/互动中自行推断。电影/戏剧只能走后一条路（进不了角色脑子）：
  > "Direct or explicit characterization — The author literally tells the audience what a character is like... Indirect or implicit characterization — The audience must infer for themselves what the character is like through the character's thoughts, actions, speech (choice of words, manner of speaking), physical appearance, mannerisms and interaction with other characters..."
  > "in drama, it is not possible to 'go inside the character's head' in the way possible in a novel, meaning this method of character exposition is unavailable."
- **前史/创伤（S5）**：backstory 的定义 = 正片开始前发生在角色身上的事件集合，其功能是给主线"深度与可信度"；演员也会自建角色前史来丰满角色：
  > "a backstory or the background (information) is a set of events that establishes a character's past or that precedes and leads up to the main plot."
  > "As a literary device, backstory is often employed to lend depth or believability to the main story."
- **前史使用禁忌——记忆倒叙不能是巧合（S5，Orson Scott Card）**：
  > "If it's a memory the character could have called to mind at any point, having her think about it just in time to make a key decision may seem like an implausible coincidence..." "If the memory is going to prompt a present decision, then the memory in turn must have been prompted by a recent event."
- **⚠️ 词条陷阱警示（S23）**：`Character_creation` 维基条目实为 RPG 角色创建（掷骰/加点/职业模板），其可借鉴点仅是"角色属性清单"（physical/mental/psychological/social attributes + back-story + possessions）的结构化思维，编剧人物小传应另见 Characterization（S1）：
  > "This article is about customizable game characters. For the process of designing fictional characters, see Characterization."
- **性格外显清单（S1，Character's voice 节）**：语域、词汇、语速、口癖、教养、口音、教育、场合——"说话方式必须从内向外长出来"：
  > "Tone of voice, volume, rate of delivery, vocabulary, inflection, emphasis, pitch, topics of conversation, idioms, colloquialisms, and figures of speech: all of these are expressions of who the character is on the inside."
  > "A character's manner of speech must grow from the inside out. The speaking is how their essential personality leaks out for the world to see; it is not the sum total of their personality."

## 1.2 角色内在矛盾如何驱动行为（believable contradiction）

**要点：圆形人物 = 多原型叠加 + 缺陷；弧光 = 由「不敢/不能」到「不得不改变」；而亚里士多德早就给出判定标准——角色即「抉择的显现」。**

- **圆形 vs 扁平（S3，E. M. Forster）**：扁平人物一维单纯；圆形人物复杂、会发展、能"让读者意外"——意外性即内在矛盾的外部表现：
  > "Flat characters are two-dimensional, in that they are relatively uncomplicated. By contrast, round characters are complex figures with many different characteristics, that undergo development, sometimes sufficiently to surprise the reader."
- **复杂 = 多原型组合（S1，荣格十二原型 + 批评）**：单一原型 = 扁平；现实人物是多原型混合、有主有次。同时警告：原型套用常把人物简化成陈词滥调：
  > "'Flat' characters may be considered so because they stick to a single archetype without deviating, whereas 'complex' or 'realistic' characters will combine several archetypes, with some being more dominant than others – as people are in real life."
  > "the use of these archetypes is often reductive and unhelpful for many writers since it simplifies character complexity into clichéd tropes."
- **弧光的定义（S2）**：从"某种人"逐渐变成"另一种人"，常是向对立面（如贪婪→仁慈）的转变：
  > "If a story has a character arc, the character begins as one sort of person and gradually transforms into a different sort of person in response to changing developments in the story."
- **矛盾驱动行为的机制（S2 + S20）**：主角起初**缺乏能力/知识/资源/盟友**，无法战胜对立力量 → 必须改变自己 → 弧光与三幕并行（激励事件→第一转折点→戏剧性问题→高潮作答）。这就是"内在缺陷/谎言驱动外部行为"的结构学表达：
  > "A driving element of the plots of many stories is that the main character seems initially unable to overcome opposing forces, possibly because they lack skills or knowledge or resources or friends. To overcome such obstacles, the main character must change..."
  > "Part of the reason protagonists seem unable to resolve their problems is because they do not yet have the skills to deal with the forces of antagonism that confront them. They must learn new skills and arrive at a higher sense of awareness of who they are and what they are capable of in order to deal with their predicament, which in turn changes who they are."（S20）
- **亚里士多德：角色 = 抉择（S3）**：人物不靠描述成立，靠"在情境中做出决定"成立；不决定、不回避的人没有"性格"。这直接给"矛盾驱动行为"提供了最古老的权威依据：
  > "He defines character as 'that which reveals decision, of whatever sort' (1450b8)."
  > "If, in speeches, the speaker 'decides or avoids nothing at all', then those speeches 'do not have character'."
- **创伤转化为弧光（S2 电影例）**：《太阳帝国》Jim 因战争创伤被迫改变；《教父》Michael 从"不想沾家族生意"到"成为教父"——创伤事件（父亲遇刺）是改变引擎：
  > "In Empire of the Sun, Jim begins as a carefree young boy. After the Japanese take over Shanghai and he is separated from his family, he is forced to suffer trauma because of the war."
- **戏剧性问题必须落到主角行动（S2）**：
  > "The dramatic question should be framed in terms of the protagonist's call to action, for example, Will X recover the diamond? Will Y end up with their love interest? Will Z capture the killer?"

## 1.3 配角设计（镜像 / 对照 / 陪衬）

**要点：配角不是"戏少的角色"，而是功能性光学装置——foil 的英文原义是"垫在宝石下的箔片"，让主角的光芒更亮。**

- **Foil 定义（S4）**：与主角（或另一角色）形成对照、从而凸显主角特质的角色；反派也可兼作 foil：
  > "In any narrative, a foil is a character who contrasts with another character, typically, a character who contrasts with the protagonist, in order to better highlight or differentiate certain qualities of the protagonist." "A foil to the protagonist may also be the antagonist of the plot."
- **Foil 词源（S4）**——直接决定了"陪衬"的用法：
  > "The word foil comes from the old practice of backing gems with foil to make them shine more brightly."
  > 莎剧《亨利四世》上篇：Prince Hal——"And like bright metal on a sullen ground / My reformation, glittering o'er my fault / Shall show more goodly and attract more eyes / Than that which hath no foil to set it off."
- **经典 foil 结构（S4）**：同构对照——越相似，对照越锋利。《哈利·波特》Harry 与 Tom Riddle 同是混血孤儿、同样聪明强大，唯独道德选择不同 → 主题"选择高于宿命"：
  > "While similar in looks and capability, they are dissimilar in their morality, emphasising the importance of choice as opposed to destiny."
- **镜像关系（S4）**：《弗兰肯斯坦》博士与怪物互为 foil（"functioning to compare one to the other"）；《麦克白》Macbeth 与 Banquo；《哈姆雷特》Hamlet 与 Laertes（"I'll be your foil, Laertes"）。
- **副线本身可作主线的 foil（S4）**——副线与主线的对照用法（详见第四章）：
  > "In some cases, a subplot can be used as a foil to the main plot. This is especially true in the case of metafiction and the 'story within a story' motif."
- **喜剧陪衬机制（S4）**：straight man（捧哏/正经人）= 喜剧中的 foil 形式。
- **配角类型学（S3）**：recurring（常驻配角）/ guest（客串，不承载长线因果）/ breakout character（配角爆红转正）——配角也分"承载结构"与"不承载结构"两种，编剧要分清：
  > "unlike core characters, for which any significant conflict must be traced during a considerable time"（客串角色无需承担因果闭环）
- **反面教材（S3）**：Mary Sue——"virtually devoid of flaws" 的扁平角色，即"无内在矛盾"的失败案例。

### 人物塑造 · 可操作清单
1. 小传写两层：表层档案（外形/职业/社会关系）+ 内在机制（创伤事件、核心谎言/世界观、真实欲望、价值观、秘密），后者才是驱动源。
2. 每个主角写一句「矛盾句」：他想要 X（外在目标），但他真正需要的是 Y（内在需求），而他的缺陷 Z 恰好阻止 X→Y 的转化。
3. 用"抉择"验证角色：把角色放进 3 个两难情境，看他的选择是否一致地暴露内在矛盾；没有抉择戏的角色等于没性格（S3）。
4. 配角矩阵：为每个主要配角标注其与主角的关系类型——foil（对照）/ 镜像（同构殊途）/ 支持（mentor/co-protagonist，S20）；避免无功能的"站桩配角"。
5. 前史创伤只在"被当下事件触发"时闪回（S5 Card 规则），不凭空巧合。

# 二、类型惯例（genre conventions）

## 2.0 总纲：类型 = 惯例 + 观众期待

- **类型定义（S6）**：类型按叙事元素、美学路径、**情绪反应**分类；由"conventions, iconography, settings, narratives, characters and actors"界定；**观众期待是类型的构成性要素**：
  > "A film genre is a stylistic or thematic category for motion pictures based on similarities either in the narrative elements, aesthetic approach, or the emotional response to the film."
  > "film genres are usually delineated by 'conventions, iconography, settings, narratives, characters and actors'."
  > "a key role is also played by the expectations of an audience about a film, as well as institutional discourses that create generic structures."
- **类型片 = 重复与变奏（S6）**：
  > "commercial feature films [that], through repetition and variation, tell familiar stories with familiar characters and familiar situations".
- **类型影响技法（S6）**：黑色电影的闪回+低调光、恐怖片的紧构图、爱情片的弦乐配乐——类型不是题材标签，是整套视听与叙事约定。

## 2.1 悬疑/惊悚：信息差 + 红鲱鱼

- **悬念的两种形态（S7）**——这是"信息差"的精确表述：
  > "It may be of several major types: in one, the outcome is uncertain and the suspense resides in the question of who, what, or how; in another, the outcome is inevitable from foregoing events, and the suspense resides in the audience's anxious or frightened anticipation in the question of when."
  > "Readers feel suspense when they are deeply curious about what will happen next, or when they know what is likely to happen but do not know how it will happen."
  - 推论：第一种靠"未知"（信息不对称：观众知道得比角色少），第二种靠"预知"（观众知道得比角色多，等待"何时"降临——即戏剧反讽）。
- **悬念的对象必须有共情（S7）**：
  > "particularly as it affects a character for whom the audience feels sympathy."
- **伏笔是悬念的助手（S7）**：
  > "An adjunct to suspense is foreshadowing."
- **红鲱鱼定义（S8）**：把观众导向错误结论的假线索/假对象，悬疑与推理的标配：
  > "A red herring is something that misleads or distracts from a relevant or important question... a false clue intended to lead readers or audiences toward a mistaken conclusion."
- **红鲱鱼案例（S8）**：《达·芬奇密码》Aringarosa 主教（名字本身是意大利语"红鲱鱼"的双关）；《血字的研究》现场德文 Rache——误导警方与读者以为是德国人：
  > "The character's name is a loose Italian translation of 'red herring' (aringa rosa...)."
- **红鲱鱼的正确使用（S8）**：看似可信（"seemingly plausible, though ultimately irrelevant"）——假线索必须本身合理，不能是明摆着的幌子。

## 2.2 爱情片：meet-cute + 障碍 + 团圆承诺

- **类型定义与障碍清单（S9）**——爱情片的核心冲突 = 障碍，且障碍类型有固定谱系：
  > "These films focus on the search for romantic love as the main plot focus. Occasionally, romance lovers face obstacles such as finances, physical illness, various forms of discrimination, psychological restraints, or family resistance."
  > "Romantic dramas usually revolve around an obstacle that prevents deep and true love between two people."
- **观众期待：圆满结局（S9）**：
  > "Romantic films serve as great escapes and fantasies for viewers, especially if the two leads finally overcome their difficulties, declare their love, and experience their 'happily ever after', often implied by a reunion and final kiss."
- **Meet-cute 定义（S10）**——爱情喜剧的标志性开场惯例：
  > "A meet cute is a scene in romantic fiction in which two people meet for the first time, typically under unusual, humorous, or adorable circumstances, and go on to form a future romantic couple."
  > "Frequently, the meet cute leads to a humorous clash of personalities or of beliefs, embarrassing situations, or comical misunderstandings that further drive the plot."
- **好莱坞铁律（S10，George Axelrod 台词）**——初次相遇必须"cute"，不能像正常人社交场合那样平淡：
  > "Dear boy, the beginning of a movie is childishly simple. The boy and girl meet. The only important thing to remember is that—in a movie—the boy and the girl must meet in some cute way. They cannot... meet like normal people at, perhaps, a cocktail party or some other social function."
- **罗杰·伊伯特的实操定义（S10）**：
  > "somebody runs into somebody else, and then something falls, and the two people began to talk, and their eyes meet and they realize that they are attracted to one another."
- **浪漫喜剧主题惯例（S13）**：reinforce 社会对爱的信念（一见钟情、爱战胜一切）：
  > "central themes that reinforce societal beliefs about love (e.g., themes such as 'love at first sight', 'love conquers all', or 'there is someone out there for everyone')".

## 2.3 动作片：奇观 + 身体考验 + 生存/抵抗/复仇

- **类型定义（S11）**：
  > "The action film is a film genre that predominantly features chase sequences, fights, shootouts, explosions, and stunt work."
- **奇观 vs 叙事的张力（S11）**——动作片的核心争议，即编剧的操盘空间：
  > Bordwell: "the genre is regularly lambasted for favoring spectacle over finely tuned narrative."
  > King 反驳："the spectacle can also be a vehicle for narrative, opposed to interfering with it."
  > O'Brien 综合："best understood as a fusion of form and content. It represents the idea and ethic of action through a form in which action, agitation and movement are paramount."
- **主题惯例（S11，Yvonne Tasker）**：
  > "the most broadly consistent themes tend to be a characters quest from freedom from oppression such as a hero overcoming enemies or obstacles and physical conflicts or challenge, usually battling other humans or alien opponents."
- **结构与身体（S11）**——经典期动作片套三幕，且以"英雄肉体被考验-创伤-最终胜利"为骨架：
  > "built around a three-act structure centered on survival, resistance and revenge with narratives where the physical body of the hero is tested, traumatized and ultimately triumphant."
- **奇观单元惯例（S11）**：追车戏（"the fusion of man and machine"）等独立 spectacle 段落——对应序列化写作（见第四章八序列法）。

## 2.4 恐怖片：等待（dread）与惊吓（shock）的交替

- **类型定义（S12）**：
  > "seeking to elicit responses of fear, terror, disgust, shock, suspense, and (of course) horror from their viewers."（《电影研究词典》）
  > 核心三要素："characters with physical, psychological, or spiritual deformities"; "narratives built on suspense, surprise, and shock"; and "visual compositions that move between the dread of not seeing and the horror of seeing".（《The Film Experience》）
  - **"看不到的恐惧 ↔ 看到的恐怖"**——这就是"等待+惊吓"节奏的权威表述：恐怖片在"藏"与"露"之间来回摆动。
- **核心主题（S12，Robin Wood）**：
  > "normality is threatened by the monster."（常态被怪物威胁）
- **Jump scare 与反套路（S12）**——惊吓节拍可以被"预期化"转化为持续不安：
  > "The jump scare is a horror film trope where an abrupt change in image accompanied by a loud sound aims to surprise the viewer. This trope can also be subverted to create tension, as audiences may feel increased unease and discomfort in anticipating jump scares."
  > 镜像段落："subverting audience expectations of a jump scare in a mirror can further build tension."
- **紧构图制造焦虑（S12）**：
  > "Tight framing and close-ups are also commonly used; these can build tension and induce anxiety by not allowing the viewer to see beyond what is near the protagonist."
- **音乐惯例（S12）**：不协和音（dissonance）、无调性制造威胁感；触发 fight-or-flight 生理反应：
  > "music in horror film frequently makes us feel threatened and uncomfortable"; "Dissonance, atonality and experiments with timbre are typical techniques used by composers in horror film music."
- **两难美学（S12，Noël Carroll）**：
  > "repulsion must be pleasurable, as evidenced by the genre's popularity."

## 2.5 剧情片：人物驱动 + 戏剧性问题 + 结构传统

- **剧情片的历史定位（S1）**：19 世纪现实主义小说与心理学兴起后，"人物优先于情节"取代了亚里士多德的"情节优先"——剧情片 = 人物驱动叙事的正典：
  > "Aristotle promoted the primacy of plot over characters... This view was reversed in the 19th century, when the primacy of the character... was affirmed first with the realist novel, and increasingly later with the influential development of psychology."
- **结构惯例（S22）**：Freytag 五段（Introduction, Rise, Climax, Return or Fall, Catastrophe）；亚里士多德实为两幕（complication + dénouement，以 peripeteia 反转分隔）：
  > "He outlined the sections of the story as Introduction, Rise, Climax, Return or Fall, and Catastrophe."
  > "Aristotle, who in fact argued for a two-act structure consisting of a 'complication' and 'dénouement' split by a peripeteia."
- **戏剧性问题贯穿（S20）**：Will the boy get the girl? / Will the hero save the day? / Will the detective solve the mystery? —— 高潮作答。
- **喜剧/悲剧的角色分野（S3，亚里士多德）**：悲剧写"serious people"，喜剧写"inferior people"——角色设定即类型设定。

## 2.6 喜剧：夸张 + 错位 + 大团圆

- **类型定义与结局惯例（S13）**：
  > "The comedy film is a film genre that emphasizes humor. These films are designed to amuse audiences and make them laugh. Films in this genre typically have a happy ending, with dark comedy being an exception to this rule."
- **机制惯例（S13）**：
  - Farce 夸张："Farcical films exaggerate situations beyond the realm of possibility—thereby making them entertaining."
  - Sitcom 机制（= 喜剧副线的底层逻辑）："humor comes from knowing a stock group of characters (or character types) and then exposing them to different situations to create humorous and ironic juxtaposition."
  - Slapstick："exaggerated, boisterous physical action to create impossible and humorous situations."
  - Surreal："illogical—including bizarre juxtapositions, absurd situations, and unpredictable reactions to normal situations."
- **日复一日喜剧（S13）**：小事件被抬升到与动作片决战同等重要——"以小搏大"的喜剧编剧法：
  > "Day-in-the-life films take small events in a person's life and raise their level of importance. The 'small things in life' feel as important to the protagonist (and the audience) as the climactic battle in an action film."

### 类型惯例 · 可操作清单
1. 立项先回答：本片的"情绪承诺"是什么（笑/怕/心跳/流泪/悬念）？——类型定义即情绪响应（S6）。
2. 悬疑：先定"信息差方向"——观众比角色多（等"何时"，戏剧反讽）还是少（猜"是谁/如何"）；每幕埋 1 个看似可信的假线索（S7/S8）。
3. 爱情：meet-cute 必须"不平凡"且带性格冲突；中段障碍从五类谱系（钱/病/歧视/心理/家庭）中选；结局兑现团圆承诺（S9/S10）。
4. 动作：奇观段落必须同时推进叙事（King 观点）；全片骨架 = 生存/抵抗/复仇 + 主角身体被反复考验（S11）。
5. 恐怖：用"藏-露"节奏写惊吓——先 dread（等待），再 shock（惊吓），可反转跳吓预期制造持续不安；紧构图+不协和音辅助（S12）。
6. 喜剧：夸张 + 错位 + 铁律性大团圆；配角组用 stock types + 情境错位出笑点（S13）。

# 三、对白体系

## 3.1 对白的信息分层（表层信息 / 潜台词 / 情绪暗流）

**要点：对白铁律——一句话必须同时干多件事（信息+潜台词+情绪+行动意图）；"字面直说"是失败对白的标志（on the nose）。**

- **铁律（S14，William Sloane《The Craft of Writing》）**：
  > "There is a tentative rule that pertains to all fiction dialogue. It must do more than one thing at a time or it is too inert for the purposes of fiction."
- **潜台词定义（S15，OED）**：
  > "an underlying and often distinct theme in a conversation, piece of writing, etc."；"the implicit or metaphorical meaning (as of a literary text)".
- **"on the nose" 批评（S15）**——字面直说 = 摧毁戏剧张力：
  > "writers may be criticized for failure artfully to create and use subtext; such works may be faulted as too 'on the nose', with the characters meaning what they literally have said, undermining dramatic tension, and leaving the work too prosaic."
- **潜台词谱系源头（S15，斯坦尼斯拉夫斯基）**——表演/写作通用的"言外之意"体系：
  > "The modern concept of subtext in acting was developed significantly by Russian theatre practitioner Konstantin Stanislavski, who popularized the idea of unspoken intentions and internal motivations beneath dialogue."
- **电影潜台词的六种实现手法（S15，原文列出的电影用法）**：
  1. 借谈别人关系暗指当前关系："Have a character comment on an aspect of another person's relationship which is present in the relationship of the people in the current conversation."
  2. 身体语言："Body language is often used to convey whether a character is welcoming or threatening."
  3. 隐含指控（诱导式提问）："when a lawyer asks you 'what were you doing on the night of the 23rd', that is an implied accusation."
  4. 表情与语气："a quiet one-word answer to a question implies that the speaker is lying."
  5. 关心程度本身即潜台词："showing the amount of care, interest, and attentiveness people have for each other's opinions, desires, and character traits."
  6. 语言含意学（implicature）；海明威冰山理论同源。
- **情绪暗流 vs 表层信息（S15）**：潜台词 = "hidden beneath the verbatim wording"——观众必须"read between the lines"。

## 3.2 对白节奏：长短句 / 打断 / 停顿

- **节奏与性格绑定（S1，Character's voice）**——节奏（rhythms of speech）是人物识别标志：
  > "Different characters use different vocabularies and rhythms of speech. For example, some characters are talkative, others taciturn. The way a character speaks can be a powerful way of revealing the character's personality."
- **"盲听识别"测试（S1）**——对白写得好不好，标准是能否去掉说话人标签仍能认出是谁：
  > "In theory, a reader should be able to identify which character is speaking simply from the way they talk."
- **话标（dialogue tag）的节奏功能（S14）**：默认用 "said"（Stephen King 推荐）；花式话标（murmured/thundered，称 said-bookisms）是"写作懒惰"，因为靠标签告诉读者情绪、而不是让对白本身传达：
  > "King asserted that said is the best dialogue tag to use."
  > "said-bookisms lead to 'writerly laziness' because it is easier to use dialogue tags to tell the reader how the character is speaking than have the dialogue itself convey this, and it thus weakens the story."（Cory Doctorow）
- **停顿/留白的结构证据（S15）**：简短的"一字回答"本身就是撒谎的信号——停顿即信息。

## 3.3 语域（register）：不同场景不同说话方式

- **语域的决定因素（S1）**——性格、年龄、文化、家庭、地区、性别、教育、场合：
  > "The writer can make the characters' dialogue more realistic and interesting by considering several factors affecting how people speak: personality psychology, age, culture, family background, region, gender, education, and circumstances."
- **正式度分层（S1）**——内向者更正式、外向者更口语化；长期处于正式环境的人全时域都正式：
  > "Introverts tend to use more formal language compared to extroverts, who typically speak in a more casual and colloquial manner. Characters who spend a lot of their lives in a more formal setting often use a more formal language all the time, while others never do."
  > "Considering the degree of formality in spoken language is also useful."
- **语域切换即戏剧动作**：一个角色在不同场景（职场/家庭/恋爱）切换语域，本身就是可写的层次（S1 的"circumstances"因子）。

## 3.4 独白 vs 对话的功能分工

- **对白的功能（S14）**：让角色"说出内心"、使人物可信；重大事件用直接引语，次要交代可转述或间接引语：
  > "Dialogue plays a role in making characters in literature more believable to the reader, by allowing them to voice their internal thoughts."
  > "When significant or dramatic events are happening, dialogue can be written in direct quotation. Otherwise, speech can either be summarized as part of the narrative or written as indirect speech."
- **裸对白 = 剧本（S14，Percy Lubbock）**：纯对白不交代场景动作，写出来的就是"戏剧"而非小说——电影剧本恰恰以此为常态：
  > "If he offers nothing but the bare dialogue, he is writing a kind of play; just as a dramatist, amplifying his play with 'stage-directions'... has really written a kind of novel."
- **独白定义（S17）**：单个角色的长篇言说，用于"把思想说出声"；与 soliloquy（自言自语，其他角色听不到）、aside（短旁白）、apostrophe（对不在场者说话）相区别：
  > "a monologue... is a speech presented by a single character, most often to express their thoughts aloud."
- **独白的四种类型与功能（S17）**：
  - 内心独白（interior）：外化本不可见的内心过程；
  - 戏剧独白（dramatic）：对一个在场角色说话；
  - **主动独白（active）**：用言语达成明确目标——"speech to achieve a clear goal"；
  - 叙述独白（narrative）：讲故事，常用过去时。
  - 历史功能（罗马喜剧）：linking monologue 用来表示时间的流逝——独白可承担"时间压缩"职能。
- **独白的起源早于对白（S17）**——对白由独白演化而来："dialogue evolved from the monologue."
- **交代 vs 独白的禁区（S16）**：info-dump 是叙事大忌，放进对白里更糟；"idiot lecture"（角色互相告知彼此早已知晓的信息）必须避免：
  > "An information dump... is ill-advised in narrative and is even worse when used in dialogue."
  > "Writers are advised to avoid writing dialogues beginning with 'As you well know, Professor, a prime number is...'"
- **正解：incluing 洒点式交代（S16，Jo Walton 定义）**：
  > "the process of scattering information seamlessly through the text, as opposed to stopping the story to impart the information."
  > "Exposition works best when the author provides only the bare minimum of surface information and allows the readers to discover as they go."

### 对白体系 · 可操作清单
1. 逐行自检：这句对白同时干了 ≥2 件事吗（信息/潜台词/情绪/行动）？只干一件事 → 删或改（S14）。
2. 写"潜台词版"与"直说版"两稿对照：角色回避、撒谎、转移话题时，让表层话与真实意图错位（S15）。
3. 给每个主要角色建"语域档案"（词汇层/正式度/句长/口癖），场景切换时语域跟着变（S1）。
4. 重要信息用直接引语，交代性信息用转述/间接引语；任何对白都禁止"as you well know"式 idiot lecture（S14/S16）。
5. 独白只保留三种：主动型（有目标地说话）、内心型（关键抉择时刻）、时间压缩型；叙述型独白慎用（S17）。
6. 话标只用 said / 动作描写；写不出情绪就重写对白本身，不靠副词标签（S14）。

# 四、序列设计与副线管理

## 4.1 序列（sequence）设计：目标 / 障碍 / 转折

- **序列概念的定义（S22）**：
  > "Story is a sequence of events... Story structure is a way to organize the story's elements into a recognizable sequence."
- **八序列法（S21，Frank Daniel，USC 编剧研究生项目主任）**——序列设计的正典工具：
  > "The sequence approach to screenwriting, sometimes known as 'eight-sequence structure', is a system developed by Frank Daniel, while he was the head of the Graduate Screenwriting Program at USC. It is based in part on the fact that, in the early days of cinema, technical matters forced screenwriters to divide their stories into sequences, each the length of a reel (about ten minutes)."
  > "The story is broken up into eight 10–15 minute sequences. The sequences serve as 'mini-movies', each with their own compressed three-act structure. The first two sequences combine to form the film's first act. The next four create the film's second act. The final two sequences complete the resolution and dénouement of the story. Each sequence's resolution creates the situation which sets up the next sequence."
  - **核心机制**：①每个序列 = 迷你电影，自带迷你三幕（目标→障碍→转折→解决）；②**序列的解决 = 下一个序列的启动情境**（链条式因果）；③2+4+2 对应三幕的 30/60/30 页分配。
- **Field 范式：页数与节拍（S21）**——序列内的"目标/障碍/转折"对应具体节拍：
  > 情节点（plot point）定义："any incident, episode, or event that hooks into the action and spins it around in another direction".
  > 120 页剧本：Act1≈30 页（plot point I 结尾）、Act2≈60 页（midpoint 在第 60 页附近，2a/2b 各 30 页）、Act3≈30 页（plot point II 结尾）。
  > pinch I（约第 45 页）/ pinch II（约第 75 页）："reminder scene... that brings up the central conflict of the drama, reminding the audience of the overall conflict"——即序列内的"障碍重申节拍"。
  > midpoint："an important scene in the middle of the script, often a reversal of fortune or revelation that changes the direction of the story. Field suggests that driving the story toward the midpoint keeps the second act from sagging."
- **四幕结构实证（S21，Bordwell/Thompson）**——现代主流电影常呈四幕，且每幕时长近等（以《碟中谍3》实测 31/30.5/32/22 分钟）：
  > "The Set-up (which establishes a primary story situation...); Complicating Action (a 'counter Set-up' that takes the action in a new direction); the Development (story premises and goals have been firmly introduced and the protagonist struggles toward their goals, often with incidents and set-backs that create delays, action, suspense); Climax (the final progression toward a resolution)."
- **故事脊柱（S21，Michael Welles Schock）**——序列串起来的四件套：Story Problem（=激励事件）、Story Goal、Path of Action、Stakes（代价），其中 Goal 的定义：
  > "The Story Goal: the very thing that drives the main character. The MC believes that once the goal is achieved, the Story Problem will be solved and life can return to normal."
- **序列内的戏剧性问题（S20）**：三幕各段都服务于一个总戏剧性问题（Will X...?），高潮作答——序列级可设子问题，幕级设总问题。
- **行动片的序列实证（S11，Bordwell《Anatomy of an Action Picture》**：《碟中谍3》四段 = 目标建立→失败（Lindsey 死）→换目标（抓 Davian 成功）→障碍升级（妻子被绑+限期）→高潮（解救+终结反派）——每一段的"解决"都制造下一段的"问题"。

## 4.2 副线（subplot / B-story）与主线交织

- **副线定义（S19）**——副线是主线的支持性支线，可与主线在**时间/地点/主题意义**三个层面连接，并可**在故事某点与主线交织**：
  > "In fiction, a subplot or side story is a strand of the plot that is a supporting side story for any story or for the main plot. Subplots may connect to main plots, in either time and place or thematic significance. Subplots often involve supporting characters... Subplots may also intertwine with the main plot at some point in a story."
- **副线与主线的区分度（S19）**——四条硬标准：
  > "Subplots are distinguished from the main plot by taking up less of the action, having fewer significant events occur, with less impact on the 'world' of the work, and occurring to less important characters."
- **B-story 术语（S19 + S18）**：编剧行话中副线 = "B story"，主线 = "A story"：
  > "In screenwriting, a subplot is referred to as a 'B story' while the main plot point can be referred to as the 'A story'."（S19；S18 的 B_story 条目 301 重定向至同一页面，佐证二者同义）
- **交织范本（S19，《Mr. Robot》第一季）**——副线行为反向改写了主线的障碍：Angela 的副线行动（被 Dark Army 胁迫装间谍软件）导致 Dark Army 退出合作 → 主线黑客行动被迫改策略、出现新障碍：
  > "The result of her action in this subplot ended up affecting the main plot... introducing a new obstacle in the main plot that had to be overcome. Throughout the story, her subplot repeatedly affected this main plot."
  - **操作含义**：副线不是"插花"，每个副线节拍都应具备对主线产生因果涟漪的潜力。
- **副线 = 主线的 foil（S4）**：
  > "In some cases, a subplot can be used as a foil to the main plot."
- **主题镜像（S4 同构案例）**：副线与主线"同构殊途"（同样的处境、不同的选择）→ 主题通过对照显影，如《哈利·波特》Harry/Draco 双线（见 1.3）。
- **副线在结构中的收束（S20）**——第三幕必须同时解决主线与全部副线：
  > "The third act features the resolution of the story and its subplots."
- **角色弧光可借副线承载（S2）**：次要角色同样可以有弧光（"lesser characters often change as well"）——副线是配角弧光的容器。
- **剧集化的副线钩子（S2）**：在剧集中，角色弧光/副线是留住观众的结构钩子：
  > "In episodic TV series, the character arc functions as a narrative hook that writers often use to ensure viewers continue watching."

## 4.3 序列 + 副线 · 可操作清单
1. 用八序列法拆 120 页剧本：8×15 页 = 8 个迷你三幕；每序列结尾写"解决 → 下一序列开场情境"的因果链（S21）。
2. 每序列内部标注四节拍：本序列目标 → 首次障碍（pinch 类提醒）→ 转折（midpoint 反转或 plot point）→ 序列解决（S21）。
3. 副线立项三问：连接层是时间/地点还是主题？在哪一页与主线第一次交织？副线结局是否影响主线结局？（S19）
4. 副线与主线做"主题镜像"检查：副线主角面临与主线主角同构的两难、做出不同选择 → 主题自动显影（S4）。
5. 第三幕收束清单：主线戏剧性问题 + 每条副线 + 每个配角弧光，三者在 climax 前后全部闭合（S20/S2）。
6. 剧集/长片系列：用角色弧光与副线做跨集钩子，但每集必须有该集自身的迷你解决（S2/S21）。
