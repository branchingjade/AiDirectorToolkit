# 制作大师卡片 · Dennis Muren（丹尼斯·穆伦，1946–，视效监督/特效摄影指导）

> 取证范围：一手访谈原文（ScreenCrush 2017、Den of Geek 2017、ILM.com 官方 2026、Looper 2022、3Dgate/3dvfx.net 2000 文字版）+ 一手同事口述（vfxblog 2017《T2 技术口述史》）+ 二手汇编（英文维基主条目、Future of the Force 2016 BFI 现场报道、/Film 2022 引 Kretchmer 口述）。视频类来源（《Light & Magic》纪录片、Cinefex #65 访谈、Vulture 口述史）未取得原文/被拦，见「诚实声明」。
> 编号：S1–S10 见文末「取证来源清单」。**原文摘录均经 grep 对本轮 pages/ 存档逐条验证。**
> 一句话地位：9 座奥斯卡（8 座最佳视效 + 1 座技术成就奖），1999 年好莱坞星光大道第一位视效艺术家 [S1]。

## 1. 专业签名（一句话）

**以「实体世界观察者」身份驱动数字革命——用真实世界的光、质感、运动做标尺，把每一代新工具（微缩模型、运动控制、CG、数字合成）都「约束到胶片记录的世界」里，做出观众认不出是特效的照片级影像；「你关心的是镜头看到什么」，技术永远让位于画面的情感力量。**
依据：穆伦自述软件可无限、胶片有限——"part of the process is constraining it down to what film records... When it is constrained to the world of photography, then I can start to understand it again"（[S4]）；"What you care about is what the camera sees"（[S4]）；"I've never been concerned about technology. I'm interested in the imagery; the power of the imagery and how emotional the image is."（[S2]）；Spielberg 评语——"set the example at Industrial Light & Magic for visual effects excellence with effects that add strong, appropriate emotion to a shot and fit seamlessly into a movie"（[S1] 转引自 Iwerks 纪录片）。

## 2. 美学体系（手法 | 成片实例 | 取证来源）

| 维度 | 手法 | 成片实例 | 取证来源 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 真实感哲学 | **照片级是唯一标准，效果必须"无缝融入"**：数字合成消除接缝（无 matte line），"对象看起来真的在场景里，没有任何人工痕迹" | 少年福尔摩斯彩窗骑士（电影史上第一个写实 CG 角色）；T2 液态金属 | [S4][S2][S1] |
| 实体优先 | **一切可触摸之物实体制作，CG 只做不可能之物**：实体做主体、数字补位/补远景 | T2 的 Stan Winston 全尺寸机械臂与机械头（"when it's wiggling around – all that was full-size stuff"）；侏罗纪公园 Stan Winston 实尺恐龙；少年福尔摩斯真雪（150 吨树枝盐 + 100 吨硫酸镁 + 高膨胀泡沫）与 25 英尺翼展全尺寸飞行器 | [S3][S4] |
| 分层合成 | **每个元素分开拍摄再合成**：实体木偶/模型逐个拍、最后叠成一个画面 | 少年福尔摩斯 12 个点心木偶各拍一次合进同镜；星战死星深渊分段拍摄再叠合 | [S4][S6] |
| 数字角色递进 | **先解决"反光体"再攻"皮肤"**：反射材质简单、皮肤最难 | T2 铬面 T-1000（反射体）→ 侏罗纪公园恐龙（皮肤） | [S3][S2] |
| 细节锚定 | **纯反射=像特效，脏与瑕疵才是锚**：100% 反射不真实，要散射、要脏 | T2 主动把铬面"弄脏"（"pewter" 灰锡质感）；直升机镜头加飞行员倒影；钢厂火花反射 | [S3][S5] |
| 运动模糊 | **24 帧运动模糊=写实必备**：不做模糊就"像 ILM 贴上去的"；一帧渲染 9 次合成模糊 | 少年福尔摩斯彩窗骑士全部镜头 | [S4] |
| 手工作业 | **关键镜头手 K 帧动画**：不为通用软件写代码，"电影一个镜头只需成立一次" | T2 熔钢池末镜逐帧手调；溶解 fractal 效果逐帧挑随机种子 | [S3][S5] |
| 试错与备份 | **新方法先试，不行就退回旧法**（bake-off 竞赛制）；任何冒险都有实体 fallback | 少年福尔摩斯"彩窗骑士先试 CG 两个月，不行就退回木偶/Go-Motion"；T2 全程 Winston 实体机械后备 | [S4][S3] |
| 观察者训练 | **技术不是重点，观察现实世界/理解物理才是**：没上过电影学院、自学成才 | 6-7 岁拍模型飞船恐龙、12 岁拿到摄影机；"I can always reference it to what we did before computers" | [S9][S1] |
| 廉价预演 | **用最廉价工具先"看见"镜头**：动手拍，不空想 | 星球大战 3 天用芭比/肯娃娃+纸筒在长毛地毯上预演 100+ 镜头；T2 让 Robert Patrick 全身画网格拍参考 | [S1][S3] |

## 3. 创作思路（工作方法/理念，附原话与出处）

- **源头：6 岁起的"图像囤积症"**。"I've always been interested in visual effects, things like spectacles and creatures... I've been interested in this since I was six or seven. I started with a still camera and got a movie camera when I was about 12."（[S9]）没有录像带的年代，他在影院里对着银幕拍静照留存影像——"I had this incredible urge to keep these images, which is why I guess I developed ability to hold images in my head"（[S9]）。研究 John Fulton、Ray Harryhausen、Lydecker 兄弟，自学成才、未上电影学院（[S1]）。
- **《Equinox》(1970)：自证式起点**。1965 年暑假筹 6500 美元拍 71 分钟超自然片《Equinox》，自己执导大部分并做全部特效，后由 Jack Harris 买下补拍发行——"despite its mixed to weak reviews, the movie made enough money for Muren to recoup his investment"（[S1]）。
- **加入 ILM 的偶然与必然**。1976 年以 2nd cameraman 身份进 ILM 拍《星球大战》；随后帮 Douglas Trumbull 拍《第三类接触》母舰；为 John Dykstra 拍《太空堡垒卡拉狄加》数周；再搬到 Marin County 参与重建 ILM，任《帝国反击战》特效摄影指导（[S1]）。他自述带人逻辑——"I brought Ken Ralston into it and then eventually brought Phil and John Berg into it. I brought my little group of people that I trusted."（[S8]）。星战首映回忆：他带病（walking pneumonia）参加剧组放映，"The audience went nuts from that very opening shot all the way through it. I thought, 'I've never seen anything like this before.'"（[S8]）。
- **ILM 文化=敢说"我们能"**。"People look to ILM for the future because we've been the future for so long... it's from George walking in and saying, 'I want to pan the camera around these spaceships,' and everybody's saying, 'No, you can't do it,' and John Dykstra said, 'We can do it.'"（[S8]）
- **技术恐惧与一年休假**。1990 年《深渊》后休假一年专学 CG 软硬件理论，自述这是 T2 成功的关键——"It gave me the confidence to go back and risk doing Terminator 2 and Jurassic Park because it was no longer a mystery to me. It certainly was before--it was terrifying because I just didn't understand it. You don't actually have to know how to do all these effects on the computer, which I still don't know how to do, but you need to know what you can do with it."（[S9]）维基同述："Muren took a one-year sabbatical to study CGI software and hardware theory, to which he credits much of the success of the Terminator 2 digital effects"（[S1]）。
- **艺术大于技术（最常引用的自白）**。"I've never been concerned about technology. I'm interested in the imagery; the power of the imagery and how emotional the image is. And that's why you're doing it."（[S2]）少年福尔摩斯同义表述："Everything in movies is feelings. And if I didn't feel it, and the audience didn't feel it, then you're just telling a story, and you might as well be doing it by telephone."（[S4]）
- **约束到摄影世界**。软件默认无限，胶片记录有限——"A lot of what's made for software manipulation, whether it's brightness, camera movements, or distance, go to infinity... part of the process is constraining it down to what film records... When it is constrained to the world of photography, then I can start to understand it again."（[S4]）
- **"作弊"是电影方法**。彩窗骑士看穿玻璃的透射被做成黑白提亮（"it's a cheat, just black and white to light it up"），并坦承"we did a lot of that later in The Abyss with the water snake, all the refractions in the rooms, we cheated all the way through"（[S4]）；剑刃高光用反推虚拟光源——"No one's going to know that you cheated that light and it didn't take a lot of time... What you care about is what the camera sees."（[S4]）
- **细节=ILM 传统**。"All that stuff is the same thing – it ties it together, keeps everything looking real and more frightening. It's the detail, and we've been doing that detail stuff since the original Star Wars. The attention to detail is one of the things at ILM."（[S3]）
- **故意让作品过时**。"Doing a show is great, but as soon as I finish it I sort of intentionally try to make it obsolete so I can go on to another visual stimulation that's really neat. And I never used to be able to do that because I was limited by plastics, rubber, metal."（[S9]）
- **反抄袭：为什么 T2 不过时**。"All of us... were trying to do something that had never been done before. We were not copying anything... a lot of work that's being done now is imitation... they're copying the stuff that we did a long time ago. And we weren't copying anything."（[S2]）另一版本："it's like a xerox of a xerox of a xerox. It's not real anymore."（[S3]）判断标准："know why they're doing it... Not technically – I'm talking about artistically. It's all about art."（[S2]）
- **对 AI/按钮式生成的警惕**。"You can't push a button. The human being is so complicated that it's going to be really hard to get all that emotion and life into a CG character... if it is button pushing, it'll look very boring."（[S3]）并预言若纯靠程序"你得到的就是以前看过的东西的拷贝"（[S3]）。
- **对数字艺术家的忠告（2000 年已预见）**。"You should never get away from a sense of design. For my work... it's understanding composition, lighting, and art. This will make your work something beyond just a representation."（[S9]）虚拟世界"should have light sources, and light should reflect off objects correctly"（[S9]）。
- **领导风格（同事证词）**。George Joblove："Everything was done step by step with a lot of tests along the way guided by Dennis Muren who had great faith in what we could do. He was also excited about the prospects of being able to do things that hadn't been done before."（[S5]）Alex Seiden："Some of the best feedback was, no surprise, from Dennis Muren. In particular, he was really great at guiding the overall look, such as making sure we had enough diffuse shading mixed with our reflections. We called it the 'pewter' look. Without that, the T-1000 didn't have any mass."（[S5]）Jim Morris（ILM 前总裁）："could always find a way to look at a problem from a different angle and come up with a shot or scene that would be wondrous to watch"（[S1]）。
- **正在写一本关于"观察"的书**（2025 年状态）——"Muren is working on a book focusing on 'observation' for digital artists"（[S10]，播客页 bio）。

## 4. 招牌作品拆解

### 拆解一：《星球大战》—《帝国反击战》—《E.T.》—《夺宝奇兵》（1977–1984）——ILM 黄金时代的全能手
- **履历骨架**：1976 年以 2nd cameraman 加入 ILM 拍《星球大战》（[S1]）；《帝国反击战》任特效摄影指导，1980 年凭此获奥斯卡（[S1]）；此后 7 年内再赢 5 座奥斯卡（[S1]）；《E.T.》(1982)、《绝地归来》(1983)、《魔域奇兵》(1984)、《InnerSpace》(1987) 等接连获奖（[S1]）。
- **工作法三件套（皆为"廉价预演/改造工具"思维）**：①1980–83 把动画摄影台改造成 4 轴光学印片机做《帝国》《绝地》的"pin blocking"镜头（[S1]）；②1983 年 3 天用芭比/肯娃娃和纸筒在长毛地毯上手持第一台微型摄像机预演《绝地归来》飞车追逐 100+ 镜头（[S1]）；③1984 年用 Nikon F3 相机当 go-motion 摄影机拍《魔域奇兵》矿车追逐，省建造与拍摄时间（[S1]）。
- **《帝国反击战》冰原 Tauntaun 难题**：现场讲述"15 分钟解决"——本打算放弃、再给自己 15 分钟，结果当场解决问题并纳入成片（[S6]，现场活动转述）。
- **《E.T.》**：1982 年凭 E.T. 获最佳视效奥斯卡（[S1]）；数字革命的种子同期埋下——1979 年卢卡斯成立计算机图形组，穆伦推动把技术用于写实电影影像（[S1]），1985 年《少年福尔摩斯》结出第一个果实（[S4]）。

### 拆解二：《少年福尔摩斯》(1985)——第一个写实 CG 角色：彩窗骑士
- **历史地位**：电影史上第一个"完全数字化的角色"——"The film holds a unique place in Industrial Light & Magic's history. It includes an abundance of practical visual effects methods... as well as the first fully digital character ever depicted in a feature film, a stained glass knight."（[S4]）；同时诞生首批数字合成与第一张 CG 背景（[S4][S1]）。获奥斯卡视效提名（[S1]）。
- **动机与试错制**：穆伦"just needed to see if this technology had the controls necessary to make something look 100% real or not"（[S4]）；决定做"bake-off"——"With the stained glass man, that looks small enough, so let's try CG, right? If we find out in two months it's not working, we can back off and do it another way."（[S4]）——**新方法两个月试错期、随时退回旧法**，这是穆伦式风险控制模板。
- **彩窗骑士设计**：妻子 Zara 提议骑士以碎玻璃片各自飞出、落地重组（[S4]）；每片玻璃 1 英寸厚、锯齿边缘、凸面朝人（"Everything in movies is feelings"）（[S4]）。
- **运动模糊=写实关键**："I didn't want the stained glass knight to look like it came from ILM, that it was stuck onto the background."（[S4]）当时没有数字模糊能力，把每帧渲染 9 次、位置微差，叠成运动模糊（[S4]）——**为"贴上去"的违和感付出的手工代价**。
- **实体与数字同片混用**：真雪（150 吨树枝盐铺地、100 吨硫酸镁撒建筑、蒸发泡沫铺大面积、农业谷物吹机喷碎纸当落雪）（[S4]）；25 英尺翼展全尺寸飞行器+两台 120 英尺塔吊真飞（[S4]）；12 个橡胶点心木偶各拍 2-3 天再合成同镜（"Each element was shot in front of a blue screen, so when we combined them, twelve pastries would be in the same shot"）（[S4]）。
- **技术约束**：激光扫描仪约 1 帧/分钟，"we just did everything at either 1 or 2K"（[S4]）。

### 拆解三：《终结者2》(1991)——液态金属 T-1000：第一个"完美数字角色"
- **规模**：ILM 完成约 50 个 CG 镜头（穆伦自述"I think it was 52 shots or something like that"）（[S3]），当时前所未有（[S3] 编者按）。
- **为什么敢做**：反射体比皮肤简单——"a lot of work had been done with reflective figures in computer graphics – that's a much simpler problem than having to put skin on something... But the flexibility, making a walking character that looks like it's made out of mercury or chrome, and making it look like it has weight and actually in the shot is what was really extremely difficult."（[S3]）
- **"弄脏"铬面（穆伦最精彩的一课）**：Cameron 要 100% 完美反射，穆伦反对——"Jim said he wanted him to look absolutely perfect – clean and everything... And I just thought, no, you don't want that, because it's not going to look real. You never see that in real life – it's going to look like an effect. So I went in and muddied it up a little bit, so that the surface isn't 100 percent reflective. The light's scattering, it's a little dirty... That's what anchors it into the shot."（[S3]）同事称之为 "pewter"（灰锡）质感，没有它"T-1000 没有质量"（[S5]）。
- **走姿=人形角色的灵魂**：让 Robert Patrick 全身画网格拍参考，动画师 Steve Williams 研究其步态——"It was then that we started to realise how incredibly difficult it is to do a person. I don't think any of us quite knew that before – how one person is different from another... But without that gait, it didn't look like Robert Patrick"（[S3]）。
- **最难的镜头**：①精神病院穿铁栅栏——"I'm looking at it and I've been watching movies my whole life and it's impossible, and I'm seeing it"（[S2]），技术难点是几何撕裂（帧间大位移导致表面破洞）（[S2]）；②熔钢池末镜——冲 2000 线分辨率渲染、大部分以低于 1000 线赶工、"Some of the frames went down to Silicon Graphics, some of them went to universities across the country"（[S3]）；溶解部分用 fractal 随机算法，穆伦逐帧挑种子指挥（"I want it to move left and I want that one to dissolve faster"）（[S5]）。
- **实体+CG 分工**：Stan Winston 团队做全尺寸机械臂、机械头——"Not when the T-1000 is coming apart, but when it's wiggling around – all that was full-size stuff"（[S3]）。
- **没有备份的豪赌**："it's not like there was a backup if the CG failed... If we hadn't made the CG work, or Stan hadn't made his stuff work, then Jim wouldn't have had a movie"（[S3]）——与《深渊》可剪掉 pseudopod 不同（[S3]）。
- **自评**：职业生涯最自豪的作品——"I worked hard on bringing the digital stuff into ILM and getting it in place. It had been in the R&D group for a decade and never quite made it into the mainstream."（[S9]）；"That's my favorite part of my career, doing those two films [T2 & JP]... we would look at the dailies and say to ourselves, 'Did we do this? This isn't even possible!'... up until that time we were so reliant on plastic and wood and metal and plaster and rubber and all the old tools that were limiting. This just freed us up."（[S2]）

### 拆解四：《侏罗纪公园》(1993)——CG 恐龙里程碑
- **从 go-motion 到 CG 的转折**：Spielberg 原计划用 Phil Tippett 的 go-motion 恐龙，穆伦团队用 Steve Williams 与 Mark Dippé 做的"行走恐龙骨架"CG 测试（背景为 Marin County）说服 Spielberg/环球出资做"照片级、无借口"的验证镜头（[S1]，维基转引 Iwerks 纪录片与 StarWars.com 口述史）。Tippett 看罢自嘲——"I think I'm Extinct"，Spielberg 把这句话写进了成片台词（[S6]，BFI 现场转述）。
- **三个月攻皮肤**："In three months, following Muren's cinematic goals, the ILM CG department broke new ground, adding organically moving flesh and muscle to the creature's skeleton, covering it with animal-like skin texture and exterior sun and bounce lighting to make a photorealistic walking T-Rex."（[S1]，维基转引）穆伦自述与 T2 的递进关系——"And Jurassic is kind of the next step; we added skin to the effects, which was a major problem; to make them not look like plastic or artificial dinosaurs. And also the performances were very difficult in Jurassic Park."（[S2]）Spielberg 当时预言："It's going to be amazing. People are really going to believe that dinosaurs are walking this earth today."（[S1] 转引）
- **实体+CG 混合**：Stan Winston 的实尺机械恐龙与 CG 恐龙同片分镜（[S1] 记载 Winston 共享奥斯卡；[S3] 佐证 Winston 实体团队传统）；穆伦明确把两片并列——"I always credit T2 as being equal at least to Jurassic Park"（[S2]）。
- **结尾 T.rex 回归=临时加戏**：拍摄中 Spielberg 现场决定——"I think we have to bring the T. rex back, the audience is going to want to see the T. rex."（[S7] 引 Kretchmer）穆伦团队用 CG 完成大部分，"no storyboards"、"no plans for this whatsoever"（[S7] 引 Kretchmer）。
- **产业后果**：JP 让卢卡斯确信技术成熟、可拍星战前传；也让 Peter Jackson 下定决心规划《指环王》与《金刚》（[S1]）。

## 5. 可复用时机（国风志怪/武侠创作——CG+实体混合）

⚠️ 本节为分析框架：把已取证的穆伦方法映射到目标类型，非穆伦本人言论。

| 场景 | 用哪招 | 证据出处 |
|---|---|---|
| **妖/神兽的实体化（核心）** | **实体优先铁律**：凡"要摸、要站、要依赖"的（妖的手、衣袂、法器、坐骑、爪牙）一律实体制作（对应 T2 全尺寸机械臂、JP 实尺恐龙、少年福尔摩斯真雪真飞行器）；CG 只做不可能形体——化形中间态、巨兽全景、消散（对应 T2 液态身、JP 全景恐龙）。实体与数字的分工线=可触摸性 | [S3][S4][S1] |
| **妖的"皮肤"质感（防塑料感）** | 数字角色最难的是皮肤：JP 配方——"organically moving flesh and muscle + animal-like skin texture + exterior sun and bounce lighting"；志怪妖物/神兽的皮肤按动物标准做（毛孔/受光/肌肉滑动），否则"plastic or artificial" | [S1][S2] |
| **变形/化形戏（液态金属法）** | 先从"反光体"做起再攻皮肤；变形材质绝不 100% 完美——"muddied it up"：弄脏、散射、瑕疵才是真实锚点；妖的化形（雾化/水化/金光化）同理，材质要有"脏旧"细节而非纯净特效 | [S3][S5] |
| **透明体（魂/鬼/灵体）** | **彩窗骑士看穿玻璃的"作弊"法**：透过魂体看到的东西做提亮/黑白化处理，不做全彩折射（"cheat, just black and white to light it up"）；《深渊》水蛇所有折射都是"作弊"——简化物理保戏剧性。与视效密码"透明体只做光照属性"同构，且给出更狠的操作：连折射都可不做对 | [S4] |
| **百鬼夜行/妖群/群演** | **分层拍摄合成法**：每个元素单独拍再合成（12 点心木偶法）——实体木偶/演员各拍一遍叠进同镜，避免全 CG 群演；星战死星深渊分段拍再叠（[S6]）同法 | [S4][S6] |
| **妖的"人形"破绽（步态）** | **走姿研究法**：Robert Patrick 步态=角色成立的关键——志怪里"妖在学人"的异质感首先来自走路/动作的不对；先拍真人参考再偏离（对应知识库"学人的别扭处"招式，这里给出技术实现路径：参考拍摄→逐帧研究→CG 修正） | [S3] |
| **快动作妖物/打斗（防"贴上去"）** | **运动模糊纪律**：24 帧运动模糊是写实必要条件，无模糊=特效穿帮（"stuck onto the background"）；AI 生成快动作时显式要求运动模糊、禁止抽帧锐化 | [S4] |
| **倒影/水面戏（反射锚定）** | **反射卖镜头法**：T2 直升机飞行员倒影、钢厂火花倒影——"it ties it together, keeps everything looking real and more frightening"；志怪的水镜/铜镜/妖物反光戏主动设计环境反射内容 | [S3] |
| **新效果上马（风险控制）** | **bake-off 两个月试错制**：新技术先试 N 周、失败即退回实体方案（少年福尔摩斯 CG↔木偶；T2 CG↔Winston 机械）——AI 分镜同理：高风险镜头先小样验证再定方案 | [S4][S3] |
| **重场戏预演** | **芭比娃娃 previz 法**：志怪重场（化形/斗法/百鬼）先用玩偶/纸片/白模拍调度预演（穆伦 3 天预演 100+ 镜头先例）；T2 的网格参考拍摄法=真人动作先录再 CG | [S1][S3] |
| **预算纪律（手工 vs 通用化）** | **一镜一次原则**："a movie only has to work one time, for one shot"——不为罕见镜头写通用工具，手 K 帧；AI 项目对应：关键镜头多关键帧锁定、不指望一次性抽卡成功 | [S3][S4] |
| **"镜头看到什么"决策** | **作弊优先原则**：只优化镜头内可见之物——反推虚拟光源加高光、黑白透射提亮；志怪片特效预算分配按此排序，不为理论物理正确性烧钱 | [S4] |

## 6. AI 提示词对接（美学语言 → 视频生成提示词）

按取证要点编写，非原片描述。

**妖的实体+CG 混合（实体道具+数字延伸）**
- EN: "a creature half-practical and half-digital: the hands, the robe, the staff are real physical props with real fabric and wood grain; only the impossible parts are digital — the form dissolving at the edges, the body growing to giant size in the distance; practical elements stay crisp and tangible, digital parts stay in the far ground or the impossible motion"
- 中注：实体做可触摸之物（手/衣/法器），数字只做不可能之物（化形/巨量/远景）——穆伦实体优先铁律（依据 [S3][S4]）

**妖的皮肤质感（防塑料感）**
- EN: "the beast's skin is animal-like: organically moving flesh and muscle beneath, pores and texture visible, lit by exterior sun with soft bounce light — absolutely not plastic, not artificial, not a smooth render"
- 中注：JP 恐龙皮肤配方原文改写——organically moving flesh and muscle / animal-like skin texture / sun and bounce lighting（依据 [S1]）

**化形/液态变形（弄脏的反射）**
- EN: "the demon dissolves into liquid metal-like form, but the surface is never 100 percent reflective: slightly dirty, light scattering, subtle imperfections anchoring it to the scene; reflections of the environment move across it, including the hero's face reflected in the metal"
- 中注：pewter 灰锡原则——绝不纯净反射、必须脏与散射锚定；环境反射（人面/火光）主动设计（依据 [S3][S5]）

**透明魂体（看穿作弊法）**
- EN: "a translucent spirit: what you see through her body is brightened and desaturated, almost black and white, not full color refraction; she is solid and cannot be passed through, her sleeve stays real wet fabric"
- 中注：透射提亮/去饱和作弊，不做全彩折射（彩窗骑士/深渊水蛇法）；与视效密码透明体语法同构（依据 [S4]）

**快动作妖物（运动模糊）**
- EN: "the spirit moves at unnatural speed, (24fps motion blur on every fast movement, no freeze-frame sharpness, nothing looks stuck onto the background)"
- 中注：运动模糊是写实必要条件——每帧渲染 9 次的现代等价物；括号防穿帮（依据 [S4]）

**百鬼夜行（分层合成）**
- EN: "a crowd of spirits: the three spirits in the foreground are practical puppets with visible fabric and breath, the dozens behind them are digital and half-transparent — each element shot separately and composited together, layers never blend into mush"
- 中注：前景实体+背景数字分层，元素独立拍摄合成（12 点心木偶法）（依据 [S4]）

**妖的步态（人形破绽）**
- EN: "the demon walks like a person but slightly wrong: studied from a real human gait, then subtly off — a half-beat delay in the hips, the feet too precise; the more human she looks, the more unsettling the walk"
- 中注：先真人参考再偏离——步态是"人形妖"异质感的来源（依据 [S3]）
