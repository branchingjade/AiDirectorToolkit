# 导演美学卡片 · 贝拉·塔尔（Tarr Béla, 1955–2026）

> 取证范围：成片表现 + 一手访谈（SoC 2001 访谈、Film Comment 2012、BFI 2024 撒旦探戈 30 周年访谈、Cineuropa 2011 经英维转引）+ 学者/影评（SoC Great Directors 专条、Criterion essay、Kovács/Rancière 专著）+ 维基条目（英维主条目/撒旦探戈/都灵之马/鲸鱼马戏团、中维主条目）。无官方剧本出版物；《撒旦探戈》有 Krasznahorkai 原著小说可作结构证据（见 S2）。
> 编号：S1–S12 见文末「取证来源清单」。手法体系深化文档共用本编号表。
> 2026-08-09 取证。⚠️ 本代理未逐帧看片，成片观感类描述均引自评论家/学者原文并保留署名。

## 1. 风格签名（一句话）

**固定黑白 + 深焦摄影下漫游的摄影机，用 145 分钟 39 镜、155 分钟 30 镜、7 小时 150 镜的超长镜头，把"日常重复"拍成"宇宙级绝望"——景观、音乐、时间都是主角，故事只是其中一小部分。**

依据：Criterion essay 概括其美学宇宙——"From Damnation (1988) onward, all his films are set in weather-beaten wastelands and shot in deep-focus, chiaroscuro black and white, with a ceaselessly roving camera"（[S12]）；塔尔自述 "the location must be the other main character as must Time"（[S9]）、"I need the music before shooting, because music is also one of the main characters"（[S10]）；SoC 专条对其成熟期风格的总结——"stark black and white imagery, prolonged single takes, depressed and austere settings, and a cast of forlorn, cynical, and destitute characters"（[S8]）。

## 2. 美学体系（手法 | 成片实例 | 取证来源）

| 维度 | 手法 | 成片实例 | 取证来源 |

| 构图/画面 | 人物被空旷环境包围、孤绝于深焦景深之中（Rancière："long sequence shot that delve into the empty depth of field surrounding individuals enclosed in their solitude"） | 《撒旦探戈》集体农庄村民；《都灵之马》父女轮流独坐窗边 | [S8][S3] |
| 运镜/调度 | 持续漫游的摄影机（ceaselessly roving camera），轨道长镜为主、必要时斯坦尼康；摄影机绕行人物与空间（"circumnavigates in sinuous sequence shots"） | 《鲸鱼马戏团》人行道 150 米轨道跟拍（塔尔："It was on tracks.150 metres long."）；医院暴乱镜头从高空俯拍降入人群再升起 | [S12][S9][S12] |
| 运镜/调度 | 镜头运动与演员调度精密编排，剪辑在机内完成（"the editing, to some extent, takes place in the camera"） | 《鲸鱼马戏团》开场酒馆日食 10 分钟单镜（三人扮日月地、摄影机同步旋转） | [S12] |

## 3. 创作思路（工作方法/理念，附原话与出处）

- **音乐、地点、时间皆为主角**。Film Comment 访谈原话——"The landscape is one of the main characters. The landscape has a face. We have to find the right location, like we have to find the right music. That's why I need the music before shooting, because music is also one of the main characters."（[S10]）；SoC 访谈——"the location must be the other main character as must Time"（[S9]）。
- **先地点、先音乐、后剧本**。SoC 2001 访谈——"We take a lot of impressions... we didn't start the script before looking for locations. I need some impression from the locations. And afterwards I need some impression from the music which is composed before we start work. And when every element is ready, then we will write the final script."（[S9]）；"The script was very quick. I think it took two weeks or something like that. You know, we never use the script."；"Storyboards are stupid, stupid things."（[S9]）
- **实拍即兴修正**：演员真身一动，当场推翻理论预设——"we have planned but when the actor moves in reality, we see immediately that it's wrong – Agnes watches everything on the monitor as I do – and we see immediately that we have to change everything."（[S9]）剪辑长度由 Hranitzky 把关——"Agnes says several times 'A bit faster' or 'A bit longer' and often she decides the length. You know the final cut took just half a day!"（[S9]）
- **演员要"是"不要"演"**：Film Comment 原话——"I'm looking for their personality, how they react… They have to be natural, they have to be dancers. If someone is acting in my movies, I become mad and I stop them and say, 'OK, this is nice, what you're doing, but not in this movie. I'm interested in what is happening inside of you.'"（[S10]）；选角=找真人（非职业演员/街头音乐人/孤儿院女孩），如 Lars Rudolph 本为街头小号手（[S10][S9]）。
- **故事只是电影的一小部分**：SoC 原话——"the story is only a little part of the whole movie... I absolutely hate the movies that I can watch at the theatres. They are like comics. They always tell the same stories... for us every story is always the same old story from the Old Testament."（[S9]）；Film Comment——"It's a very cheap story. It's not about the story. I wanted to show more than the story, because all stories are the same. But I really love the people, and I wanted to show you the people."（[S10]）
- **去时代标记、拍"永恒"**：BFI 2024 原话——"We never, ever touched anything which is talking about the current time, the daily events. No. My goal was to talk about a kind of eternity... That's why you never see any cars or anything in my movies, which is showing you the date. You never see any newspaper with the cover page and the date... because I think it's all eternal."（[S11]）
- **反符号、反寓言**：BFI 原话——"It's not spiritual. It's happening. I don't like the word 'symbol'... I say no symbols, no allegories. It doesn't fit the motion picture, because you cannot shoot anything which is not existing in reality. A table is a table."（[S11]）Criterion essay 呼应："Tarr is, to invoke his ardent champion Susan Sontag, squarely against interpretation, adamant that his movies eschew metaphor and allegory."（[S12]）
- **风格是逐步长出来的**：BFI 原话——"The style is coming up step-by-step. You're not born with this style... When you finish a movie you will... have some new questions. And for the new questions, you cannot use your former answer, your former style is not working. You have to go ahead. The next project provokes you to take one more step."（[S11]）；"The takes get longer and longer to go along with my thinking."（[S10]）
- **从社会问题到本体论问题**：Criterion essay——"he has acknowledged an overall movement toward the metaphysical, from what he calls 'social problems' to 'ontological problems' (or, as he memorably put it in a 2001 interview, 'a whole pile of shit . . . coming from the cosmos')"（[S12]）；SoC 访谈——"Everything is much bigger than us. I think the human is just a little part of the cosmos."（[S9]）；"I don't believe in God. This is my problem."（[S9]）
- **反"社会现实主义"标签**：Film Comment——"I don't like this term 'social realism.' If you create a movie, you create a fiction. It's something that looks real, but of course it's not real because it's created."（[S10]）
- **黑色幽默自我定位**：Film Comment——"All my movies are comedies! Except The Turin Horse."（[S10]）SoC 专条也记"even with his contention that most are comedies"（[S8]）。
- **对"长"的态度**：BFI——"The length of a movie all depends on what you want to say. Maybe you saw my short, let's say haiku, called Prologue (2004). It was only five minutes. I don't care what is acceptable. We are just doing what we feel. You feel the length, and you feel the rhythm, because this is the form of what you are doing."（[S11]）
- **影响谱系**：承认年轻时的 Jancsó（"we like his movies very much"但"no direct connection"）（[S9]）；不承认黑色电影影响——"If you go to a small Hungarian town, a miner's town, you don't need American film noir. You have the real thing."（[S10]）；却自认从希区柯克学到的功课——"You have to show it very clearly, what could happen and what is really happening physically. I don't like so many filmmakers, but this is the lesson you can get from Hitchcock. To shoot what is really happening."（[S11]）

## 4. 桥段设计（3 个招牌桥段）

### 桥段一：《撒旦探戈》——探戈结构 + 开场牛群长镜（7 小时的形式宣言）
- **设计逻辑**：全片 12 章，结构来自 Krasznahorkai 原著——塔尔原话："We kept the structure of the book. Like the tango, it's six steps forward, six steps back."（[S10]；BFI 同句"six steps forward, six steps back"[S11]）；章节间时间重叠、视角切换（SoC："switching character emphasis and spectator identification, sometimes for an hour or more at a time"，"Rashomon-like variances in perception"）[S8]。
- **节拍层**：开场一个 7 分钟单镜拍牛群在泥泞田里行走（BFI："it opens with a seven-minute shot showing cattle walking through a muddy field"）[S11]——先把"时间"本身摆到观众面前；结尾章《圆圈闭合》（"The Circle Closes"）[S2][S12]。
- **主题层**：末日后的集体农庄，村民被假先知 Irimiás 欺骗；SoC 评其"post-apocalyptic despondency"（[S8]）。
- **观众情绪**：Sontag 的话最能说明观众关系——"Devastating, enthralling for every minute of its seven hours. I'd be glad to see it every year for the rest of my life."（[S9]；英维引 Artforum 版："devastating, enthralling for every minute of its seven hours," adding she would be "glad to see it every year for the rest of [her] life." [S2]）

### 桥段二：《鲸鱼马戏团》——开场酒馆日食戏（10 分钟单镜的"表意"）
- **设计逻辑**：Criterion essay——"the ten-minute opening scene, a closing-time pantomime in the quintessential Tarr location of the village tavern, where János enlists the local sots to act out a solar eclipse. With a trio of barflies playing the roles of the sun, the earth, and the moon, János sets them spinning, the camera rotating in tandem, then brings them to a halt to demonstrate the rare heavenly alignment capable of plunging the planet into darkness."（[S12]）一场戏同时交代人物（János 的天真）、空间（酒馆=塔尔标志地点）、主题（日食=黑暗降临的寓言），且单镜完成。
- **节拍层**：塔尔式"重复句式"——János 台词 "At first, we don't notice the events we are witnessing"，既讲日食也预言全片（[S12]）。
- **主题层**：末日将至的小镇+充气鲸鱼；医院暴乱夜戏（无声群氓打砸医院）被 Lim 称为其最非凡的段落之一（[S12]）。
- **观众情绪**：日食戏"a representation of representation"——让观众提前学会读塔尔的镜头（摄影机绕行遮挡=制造日食式遮蔽）[S12]。

### 桥段三：《都灵之马》——六日递减结构（末日不是爆炸，是水、光、土豆逐日消失）
- **设计逻辑**：片头 epigraph 讲尼采 1889 年都灵抱马痛哭的轶事，片尾追问"马的下落无人知晓"（[S10] epigraph 全文；[S3]）；正文拍马车夫父女六天日常。塔尔谈结构目的——"We just wanted to see how difficult and terrible it is when every day you have to go to the well and bring the water, in summer, in winter... All the time. The daily repetition of the same routine makes it possible to show that something is wrong with their world. It's very simple and pure."（[S3]，Cineuropa 访谈经英维转引）
- **节拍层**：六日递减——第二日马拒绝进食饮水；第三日吉普赛人来喝井水被驱赶；第五日日光消失、灯笼熄灭、全黑；第六日无风无光、只吃生土豆、女儿拒绝进食、父亲放下土豆沉默（[S3]）。
- **主题层**：塔尔自述影片关于"the 'heaviness of human existence'"（[S3]）；邻居 Bernhard 声称邻近城镇已彻底毁灭、把灾难归咎于上帝与人（[S3]）；塔尔拍前宣言——"I want to make one more film about the end of the world, and then I will stop making films."（[S8]）
- **观众情绪**：重复制造"出问题了"的知觉（塔尔原话："The daily repetition of the same routine makes it possible to show that something is wrong with their world"）；A.O. Scott 影评标题即《Facing the Abyss With Boiled Potatoes and Plum Brandy》（面对深渊，配水煮土豆与李子白兰地）[S3]。

## 6. 可复用时机（国风长镜/时间/意境类创作）

| 场景 | 用哪招 | 证据出处 |
|---|---|---|
| 拍"时间流逝/日常重复" | 同机位/同动作跨天重复（打水、削土豆、坐窗边），让观众自己察觉"出问题了" | [S3] |
| 拍末日/荒芜/绝望 | 环境先于人物说话：泥、雾、雨、风做主角；叙事不去解释，只呈现 | [S12][S8] |
| 拍"沉重历史/时代终结" | 去时代标记的永恒感（画面里没有车、没有报纸日期）；大事件侧面抵达（邻居转述城镇毁灭） | [S11][S3] |
| 拍仪式/开场 | 一个 10 分钟单镜完成"人设+空间+主题"三合一（酒馆日食戏）；长镜开场定全片节奏 | [S12] |
| 拍人物孤独 | 深焦空旷环境中的人物孤点；人物轮流独自坐窗边 | [S8][S3] |
| 拍声音意境 | 脚步声、风声、雨声放大为叙事主体；音乐先于画面创作、与画面同构 | [S12][S10] |
| 拍循环/宿命 | 首尾闭环结构（"The Circle Closes"）；镜头绕行、音乐重复 | [S12] |
| 拍黑白/水墨/版画感 | 深焦黑白+明暗对比（chiaroscuro），用光影"灭灯"推进剧情 | [S12][S3] |

⚠️ 本节为分析框架：把已取证的塔尔手法映射到目标类型，非导演自述。

## 7. AI 提示词对接（美学语言 → 视频生成提示词）

按取证要点编写，非原片描述。构图/调度、天气光线、色彩、声音节奏、时间结构五组：

**构图/调度（深焦黑白·漫游长镜）**
- EN: "Single continuous shot, 2+ minutes, camera slowly tracking on rails through a muddy village street; deep-focus black-and-white photography; a lone figure in ragged clothes walks toward camera, passes, exits frame; camera keeps moving past them into the fog; no cuts, no close-ups, no dialogue; heavy chiaroscuro contrast"
- 中注：单镜长镜+轨道漫游，人物走过镜头后摄影机继续前行；深焦黑白；禁止剪辑/特写（依据 [S12][S9]）

**天气光线（环境主角·人工风）**
- EN: "Endless windstorm, rain lashing a small stone hut, bare trees bending; dust and dead leaves blowing across a yard; the wind is audible as a continuous howl; interior lit by a single oil lamp, then the lamp dies and the room falls into total darkness"
- 中注：风/雨/泥做画面主角；光以"熄灭"推进剧情（依据 [S3][S9]）

**色彩（黑白·灰阶层次）**
- EN: "Black and white film, deep blacks and pale greys, no mid-tone compromise; overcast sky merging with wet mud; faces emerging from darkness; scenes of a hut interior dominated by shadow"
- 中注：深焦黑白、无彩色；雾/烟/蒸汽参与构图（依据 [S12][S8]）

**声音节奏（放大环境声·音乐先行）**
- EN: "Sound design: amplified footsteps on wet ground, wind howling, distant bells, clinking glasses; minimal droning score repeating a single two-note motif; long silences; no music during dialogue scenes"
- 中注：脚步声/风声/远处钟声放大；单主题反复的 drone 配乐；静默时长本身是节奏（依据 [S12][S9]）

**时间结构（重复日常·循环）**
- EN: "The same mundane action repeated across days: carrying water from a well, peeling potatoes, sitting by the window; shots of the same room at different times; an ending that returns to the opening image"
- 中注：同动作跨天重复、同空间不同时辰、首尾闭环（依据 [S3][S12]）

## 8. ⚠️ 诚实声明（来源局限）

1. **未逐帧看片**：本代理未逐帧观看贝拉·塔尔任何影片；全部成片观感/镜头数描述引自评论家/学者原文（Dennis Lim、Jeremy Carr、András Bálint Kovács、Jacques Rancière、Susan Sontag 等）并保留署名，或引自维基条目剧情段。
2. **量化数据双口径**（按纪律并列，不强行统一）：
   - 《撒旦探戈》时长：英维"Running for more than seven hours"[S2]；Criterion"seven-and-a-half-hour opus"[S12]；BFI/通行说法"seven-hour"[S11]——7 小时为通行口径，精确时长 415 分钟（SoC [S8]）。
   - 《撒旦探戈》镜头数：塔尔自述"only roughly 150 shots in the entire film"（经英维转引 Bright Lights 访谈）[S2]——非逐帧统计。
   - 《麦克白》镜头结构：SoC 称全片 72 分钟仅 2 镜（片头前 5 分钟 + 67 分钟长镜，10 个 take 完成）[S8]；Film Comment 塔尔自称"I did the whole movie in one take"（视频拍摄）[S10]；中维称"兩個長鏡頭……第二個長鏡頭長達57分鐘"[S6]——一镜/两镜、57/67 分钟并存，未统一。
   - 《家庭巢穴》拍摄天数：Film Comment/SoC 5 天[S10][S8] vs 中维 6 天[S6]。
   - 拍摄季节：SoC 专条"starting in 1987, all of his films were shot from November through March"[S8] vs Film Comment 塔尔自述撒旦探戈"could not shoot in the summer, because of the leaves on the trees, and we could not shoot in the winter, because of the snow. We could only shoot early spring or late autumn."[S10]——两口径并存。
3. **一手/二手区分**：一手=SoC 2001 访谈[S9]、Film Comment 2012[S10]、BFI 2024[S11]（均为访谈原文）；二手=Criterion essay（Dennis Lim）[S12]、SoC 专条（Jeremy Carr）[S8]、英维条目（含转引的塔尔引语：Cineuropa 访谈[S3]、Bright Lights 访谈[S2]、Screen Daily[S8]）——**凡经维基转引的导演原话均标注"经X转引"**。中维主条目[S6]为二手。
4. **未取证到**：Criterion《都灵之马》essay（films 页 403、CDX 检索无 essay 快照，负面取证）；苏珊·桑塔格 1999 Artforum 原文全文（仅经英维/SoC 转引）；塔尔的电影学校教学（film.factory）细节；《都灵之马》"蜡烛"具体意象——任务预设称"蜡烛"，enwiki 剧情实际为**灯笼**（lanterns）逐日熄灭，本卡按取证到的原文写（[S3]）；《撒旦探戈》每章镜头的逐镜统计未取证到。
5. **逝世信息**：贝拉·塔尔于 2026 年 1 月 6 日去世，享年 70 岁（英维主条目 death_date 字段[S1]、中维条目[S6]、BFI 讣告导语[S11]）；本卡写作于其身后。
6. **政治立场**：塔尔有明确的无政府主义政治观点（enwiki "Political views/Anarchist movement" 段，Freedom 报刊悼词"his social realism was always suspicious of established power"[S1]），本卡聚焦美学手法，政治观仅作背景，未展开。
