## 技法卡片 1 · 开场十五分钟无对白——身体即人物

- **大师**：保罗·托马斯·安德森（Paul Thomas Anderson）
- **场景**：场景 1-21（L26-359）：1898 年新墨西哥，Daniel 独自挖银——镐头起落、骡子热死、坠井断踝、丢水壶、拖车横穿沙漠、卖矿。**全片第一句台词在 L370**（1908 年 Signal Hill 推销演讲 `Ladies and Gentlemen, I've traveled over half our state...`），此前约十五分钟零对白
- **技法**：**人物出场不靠对白，靠身体与重复劳动**。开场序列 = 三次重复（挖/挖/挖）→ 一次坠落 → 一次失去 → 一次爬行，人物全部信息（孤、狠、韧、不哀悼）都在动作里。剧本用镜头语法直接写（`CAMERA looks up a steep trail. Half the frame filled with a hill in close distance, the other half is sky...`，L28-29）
- **原文摘录**：
  - L28-31：`CAMERA looks up a steep trail. Half the frame filled with a hill in close distance, the other half is sky...over the top of the hill we see a prospector's pick come up and then down...up and then down...up and then down...`
  - L42：`He has a shaft about fifteen feet deep at this point. Nearby is a MULE and a CART. He digs and digs and digs.`
  - L104-110：`he plunges - loosing all his balance, twists around and HEADS FEET FIRST STRAIGHT DOWN THE / SHAFT WITH NO HOLD - AND LANDS AT THE BOTTOM / BOTH HIS ANKLES SNAP TOWARDS EACH OTHER.`（行首 6A 场景号并入正文为版式噪声，[sic] loosing）
  - L141-143：`DANIEL is pushing the cart with the upper half of his body...then he drags himself to catch up with the cart - he does this over and over and over and over again across the desert floor -`
  - L370-371：`Ladies and Gentlemen, I've traveled over half our state to get here this evening.`
- **画面锚点**：井口仰视——坠落者脚先着地，双踝向彼此对折（L104-110）
- **可复用时机**：主角是"狠人/孤狼/实业家"需要先立人设时——**先给足量的身体戏，再给第一句台词**；让第一句台词承担"人物开始对外说话=开始欺骗"的结构反讽（他对陌生人开口就是要钱）。史诗/传记片开场尤其适用
- **对接**：导演（画面/节奏）+ 编剧（人物建置）

## 技法卡片 2 · SOUND DROPS OUT——声轨主观化装置

- **大师**：保罗·托马斯·安德森
- **场景**：三处使用——① 井喷失聪（L2992-3062）：Daniel 视角 `SOUND COMES BACK WHEN WE ARE WITH HIM.`，切到 H.W. `SOUND DROPS OUT AGAIN. SILENT WITH HIM.`；② 小屋日常（L3663-3683）：H.W. 看"叔叔"吃饭说话，`THE SOUND DROPS OUT. WE WATCH HIM WATCH HENRY EAT AND TALK`；③ 火车送别（L4146-4148）：`For a very long cu. we hold on HW's face as he sits. SOUND drops out slowly.`
- **技法**：**把听觉障碍写成剧本级的声轨指令**——观众与角色共享失聪，而不是"看"失聪。声音的"进/出"被当作景别一样调度（`SOUND COMES BACK WHEN WE ARE WITH HIM`——声音跟着视角走）
- **原文摘录**：
  - L2992-2994：`ANGLE, DANIEL. SOUND COMES BACK WHEN WE ARE WITH HIM. He has not seen what has happened to HW. He is busy trying to deal with the GUSHER.`
  - L3009-3012：`ANGLE, HW. SOUND DROPS OUT AGAIN. SILENT WITH HIM. He watches the FIRE coming straight towards him.`
  - L3037-3039：`I CAN'T HEAR MY VOICE. I CAN'T HEAR MY VOICE. DADDY, I DON'T WANNA, I CAN'T HEAR MY VOICE.`
  - L3060-3062：`ANGLE, FROM HW'S POINT OF VIEW. We hear absolutely nothing. DANIEL leaves, HW follows, clinging, screaming. DANIEL PUSHES HIM DOWN and goes back outside --`
  - L3681-3683：`CUT BACK TO HW. AND THE SOUND DROPS OUT. WE WATCH HIM WATCH HENRY EAT AND TALK EAT AND TALK. HOLD, THEN BACK TO CONVERSATION WITH SOUND:`
- **画面锚点**：火墙直扑 H.W. 而来，画面无声（L3010-3012）——最吵的场景配最静的声音
- **可复用时机**：角色有感官损伤（失聪/失明/耳鸣）或精神隔离时——**不要演"他听不见"，要让观众听不见**；把声轨开关写进剧本动作行，剪辑/混音直接照做。也可用于"视角切换"：谁的视角，谁的声音在场
- **对接**：导演（声音设计/剪辑）+ 编剧（动作层写法）

## 技法卡片 3 · 回声式对白——洗礼的领诵与复诵

- **大师**：保罗·托马斯·安德森
- **场景**：受洗交易（L5330-5416）：为拿到 Bandy 的地皮，Daniel 在 Eli 的教堂当众忏悔。Eli 领诵、Daniel 复诵——`I AM A SINNER.` → `I am a Sinner.` → `I HAVE ABANDONED MY CHILD.` → `I have abandoned my child.` → 第三次 `I ABANDONED MY CHILD. I ABANDONED MY CHILD. I ABANDONED MY BOY.`（L5403-5407，全大写、无标点、三连）
- **技法**：**回声式对白 = 权力关系的声学显形**。复诵者必须逐字吞下对方的话，句式相同、音量递增（大小写即音量：L5352 小写 `I am a Sinner.` → L5403 全大写三连）——忏悔变成服从测试，字面完全一样，屈辱层层加深。最终一句 `I ABANDONED MY BOY.` 把"孩子"改成"我的男孩"，是假忏悔里漏出的真感情（最痛的词在第三遍）
- **原文摘录**：
  - L5333-5339：`you've lusted after women and you have abandoned your child. Your child that you raised, you have abandoned all because he was sick and you have sinned`
  - L5367-5370：`I HAVE ABANDONED MY CHILD. / I have abandoned my child.`
  - L5403-5407：`I ABANDONED MY CHILD. / I ABANDONED MY CHILD. / I ABANDONED MY BOY.`
  - L5412-5416：`GIVE ME THE BLOOD ELI, LET ME GET AWAY FROM THIS. JUST GIVE ME THE BLOOD LORD AND LET ME GET AWAY.`
- **画面锚点**：CU. Daniel——crowd 起哄声中他跪着，脸在"假装/承受/爆炸"之间（L5387-5388：`Daniel is somewhere between faking it/taking it/and exploding up at Eli...`）
- **可复用时机**：写"仪式性羞辱/服从/洗脑"场景——**让受害者复诵施暴者的话**；三遍定律：第一遍小声、第二遍大声、第三遍变形（加进真话）。也可用于爱情/师徒关系的"承诺仪式"
- **对接**：编剧（对白/潜台词）+ 导演（表演/节奏）

## 技法卡片 4 · 自我供词独白——"I have a competition in me"

- **大师**：保罗·托马斯·安德森
- **场景**：与"弟弟"Henry 的深夜谈话（L3836-3930）：Daniel 主动剖白——`I have a competition in me. I want no one else to succeed. I hate most people.`（L3851-3853）→ `I see the worst in people. I don't need to look past seeing them to get all I need. I want to rule and never, ever explain myself. I've built my hatreds up over the years, little by little, Henry...`（L3917-3926）
- **技法**：**反英雄的"自知时刻"：让他在最信任的人面前自愿供出全部罪状**。供词不是忏悔——不请求原谅，只陈述事实；句式短、断言式（`I hate most people.`）、无铺垫无解释。供词紧跟在温情之后（`...to have you here gives me a second breath...`，L3928-3930），越温情越显孤独
- **原文摘录**：
  - L3851-3853：`I have a competition in me. / I want no one else to succeed. / I hate most people.`
  - L3867-3868：`There are times when I look at people and I see nothing worth liking.`
  - L3894-3895：`I want to make enough money that I can move far away from everyone.`
  - L3917-3926：`I see the worst in people. / I don't need to look past seeing them to get all I need. / I want to rule and never, ever explain myself. / I've built my hatreds up over the years, little by little, Henry...`
- **画面锚点**：两人围坐夜谈，Daniel 全程直视——他剖析自己时眼睛不躲（对比杀 Henry 后 `HOLD ON DANIEL.` 的同一双眼睛）
- **可复用时机**：反派/反英雄需要在观众心里"立住"时——**让他自己说**，比任何旁观者评价都有效；供词要短句、要断言、要带一处"弱点泄漏"（`to have you here gives me a second breath` 暴露了他也需要人）；供词之后立刻接背叛/利用（Henry 是假的、Daniel 随后杀他）形成结构反讽
- **对接**：编剧（对白/人物弧光）

## 技法卡片 5 · 奶昔隐喻——收网式对白

- **大师**：保罗·托马斯·安德森
- **场景**：终局宅邸对峙（L6553-6561）：Eli 求钱，Daniel 当面清算——`DRAINAGE! DRAINAGE, ELI! DRAINED DRY, YOU BOY.`（L6554）→ `If you have a milkshake and I have a milkshake and I have a straw and my straw reaches ACCCRROSSSSSSSSSS the room starts to drink your milkshake: I DRINK YOUR MILKSHAKE! I DRINK IT UP.`（L6556-6561）
- **技法**：**全片的掠夺逻辑在结尾压缩成一个童谣式比喻**。收网式对白的三个条件：① 比喻的部件全部来自前文（奶昔=油、吸管=管道、跨过房间=越界抽油，观众已看过三十年的实物）；② 语气突然降格（从咆哮的 `DRAINAGE!` 降到"如果我有一杯奶昔"的平静叙述）；③ 拉长拼写指示语速（`ACCCRROSSSSSSSSSS` [sic]）——嚣张的慢
- **原文摘录**：
  - L6554：`DRAINAGE! DRAINAGE, ELI! DRAINED DRY, YOU BOY.`
  - L6556-6561：`If you have a milkshake and I have a milkshake and I have a straw and my straw reaches ACCCRROSSSSSSSSSS the room starts to drink your milkshake: / I DRINK YOUR MILKSHAKE! I DRINK IT UP.`
  - L6575-6579：`I TOOK WHAT I WANTED WHEN YOU WEREN'T LOOKING AND THE BLOOD OF THE LAMB IS IN MY POCKET. DO YOU THINK GOD IS GOING TO BALANCE YOUR INVESTMENTS? GOD DOESN'T SAVE IDIOTS, ELI.`
  - L6587-6589：`DID YOU THINK THAT YOUR SONG AND DANCE AND YOUR SUPERSTITION WOULD SAVE YOU?`
- **画面锚点**：银杯器在手中把玩——他说"吸管"的时候，观众已经知道那杯子是凶器（L6604：`along the way, he picks up a HEAVY SILVER TUMBLER-`）
- **可复用时机**：大反派的"主题宣言"场景——**不要讲道理，要打比方**；比喻的每个部件必须在前文有实物对应（观众自己会解码，解码瞬间=高潮）；宣言放在杀机已起之后，比喻本身就是处决的序曲
- **对接**：编剧（对白/主题收网）+ 导演（表演节奏）

## 技法卡片 6 · 无台词处决——双谋杀对照

- **大师**：保罗·托马斯·安德森
- **场景**：两次杀人，同一手法——**杀人不给台词，杀人后给脸**。① Henry（L5025-5027）：假弟弟忏悔到一半，`DANIEL puts the GUN TO HENRY'S HEAD AND FIRES. HENRY SLUMPS OVER. HOLD ON DANIEL.`——供词被枪声打断；② Eli（L6635-6682）：长篇羞辱后 `DANIEL is over HIM, AND SITS ON HIS CHEST, PINNING DOWN HIS ARMS...AND DRIVES THE TUMBLER STRAIGHT DOWN INTO ELI'S FACE.` → 拖尸、抛尸 → 瘫坐：`I'm finished.`
- **技法**：**处决场面的力量在"话与杀"的错位**——Henry 死在中辩白（求生的理由还没说完），Eli 死在 Daniel 的宣言高潮后（话说完即杀）。两次都让暴力瞬间"失语"：动作行直给、无慢镜渲染、无哀乐，随后 `HOLD` 停在杀人者的脸上——**句号是脸，不是血**
- **原文摘录**：
  - L5025-5027：`DANIEL puts the GUN TO HENRY'S HEAD AND FIRES. HENRY SLUMPS OVER. HOLD ON DANIEL.`
  - L5029-5031：`WIDE SHOT. STAY WITH THIS SCENE AND WATCH AS DANIEL DIGS A SHALLOW GRAVE.`
  - L6635-6636：`DANIEL is over HIM, AND SITS ON HIS CHEST, PINNING DOWN HIS ARMS...AND DRIVES THE TUMBLER STRAIGHT DOWN INTO ELI'S FACE.`
  - L6638-6641：`CAMERA looks STRAIGHT DOWN on the GORGEOUS WOOD FLOOR... ...very slowly, BLOOD COMES OOZING INTO FRAME. It spreads and moves like a slow land slide-.covering the floor`
  - L6651-6655：`Daniel drags Eli's dead body down the lane of the bowling alley, leaving a streak of BLOOD... HE THROWS HIM INTO THE BOWLING PINS AND DOWN OVER THE LIP INTO A CELLAR.`
  - L6682：`I'm finished.`
- **画面锚点**：血从保龄球道木地板缝里慢慢渗出来，像滑坡（L6638-6641）——暴力之后才给"美的画面"，是 PTA 的残忍美学
- **可复用时机**：主角第一次杀人（道德门槛）与最后一次杀人（主题完成）必须对照——第一次杀"假的亲人"，最后一次杀"真的敌人"；中间隔着他抛弃真儿子。写处决场面：**台词停、动作直给、镜头停在凶手脸上，尸体处理（拖/埋/抛）单独成段，不给反应镜头**
- **对接**：导演（画面/节奏）+ 编剧（结构对照）

## 技法卡片 7 · 时间骨架——三十年史诗的年份珠与资本形态

- **大师**：保罗·托马斯·安德森
- **场景**：全片骨架 1898（挖银）→ 1902（自建井）→ 1908（Signal Hill 演讲，L370）→ 1911（Little Boston 井喷）→ 1927（宅邸/保龄球道）。剧本只在场景标题标注三个年份（L26 `- 1898`、L158 `1902`、L363 `1908`），其余靠内容推进；维基分类确认跨度 1898-1927（来源：https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There_Will_Be_Blood ）
- **技法**：**时代史诗不拍"时代"，拍"经济形态的演变"**——每个阶段配一场"生意戏"：散户竞租（L478 `The crooks and the gamblers are near and the wolf is scratching the door.`）→ 管道革命（L1427-1433 `we take a pipeline from here to Port Hueneme or Santa Paula and we make a deal with Union Oil`）→ 铁路谈判（L4342-4359）→ 垄断收购与拒斥（L4360-4396 `Why don't you let us take it from here and make you rich.-spend time with your boy.` → `One night.---! 'm gonna come inside your house, wherever you're sleeping...and I'm gonna cut your throat.`，稿内 `!'m` 为 OCR 噪声 [sic]，即 I'm）→ 行业兄弟会（L4710-4720 黄段子）。**每一个年代 = 一种资本的说话方式**
- **原文摘录**：
  - L478：`The crooks and the gamblers are near and the wolf is scratching the door.`
  - L1427-1433：`so-so. if there's anything here...we take it to the sea -- we can go into town and see a map - but what we do - we take a pipeline from here to Port Hueneme or Santa Paula and we make a deal with Union Oil - this is what we do and we don't need the railroads and the shipping costs anymore, you see?`
  - L4381-4383：`One night.---! 'm gonna come inside your house, wherever you're sleeping...and I'm gonna cut your throat.`（稿内 `!'m` 为 OCR 噪声 [sic]，即 I'm）
  - L499：`We witness human dignity go completely out the window.`
- **画面锚点**：井喷大火夜转昼（L3093 `day turns to night and BLACK SMOKE FILLS the air...`）——一个时代的底色是黑烟
- **可复用时机**：写跨年代史诗/家族史/行业史——**用"每十年一场经济戏"代替编年史旁白**；让主角在每个年代换一种"话术"（恳求→谈判→威胁→沉默），话术的演变就是人物的弧光；群众场面给一句作者旁白点题（如 `We witness human dignity go completely out the window.`）
- **对接**：编剧（结构）+ 导演（年代质感）

## 技法卡片 8 · 首尾回收的动机道具——"sweet face to buy land"

- **大师**：保罗·托马斯·安德森
- **场景**：全片首尾两次"婴儿与交易"的并置——开场（L323-326）：矿场发现婴儿篮里的 H.W.，`DANIEL looks at the BABY, HW, in the basket...HOLD. He picks him up and walks around with him, holding onto him awkwardly...`；终局（L6196-6204）：成年的 H.W. 来告别，Daniel 撕破脸：`you're an orphan froa a basket in the middle of the desert-and I took you for no other reason than I needed a sweet face to buy land. You're lower than a bastard.`（[sic] froa）
- **技法**：**收养的动机藏了三十年，在决裂瞬间引爆**。"sweet face to buy land"回收了开场所有"慈父"戏——观众重看开场会发现：抱起婴儿时的 `HOLD`、喂威士忌、抱去给陌生母亲喂奶（L336-348），全是手段也是真情，两者不矛盾。**回收的关键：旧道具+新解释，让旧场景一夜变味**
- **原文摘录**：
  - L323-326：`DANIEL looks at the BABY, HW, in the basket...HOLD. He picks him up and walks around with him, holding onto him awkwardly...He puts him back in the basket, looks around... looks back at this baby H.W...`
  - L336-338：`Daniel wakes up, takes some WHISKEY from his bag, pours it on his FINGER and puts it in the baby's mouth. He sips some himself...baby H. W. cries and cries and cries...`
  - L6196-6204：`you're an orphan froa a basket in the middle of the desert-and I took you for no other reason than I needed a sweet face to buy land. You're lower than a bastard.`（[sic] froa；`*` 修订标记原貌省略）
  - L6211-6213：`I'm going to Mexico with my wife. / I'm going away from you.`
- **画面锚点**：开场笨拙地抱起婴儿的同一双手，终局指着他说"你比私生子还低"（L6202-6204）
- **可复用时机**：写"关系真相"的揭晓——**把动机真相留到最后一次冲突，且必须回收到开场最温情的道具/画面**；揭晓台词要具体到用途（`I needed a sweet face to buy land`——连用途都说清，比"我从未爱过你"狠十倍）；被揭晓方（H.W.）的回应要平静（`I'm going to Mexico with my wife.`）——平静即决绝
- **对接**：编剧（伏笔/主题回收）+ 导演（首尾画面呼应）
