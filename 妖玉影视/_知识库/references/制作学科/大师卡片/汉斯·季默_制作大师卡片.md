# 制作大师卡片 · 汉斯·季默（Hans Florian Zimmer, 1957–）

> 取证范围：一手访谈（MusicTech 2018 长访谈、British GQ 2017 长访谈〔Inception 配乐内幕/生涯〕、ThePlaylist 2021《沙丘》访谈、FirstShowing 2008《黑暗骑士》配乐访谈〔Zimmer 与 James Newton Howard 合访〕、CDM 2024《沙丘2》Osmose 报道中引用的 Zimmer 原话）+ 英文维基主条目与专条（BRAAAM、BR2049 原声带、Dune 原声带、Inception 原声带、TDK 原声带）+ Variety/Collider 的 Benjamin Wallfisch 访谈（BR2049 配乐细节）。Synth Anatomy 博客对 BR2049 Facebook 直播的转述为二手来源（详见第 7 节诚实声明）。SCL（Society of Composers & Lyricists）《沙丘》访谈页抓取失败（仅 497 字节空壳，见 [S15]）。
> 编号：S1–S15 见文末「取证来源清单」。原文摘录均经 grep 对存档文件验证。

## 1. 专业签名（一句话）

**以"声音设计化配乐"重构商业大片的声音世界——用合成器与管弦乐互相"重新演绎"（orchestra re-interprets the synthesizer）、单音/两音级别的极简动机（小丑=一把大提琴一个音、蝙蝠侠=两音动机、盗梦=一个低音轰鸣）、以及为"不存在的世界"发明"不存在的乐器"（沙丘），把音乐做成与画面、音效彼此渗透的声场——"What I try to do with my work is figure out how to bleed into the picture, bleed into the frame and bleed into the story."（[S2]）。**

依据：Zimmer 自述"Blurring the lines between the sound design and the music"（[S2]）；小丑主题"a single note played on the cello"（[S1]）与"a concept with one note attached to it"（[S14]）；《沙丘》"New, hybrid instruments were fabricated to conceive the 'otherworldly' tonal desert sounds"（[S8]）；"There will be new instruments, there will be new sonic textures – why can’t we go and invent new things?"（[S10]）；其生涯标志"fusing the traditional orchestral sound with electronic instruments"（[S1]）。

## 2. 美学体系（手法 | 成片实例 | 取证来源）

So sometimes you can't tell what's what."（[S2]）；"Sometimes I'd create some electronic soundscapes, then play them to the orchestra and say, 'You guys on the cellos, violins and basses or whatever, can you re-interpret the synthesiser sounds?'"（[S2]）；BR2049 摩托车声=男声合唱重度饱和/颗粒化/滤波/变调链（[S6]） | [S2][S6] |

## 3. 创作思路（工作方法/理念，附原话与出处）

- **音乐从对话中生长，而非从画面里长出来**：Zimmer 的招牌工作法是"让导演讲给我听"——"I find it's more interesting to actually say to the director 'tell me the story,' because then I get his point of view as opposed to the script's point of view"（[S14]）；"The music does come out of conversation for me. Some people call it procrastination; I actually call it thinking. I need to know what is in the director's head beyond what is up there on the screen."（[S3]）；Steve McQueen 的旁证：两次五小时长谈后，"he played me a single note. All that conversation... led to just one note."（[S3]）。
- **第一性问题："为什么这里要有音乐？"**："The basic idea remains the same and that is to ask a question: 'Why are we having music here?'"（[S2]）；"A composer has to figure that out before they begin making music."（[S2]）。音乐为叙事服务："Really it's the storytelling that is driving the process at all times."（[S2]）。
- **情感优先、不替观众做决定**："When an audience comes into a movie theatre they want to have an emotional experience. All I'm trying to do, quite seriously, is open the doors that lend themselves to that. It's not my job to tell the audience what to do or what to feel."（[S2]）。留白哲学："Music is a dangerous beast, because it can manipulate the audience in a horrible way... All I want to be is honest. What that means more and more is to leave room for the audience to virtually finish the tune, to leave holes."（[S3]）。
- **先定"声学世界"，再谈乐器**：导演讲故事时他最先冒出的念头是——"What’s the sonic world that we’re going to go and drop the audience into?"（[S2]）；并主张音乐要"渗透进画面"（bleed into the picture/frame/story，[S2]）。《沙丘》同一逻辑：未来社会为什么要用常规乐器？——"in a futuristic society why would we have conventional instruments"（[S9]）。
- **为角色/世界观发明新声音，拒绝套路**：《雨人》——"road movies usually have jangly guitars or a bunch of strings... why don't we just invent our own world music for a world that doesn't really exist?"（[S1]）；《沙丘》——"There will be new instruments, there will be new sonic textures – why can’t we go and invent new things?"（[S10]）；他青少年时读《沙丘》后看《星球大战》的第一反应："why are we hearing trumpets and cellos? I thought this was supposed to be a galaxy far far away!"（[S9]）；为《沙丘》特意不看 Lynch 版、不听 Toto 配乐——"quite simply because I was making a movie in my head"（[S9]）。
- **减法与"最简即最难"**：小丑主题"it was just so hard for me. I had this idea of this sound in my head and to get to it, it took months. And it's so simple, but it's like chiseling away and trying to make it that simple and just finding people who could even play this thing because it was all about the attitude."（[S14]）；"I didn't want to do another summer Hollywood blockbuster... Be provocative!"（[S14]）；拒绝写"能哼的英雄主题"——"there's probably a whole bunch of people out there still waiting for us to write that happy superhero tune... don't hold your breath"；并直言"superheroes need to have a tune"正是要"摆脱的包袱"（[S14]）。
- **标志性声音的"可识别性"标准**：所谓 iconic 就是"只属于这部电影"的声音——"How do you make it a singular thing that is this Batman, and this Batman only"；两音动机一响"you know they got it... this can only be this movie"（[S14]）；对 BRAAAM 被全行业滥用则持负面态度——配乐"became the blueprint for all action movies"（[S12]），并强调《盗梦空间》的创新在于拿它们"讲故事"而非当过渡音效："people were just sort of using them as transitional pieces"，而他用来"tell a story"（[S12]）。
- **叛逆出身与自学者底色**：童年钢琴课极短——"either I was going to kill the teacher, or he was going to kill me"（[S1]）；父亲是工程师/发明家，支持他"改装钢琴"——"I grew up modifying the piano... attach chainsaws and stuff like that to the piano"（[S1]）；1978 年前后在英国朋克乐队，把钱全花在合成器上（EMS VCS 3，[S2]）；与 Buggles、The Damned（唱片署名"Over-Produced by Hans Zimmer"）、Kraftwerk/Tangerine Dream/Jarre/Vangelis/Can/Neu! 等电子乐谱系渊源（[S1][S2][S3]）；"I was basically expelled from every school in Germany"（[S3]）。Morricone《西部往事》（*Once Upon a Time in the West*）配乐是他成为电影作曲家的启蒙（[S1]）；入行关键导师 Stanley Myers 教他管弦写作——"Stanley really taught me about the orchestra. Not only was he smart, but he was also incredibly generous - he didn't like writing music for car chases, so I got very good at scoring those!"（[S3]）。
- **"破规矩"进入好莱坞**：1988 年到好莱坞发现"Everyone was still writing on paper"（纸笔写作、老式抄谱机），于是"to get anywhere I would have to start breaking the rules; to start pissing people off - gently."（[S3]）；带"乐队心态"入行——"I have a much freer attitude, a collaborative philosophy. I encourage people to question me... Of course, this creates arguments. Huge arguments!"（[S3]）。
- **与诺兰的"互相试探"关系**：诺兰要音乐在剪辑之初就位（Inception 电话轶事，[S3]）；配乐常先于画面写作——TDK 小丑概念在开拍前已定（[S14]），《沙丘》在开拍前已写出大量音乐（"over an hour of music to assist the filmmakers in planning the film"，[S8]）；Interstellar 诺兰只给他一页纸："what Chris wrote down that day... had more to do with my story than the plot of the movie. He knows how to get under my skin."（[S3]）；"What haven't we done?"是两人每次开新片的起点（[S2]）。
- **老模拟合成器的执念**："I’m addicted to synthesisers"（[S2]）；"If you listen to my soundtracks for The Dark Knight trilogy and Dunkirk. The old analogue stuff is the kind of stuff that pins you to your seat."（[S2]）——类比诺兰坚持胶片拍摄。
- **对"完成"的态度**："I don't think any of my scores are ever really finished"；以 Gerhard Richter 画展自比——伟大的艺术家知道何时收手，"that's why I love working with directors, because they're very good at going, 'You're done, move on to the next piece'"（[S2]）；"the only enemy we have is time"（[S2]）。

## 4. 招牌作品拆解

### 拆解一：《银翼杀手2049》（2017）——合成器/低音/氛围的"致敬-再造"范本

- **接棒难题**：原版《银翼杀手》配乐（Vangelis）被视为史上最伟大科幻配乐之一，续集接棒压力极大——"keep it very much in the world of 'Blade Runner,' which is synonymous with Vangelis"（[S5]）；原配乐几乎全用合成器，所以新配乐要"celebrate that sound, and at the same time think about how it could be reinvented for a completely new story, 30 years later"（[S5]）。前作曲 Jóhann Jóhannsson 与维伦纽瓦分道扬镳（维伦纽瓦认为影片"needed something different"，[S4]）；Zimmer/Wallfisch 经剪辑师 Joe Walker 引荐入局（[S6]）。
- **CS-80 的仪式性使用**：Zimmer 从仓库取出 Vangelis 原配乐的主奏合成器 Yamaha CS-80——"It was important to begin and end the score with it... tipping our hat to the great Vangelis. Most of the rest of the score was created using contemporary synthesizers"（[S5]）；Synth Anatomy 转述直播：二人再次使用 CS-80，配乐"very electronically"、整体声音气质做得很暗（"they try to make the entire sound character very dark"），Zimmer 还把 CS-80 声音复刻进他钟爱的软合成器 Zebra2（[S7]，二手来源）；影片预告片阶段 El-P 也曾用 CS-80 写配乐但未被采用（[S4]）。
- **管弦被"拒收"**：Wallfisch 自述——"It rejected the orchestra outright. Whenever we tried anything orchestral it just didn’t work."（[S6]）；直接模仿 Vangelis 的旋律化路数也行不通，因为故事太不同（[S6]）。
- **四音"灵魂主题"**：开场即出现的四音旋律（"soul theme"/"horse theme"）随 K 的每次重大发现变形出现——"The idea of complexity being built on immense simplicity"，四音对应 DNA 的四种碱基（"four notes, corresponding to the four acids in the DNA strand"，[S6]）；"a very simple melody, the first thing you hear over the opening shots of the movie"（[S5]）。
- **低音/氛围的手法**：大量"synth textures and colors that evolve very slowly to match the pace of the film"（[S5]）；"creation theme"用低音提琴在高音区演奏和弦——"unnatural and very uncomfortable to play that high for a bass player, but it is also very beautiful and fragile"（[S6]）；摩托车声=男声合唱重度处理链（饱和/颗粒化/滤波 LFO/八度/随机变调），源自维伦纽瓦的要求——"the score needed to have a punk attitude"（[S6]）。
- **静默节奏**：拉斯维加斯段约 15 分钟无配乐，改用 Elvis/Sinatra 老歌（diegetic），"make a rhythm of silence in the overall score’s structure"（[S6]）；原声带含 Vangelis《Tears in Rain》原作（[S4]）。
- **成绩佐证**：BAFTA 最佳电影音乐提名（第 71 届）、2018 格莱美 Best Score Soundtrack for Visual Media 提名（败给《黑豹》，[S4]）。

### 拆解二：《沙丘》（2021）——《异域人声/史诗/自制乐器》的集大成

- **从少年执念到"拆掉辅助轮"**：Zimmer 与维伦纽瓦都是青少年时代读的原著（"we were both teenagers when we read the book"，[S9]）；他为等这部电影在脑中做了几十年——"I was making a movie in my head"（[S9]）；成片配乐他自称"相对莽撞、像少年实验"——"I took the training wheels off. I didn't need them anymore."（[S9]）；"it's relatively reckless, [like] a teenager who wants to experiment and try things out... I would have probably written a more cautious score"（[S9]）。
- **自制乐器/异域声源**："New, hybrid instruments were fabricated to conceive the 'otherworldly' tonal desert sounds heard in the film"（[S8]）；"There will be new instruments, there will be new sonic textures – why can’t we go and invent new things?"（[S10]）；两作大量使用亚美尼亚 duduk（[S1]）；风笛+重金属吉他叠置——维伦纽瓦的理由："you can’t just do trumpets, you can’t do fanfare, but this ancient instrument could have been around forever"，随后 30 名风笛手进棚（[S9]）。
- **女声=叙事的"心"**：权力在女性、驱动故事的是女性——"it is the women who carry the power, who drive the story forward"；未来社会唯一必然保留的是人的身体，"the great female singers I got to invite on this journey with me was where I felt the heart was. There’s a weird spirituality about the movie that’s not religion."（[S9]）；配乐编制上"heavily utilized choir—specifically female voices—percussion, and strings"（[S8]）；人声由 Loire Cotler 制作（[S8]）。《沙丘2》延续并强化人声路线："I wanted to create something that was an intensely human score"（[S10]），且更强调"闻所未闻的声音"（CDM 转述 Duquesne："There is much more emphasis in _Dune 2_ on the use of ‘un-heard’ sounds"，[S10]）。
- **用音乐替代内心独白**：原著大量内心独白、影片不用旁白——"part of my job was to go and... bend the action a little bit in a way that you knew people were thinking of other things"（[S9]）。
- **先于画面创作/工作流**：《沙丘2》宣布时 Zimmer 已写了超过一小时音乐供片方规划影片——"had over an hour of music to assist the filmmakers in planning the film"（[S8]）；《沙丘》配乐还是他第一张"wholeheartedly embrace"杜比全景声的专辑（"Ripples in the Sand"等曲，[S8]）。
- **成绩佐证**：第 94 届奥斯卡最佳原创配乐（第二次获奖，第一次为《狮子王》1994，[S1]）；2022 格莱美 Best Score Soundtrack for Visual Media 提名（[S8]）。

### 拆解三：《盗梦空间》（2010）+《黑暗骑士》（2008）——低音轰鸣与单音动机的诞生

- **《盗梦空间》：一个"时间"主题**。诺兰在拍摄期间就要配乐（"He wanted to unleash my imagination in the best possible way"，[S11]），并要求配乐引导观众"emotionally, geographically, temporally"（[S3]）；Zimmer 在没看到任何画面时就完成了第一轮铜管录音（[S3]）；配乐本质是"a very electronic score"（[S11]），灵感来自《哥德尔、艾舍尔、巴赫》（"the idea of playfulness in mathematics and playfulness in music"，[S11]）；无 temp score，按"小类别"而非具体场景写作（[S11]）。核心机关：整部配乐都是皮雅芙《Non, je ne regrette rien》的节奏细分与变速——"All the music in the score is subdivisions and manipulations of the tempo of the Edith Piaf track. So I could slip into half-time, I could slip into a third time. Anything could go anywhere... I could drop into a different level at any given moment."（[S3]）；Zimmer 说服险些删掉此曲的诺兰把皮雅芙歌当"signature or a leitmotif"引导观众与角色（[S3]）。其主题观："Everyone thinks the dream is the important part in the film... For me, it's time that's important. Chris had made a time-travel movie that actually worked."（[S3]）。BRAAAM 音色起源（Zimmer 自述）："I put a piano in the middle of a church and I put a book on the pedal, and these brass players would basically play into the resonance of the piano. And then I added a bit of electronic nonsense."（[S12]）；Vulture 报道的构成：大管/圆号/长号/大号同奏一个音+定音鼓（[S12]）。他自评这声音"like huge foghorns over a city"，并看穿预告片逻辑——"something iconic, lasts less than a second, and shakes the seats in the theater"（[S1]）；后来因被滥用而反感——"became the blueprint for all action movies"（[S12]）。⚠️ BRAAAM 的发明权存在争议：预告片作曲 Mike Zarin 声称抢先做出该声音（[S12]），本卡片只记录 Zimmer 版本的描述。
- **《黑暗骑士》：一个音的小丑**。小丑主题=大提琴单音（Martin Tillman 演奏），Zimmer 自述意图："I wanted to write something people would truly hate."（[S1]）；"The Joker thing was all done, or certainly a concept with one note attached to it, before he started shooting"（[S14]）；为这一个音磨了几个月——"it was all about the attitude"（[S14]）。成片主题动机分工：Zimmer 写小丑、Howard 写 Harvey Dent/双面人（[S13][S14]）。"Why So Serious"九分钟组曲基于两音——"two notes played by electric cello, solo violin, guitars and a string section"（[S13]），风格参照 Kraftwerk 与自己早年朋克乐队 The Damned；录音中在弦乐器上使用剃刀片（"Zimmer used razor blades on string instruments to achieve the tortured, twisted sound"，[S13]）。蝙蝠侠主主题被设计成"red herring"式音乐伏笔，全片只出现四次，由大提琴演奏（[S13]）。TDK 配乐曾因 cue sheet 作曲者过多被奥斯卡取消提名资格，Zimmer 2008 年 12 月成功游说翻案（[S1]）。配乐整体美学即"减法"——拒绝超级英雄进行曲（"don't hold your breath"，[S14]）。
- **方法论延伸**：《敦刻尔克》"constantly rising the tension all through the whole movie"——配乐部分基于诺兰送他的表针走动录音（"basing part of the score on a recording of a ticking watch that he had been given by Nolan"，[S1]），斯图卡俯冲轰炸机尖啸声是团队在 30 加仑钢桶里自制警笛录出来的（[S2]）——声音设计化配乐的极端案例。

## 6. AI 提示词对接（美学语言 → 音乐/视频生成提示词）

按取证要点编写。配乐类给"音乐生成提示词"（Suno/Udio 类），另附"音画联动"提示词：

**单音动机（反派/概念身份证）**
- EN: "A single sustained note as a villain's entire theme: low cello with distortion and noise, played with aggressive attitude rather than melody, unsettling and sparse, no harmony, pure tone-color menace; the simpler the material, the longer it took to make it sound right"
- 中注：一个音=一个反派；音色与演奏态度>旋律（依据 [S1][S13][S14]）

**低音轰鸣（巨物在场）**
- EN: "Massive low-end musical tone like distant foghorns: bassoon, French horn, trombone and tuba playing the same note loudly over timpani, brass played into the resonance of a prepared piano in a church, plus electronic processing; one note that shakes the theater seats"
- 中注：BRAAAM 构成（大管/圆号/长号/大号同音+定音鼓+共鸣钢琴）（依据 [S12][S1]）

**声景化配乐（音效与音乐互渗）**
- EN: "A score that bleeds into the sound design: electronic soundscapes reinterpreted by solo cello, violins and basses; granular and saturated textures evolving very slowly; the listener cannot tell where sound effects end and music begins"
- 中注：电子声景→管弦重释；缓慢演化的合成器纹理（依据 [S2][S5]）

**异域人声（精神性/心）**
- EN: "Intensely human score: female vocal ensemble chanting wordless phrases over low percussion and droning strings, a weird spirituality that is not religion; voices as the emotional heart of an epic desert world"
- 中注：女声合唱=叙事的心；无词吟唱+低频打击+嗡鸣弦乐（依据 [S8][S9][S10]）

**古老乐器置换史诗（国风对应）**
- EN: "Epic without trumpets: ancient wind instruments (duduk, bagpipes, xiao flute) layered over heavy metal guitars and sub-bass synths, as if the instrument could have existed forever in this world; no conventional fanfare"
- 中注：不用号角，用"可能自古存在"的乐器承担史诗（依据 [S9][S10][S1]）

**时间层次（变速/切分）**
- EN: "One musical source in multiple tempos: half-time and third-time subdivisions of the same melody stacked across dream levels, the score as a clock; slow ticking percussion driving constant rising tension"
- 中注：单一素材的倍数变速=时间层；钟表/滴答驱动张力（依据 [S3][S1][S2]）

**静默节奏（配乐的呼吸）**
- EN: "A 15-minute passage with no score at all, only diegetic period songs, so that the return of the score lands with real purpose; rhythm of silence in the macro structure of the album"
- 中注：大段撤乐+时代声音材料并置，配乐回归才有目的（依据 [S6]）

**音画联动工作流（给制作流程的提示）**
- 第一步让导演"讲故事"而非发剧本（[S14]）→ 先定声学世界（[S2]）→ 开拍前写出核心动机（单音/两音级，[S14]）→ 音乐交给剪辑期使用（[S3]）→ 录音时与音效组共享素材、互相渗透（[S2]）；生成工具可按"动机→变速变奏→配器置换"链条批量产出候选（依据 [S1][S2][S3][S14]）
