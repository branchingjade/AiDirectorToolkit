# 导演美学卡片 · 朴赞郁（Park Chan-wook, 1963–）

> 取证范围：成片表现 + 导演访谈原文（RogerEbert.com 2023《老男孩》20 周年访谈、Guardian 2022《分手的决心》访谈、Deadline 2023 访谈、SoC 2005 JSA 专文转引导演原话）+ 影评（Criterion Current、Roger Ebert 2005 影评、Senses of Cinema）+ 英文维基（主条目 + 7 部单片条目）+ 中文维基主条目。
> 编号：S1–S16 见文末「取证来源清单」。全部引文可对 pages/ 存档 grep 验证。
> ⚠️ 本代理未逐帧看片：所有成片观感类描述均引自评论家/维基并保留署名。

## 1. 风格签名（一句话）

**精密到近乎强迫的构图秩序（深焦宽银幕、中轴单镜、镜面与屏幕取景）装填越界的暴力、情欲与黑幽默——把类型片拆成迷宫，把复仇讲成古典悲剧神话，且永远追问"你问对问题了吗"。**

依据：英维风格总括"noted for their cinematography, framing, black humor, and often brutal subject matter"（[S1]）；JSA 深焦静态构图 + 韩国首部 Super-35（[S13]）；《老男孩》走廊单镜 17 次拍摄 3 天完成（[S3]）；《分手的决心》"gaze that frequently observes characters in mirrors, on computer monitors or through fragmenting screens"（[S10]）；导演自述复仇是"a very classical and mythological story subject"、电影核心是换一个问法（[S11]）。

## 3. 创作思路（工作方法/理念，附原话与出处）

- **出身：哲学系 + 前影评人，从"看懂电影"出发**。西江大学哲学系（兼修新闻广播），大三寒假在校传播中心观看希区柯克《迷魂记》后立志当导演（[S2]）；前两部片失败后靠写影评、上电台节目维持生计，并与后来成为其御用配乐人的曹英沃合开录像带店"电影村"（[S2]）；童年看 AFKN 美军电视台无字幕电影，"requiring him to interpret the films purely through their cinematic elements and narrative structure"（[S1]）——视觉语言是他最初学会的电影语言。
- **"半岛的分裂不是悲剧，是反讽"**：SoC 引导演原话——"[t]he division of the Korean peninsula is not a tragedy; it is an irony"（[S13]）。JSA 的题材起点即这种距离感。
- **复仇三部曲并非预谋**："Though not originally intended as a set, the films are linked by their exploration of the futility of revenge and the havoc it wreaks on those involved"（[S1]）；且导演自认 JSA 才是其处女作——"Many people think my directorial debut film is JSA, but I want to keep it that way"（[S1]）。
- **复仇母题的私人源头（军事独裁年代）**："It's likely because of my friends, seniors, and juniors who rose up and fought the military dictatorship while we were in college. I was weak and a coward and so couldn't actively fight, and the brave ended up being the ones sacrificed to this intense violence. That was when I became interested in themes such as guilt, vengeance, and redemption."（[S11]）
- **复仇无意义论（自我定位）**："All my films that deal with vengeance essentially talk about how in vain it is to lash out towards people, and there's nothing you can get out of an act of vengeance."（[S14]）；《老男孩》维基记 Woo-jin 自杀前"Realizing his vengeance has fixed nothing"（[S3]）。
- **社会病症直视为药方**："it is important for us to accept the fact that we have this illness. We have to look at it squarely, and that is the only way we can bring about a resolution."（[S14]，Guardian 访谈标题即"We must accept we have an illness in our society"）；"That is the power of art."（[S14]）
- **神话与命运优先于社会议题（转向《老男孩》的理由）**："Because I'd already dealt with the division of the Korean peninsula in JSA and class conflict within South Korea in Sympathy for Mr. Vengeance, I didn't want to handle yet another timely social issue... I wanted to tell a story that was more foundational, primordial, and universal. And I wanted to go romanticist, rather than realistic. I wanted to tell a story about fate."（[S11]）
- **"问对问题"是创作哲学**：《老男孩》关键台词"You can't find the right answer if you ask the wrong questions"——导演亲证"that line of dialogue is the key"：谜底不在"为何被囚禁"，而在"为何恰好在 15 年后被释放"，"What takes 15 years to achieve?"——米朵长成成人的时间（[S11]）；"Life is the same. How many enigmas stay unsolved because we are asking the wrong question?"（[S11]）
- **女性转向（自责驱动）**："After making JSA, Sympathy for Mr. Vengeance, and Oldboy back-to-back, I began to repent, having only created stories that pushed women to the periphery. She was who I recruited as a result... the films and series I've made since are all centered around women."（[S11]，指编剧郑瑞景 Chung Seo-kyung）
- **极简环境 + 最大张力 = "纯电影"**："Using a minimal setting to create maximum tension — I think that is the purest form of cinema."（[S15]）；"I always want to return to [making] a drama with a small number of people in an isolated, tight space and see the drama that unfolds there."（[S15]）
- **改编即再创作**："as much as it is a story by Sarah Waters, it is also my story"（[S15]，论《小姐》）；自称从不重复自己——"never repeating himself and bringing savagely original twists to genres as diverse as the vampire movie (Thirst), the erotic thriller (Handmaiden) and manga (Oldboy)"（[S15]，Deadline 综述语）。
- **梦进入画面**：橡皮筋弹走祖母（《机器人之恋》）、血海鲸鱼喷血水（《蝙蝠》）"Some of those are influences from my dreams"（[S15]）。
- **非理性人观（Kafka 影响）**："portraying something irrational: a phenomenon that cannot be explained logically. A portrait of humanity as neither good nor evil but rather as a complex existence."（[S3]）
- **演员观**："If Song Kang-ho is ice and modern, Choi Min-sik is fire and classical."（[S11]）；《老男孩》走廊戏的秃顶壮汉演员许明行（Heo Myeong-haeng）如今已是韩国顶级动作指导——"He made the hallway scene in 'Oldboy' twice as exciting with his unmatched performance"（[S11]）。
- **创作影响谱系**：2004 年 THR 访谈自列 Sophocles、Shakespeare、Kafka、Dostoevsky、Balzac、Kurt Vonnegut 为最重要影响（[S1]）；最爱片单多收 Visconti、Ferrara、Antonioni、成濑巳喜男、希区柯克（[S1]）。

## 4. 桥段设计（2–3 个招牌桥段）

### 桥段一：《老男孩》走廊锤战——单镜头暴力的范式
- **设计逻辑（技术层）**：一条走廊、一把锤子、一群打手，全程一个连续镜头："took seventeen takes in three days to perfect and was one continuous take; there was no editing of any sort except for the knife stabbed in Oh Dae-su's back, which was computer-generated imagery"（[S3]）——整段唯一的"假"是一刀 CG，其余全是真实调度。导演 20 年后仍记得那位秃顶壮汉特技演员（[S11]）。
- **节拍层**：15 年囚禁的复仇欲望在此一次性释放；Ebert 描述："a virtuoso sequence in which Oh fights with several of his former jailers, his rage so great that he is scarcely slowed by the knife sticking in his back"（[S12]）——镜头不停，观众没有喘息位。
- **主题层**：暴力被拍成"耗尽"而非"胜利"——复仇者的怒不可遏被长镜忠实记录，与全片"复仇无济于事"的命题同构（[S3][S12]）。
- **观众情绪**：单镜头剥夺了剪辑的安全感，观众被迫与角色同处一个不可逃脱的物理空间。

### 桥段二：《小姐》三幕视角反转——同一故事讲两遍
- **设计逻辑（技术层）**：维基结构分节明确——Part 1 以淑熙（女仆）视角讲述骗局，结尾揭晓——"it is revealed that Hideko's naïveté was part of the con. She and Fujiwara double-crossed Sook-hee"（[S4]）；Part 2 以秀子视角重述同一时间线（"A series of flashbacks show that Hideko's 'reading practice' was Kouzuki teaching her to read sadistic pornography since she was five years old"），补出童年虐待与入局真相；Part 3 两人合谋反转（[S4]）。与《JSA》的 Rashomon 式重复叙事（[S13]）同族：重复不是装饰，是换立场重看世界。
- **节拍层**：第一次看是骗局如何运作，第二次看是两女如何互相爱上——"Hideko unexpectedly falls in love with her. Hideko tries to confess her love"（[S4]）；结局以"On a ferry to Shanghai... celebrate their newfound freedom by having sex once again"收束（[S4]）。
- **主题层**：父权（姨父的色情朗读会/疯人院/绞刑）vs 女性联盟；Sarah Waters 原著的"Fingersmith"骗局被移植到日据朝鲜（[S4]）。导演的改编观："as much as it is a story by Sarah Waters, it is also my story"（[S15]）。
- **观众情绪**：第一幕的信以为真在第二幕被全部推翻——观众体验的就是"被骗"本身，这正是影片主题。

### 桥段三：《分手的决心》山海寓言 + 手机叙事——媒介即宿命
- **设计逻辑（技术层）**：全片以山海意象系统编码人物——维基专节"The Mountains and the Sea"：海俊（侦探）=山，瑞莱=海；瑞莱引用孔子"The wise man admires water, the kind man admires mountains."；"the sea has the ability to beat the mountains by eroding them over time"——她杀第一任丈夫于山顶、第二任于泳池、最终死于海中（[S5]）。破案证据链全程由手机/计步器/录音/追踪器构成（138 层楼梯的计步器揭穿不在场证明，[S5]）。
- **节拍层**：罗尼评"wary, wounded love story first and a procedural puzzle second"（[S10]）；Kiang："Park's ability to lean into tech's formal and thematic cinematic potential is little short of revelatory... the deathlessness of modern tech life has fundamentally changed the way we connect"（[S10]）；结尾沙滩挖坑自埋、潮水吞没（[S5]）。
- **主题层**：克制与缺席——坎城记者问"为何此片没有性与暴力"（[S14]）；导演答以"less is much more"（[S15]）；"My two protagonists are quite bad at being honest with their true emotions"（[S14]）。
- **观众情绪**：希区柯克式悬念（"recalls Vertigo in particular"，[S10]）被现代媒介放大——观众和侦探一样透过屏幕看人，屏幕即距离。

## 5. 画面锚点（8 个最强画面）

1. 《老男孩》走廊锤战：长镜头中朴赞郁式的中轴调度，血从崔岷植颈侧流下（[S3][S12]）。
2. 《老男孩》活章鱼：被囚 15 年的吴大秀"要把它嚼烂吞下去"（"He would furiously gnaw on a wriggling mass of life and swallow it"，导演亲述该镜头设计逻辑，[S11]）。
3. 《老男孩》结尾割舌：真相面前自我惩罚——"Dae-su cuts out his own tongue as penance"（[S3]，与俄狄浦斯刺目并置，[S3]）。
4. 《小姐》焚书：淑熙"begins destroying the library"——摧毁姨父的色情图书馆即摧毁父权（[S4]）。
5. 《小姐》渡船：淑熙与秀子在上海渡轮上以性爱庆祝自由（[S4]）。
6. 《亲切的金子》白蛋糕结局：金子把脸埋进白色奶油蛋糕，女儿说"你也可以纯净地活下去"（[S7]）；全片色彩此时已近黑白（[S7]）。
7. 《亲切的金子》集体复仇合影：家长们在处刑后合影，互相抵押（"take a group photo, ensuring that none of them can turn in the others"，[S7]）。
8. 《分手的决心》海滩：瑞莱在涨潮前挖沙自埋，海俊在海滩上找不到她（[S5]）；另有一镜蚂蚁爬过尸体面部（"one particularly eerie shot of ants crawling over a corpse's face"，[S10]）。
9. 《蝙蝠》梦境：血海中的鲸鱼喷出血水（导演自述出自梦境，[S15]）。

## 6. 可复用时机（复仇/悬疑/情欲/暴力/反转题材）

| 场景 | 用哪招 | 证据出处 |
|---|---|---|
| 复仇题材 | 复仇必落空：让复仇者或凶手在"成功"瞬间醒悟一切白费（Woo-jin 自杀）；导演原话"复仇无济于事" | [S3][S14] |
| 悬疑/谜题 | 设计"问对问题"型反转：谜底不在表面之问，换一个问法才见真相（15 年/成人礼） | [S11] |
| 暴力场面 | 单镜头长镜 + 真实动作 + 极少量 CG；镜头不停即不给观众喘息 | [S3][S12] |
| 反转叙事 | 同一故事用第二视角重讲一遍（Part 2 重述 Part 1），让观众体验被骗 | [S4][S13] |
| 情欲戏 | 情欲承担权力翻转与自由表达（《小姐》两场关键性爱均在"夺回主动"节点） | [S4] |
| 大尺度题材的克制变体 | "less is more"：删去性与暴力，用媒介（手机/录音/屏幕）制造张力 | [S14][S15][S10] |
| 人物系统 | 用自然物象给人物编码：山/海、水/火、冰/火（海俊/瑞莱；宋康昊=冰、崔岷植=火） | [S5][S11] |
| 群像复仇 | 集体处刑 + 合影互证（观众被拉入共谋） | [S7] |
| 悲剧性反转 | 让谜题揭底即悲剧降临——"stops being a mystery and becomes a tragedy in the classical sense" | [S12] |
| 结尾 | 色彩/形式层面的"褪色"收束（全片色彩渐褪至黑白），视觉即救赎进程 | [S7] |

⚠️ 本节为分析框架：把已取证的朴氏手法映射到目标类型，非导演自述。

## 7. AI 提示词对接（美学语言 → 视频生成提示词）

按取证要点编写，非原片描述。构图、光线色彩、声音节奏、运镜、群像五组：

**构图（深焦·媒介取景·中轴）**
- EN: "Wide anamorphic frame, deep-focus static composition, character framed inside a mirror / phone screen / CCTV monitor as a second frame within the frame; symmetrical one-point-perspective corridor with the protagonist centered, camera tracking straight behind him; every frame like a painting, layered planes of focus"
- 中注：宽银幕深焦+画中画媒介取景（镜面/屏幕/监视器），走廊中轴单镜（依据 [S13][S10][S3][S5]）

**光线色彩（漂白高对比·低饱和·原色点缀）**
- EN: "Bleach-bypass look: grainy, high contrast, low saturation, harsh fluorescent green and purple shadows with splashes of bold red; colors gradually desaturating toward black and white across the whole piece; sickly green and fluorescent purple night palette"
- 中注：粗颗粒高对比低饱和、大胆原色入画、整片级渐褪至黑白（依据 [S11][S7]）

**声音节奏（巴洛克仪式感·寂静）**
- EN: "Baroque harpsichord score with Vivaldi-like strings carrying ritual revenge mood; long silences before violent events; diegetic phone screen sounds and text-message beeps as dramatic beats; a lullaby as the final scene music"
- 中注：巴洛克配乐承担仪式感、摇篮曲收尾、手机音效作节拍（依据 [S7][S10][S5]）

**运镜（单镜不剪辑·越肩追逐）**
- EN: "One continuous unbroken take: protagonist fights through a corridor of attackers with a hammer, camera tracks steadily behind, no cuts, only practical impact; over-the-shoulder camera during a foot chase; occasional camera moves you have never seen before"
- 中注：单镜头长镜打斗、越肩追逐跟拍、机位求新（依据 [S3][S10]）

**群像（集体仪式）**
- EN: "A group of people silently taking turns to strike a bound figure, then standing together for a group photo, complicit; white and black clothing against grey walls; the camera holds wide so everyone is visible in one frame"
- 中注：集体处刑+合影互证，宽镜同框群像（依据 [S7]）
